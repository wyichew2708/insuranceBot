## Overview

The plan reimburses overseas medical expenses incurred during your trip, up to
the limit of your selected tier. Emergency dental treatment following an
accident is included.

## Limits

Limits differ by tier (Entry, Savvy, Luxury). See the plan comparison table
for the exact figures for your tier.

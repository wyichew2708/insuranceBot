## Prerequisites

Have your policy number, trip documents, and all receipts or reports ready.

## Steps

1. Log in to the customer portal or the Tiq app.
2. Select your travel policy and choose "Make a claim".
3. Upload supporting documents and submit.

## After you submit

You will receive an acknowledgement with a claim reference. Processing takes
up to the stated SLA once complete documents are received.

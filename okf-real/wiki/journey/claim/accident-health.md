## Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I make a claim? [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-foreign-worker-medical.md#frequently-asked-questions].

Click here to make a claim [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-foreign-worker-medical.md#frequently-asked-questions].

2. When should I report to insurer? [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-foreign-worker-medical.md#frequently-asked-questions].

You should report any accident or notified loss/damage/injury to the insurer immediately after the accident or upon receipt of notification of loss/damage/injury, whichever comes earlier [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-foreign-worker-medical.md#frequently-asked-questions].

3. Will Etiqa accept my claim if I am late in reporting? Should I gather all the required information/ documents before reporting to Etiqa? [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-foreign-worker-medical.md#frequently-asked-questions].

You should report to us immediately so that we can advise you the next course of action. By doing so, this would not delay claim investigation as well protect your interest in pursuing against negligent parties [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-foreign-worker-medical.md#frequently-asked-questions].

4. What happens after I submit my claim online? [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-foreign-worker-medical.md#frequently-asked-questions].

Upon submitting your claim online, you will receive an acknowledgement email with the following information for your easy reference and/or action: [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-foreign-worker-medical.md#frequently-asked-questions].

– eSubmission reference number – Summary of your claim submission [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-foreign-worker-medical.md#frequently-asked-questions].

5. Where can I submit more documents? [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-foreign-worker-medical.md#frequently-asked-questions].

You may submit to the officer in charge as stated in the acknowledgement email or to the following emails: – nonmotor@etiqa.com.sg (non motor claims) [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-foreign-worker-medical.md#frequently-asked-questions].

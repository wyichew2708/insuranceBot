## 1

iReport to Ministry of Manpower [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#1].

Completed claim form (only if iReport is not required) [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#1].

Copy of Employee’s work permit/NRIC/appointment letter [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#1].

Medical invoices and certificates [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#1].

Death Certificate (for fatal injury) [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#1].

Contract agreement for yourself and the main contractor (Note: Please confirm whether the main contractor/Principal has obtained separate insurance for all workers on the project. If yes, please provide insurance details and project policy.) [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#1].

Inpatient discharge summary/medical reports/radiology reports, if any [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#1].

Incident report or witness statements, if any [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#1].

Traffic Police Report in the event of traffic accident [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#1].

Traffic Investigation Report in the event of traffic accident [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#1].

## 2

Submit your documents online  [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#2].

Kindly note that Etiqa Insurance reserves the right to request for any medical report and to request for the original bills or certified true copies and to contact the public healthcare institutions directly if needed for validation of the bill authenticity [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#2].

All information and supporting documents may be subject to review by Etiqa Insurance and we shall reserve all rights to reject any claims, recover any and all amounts, or to impose additional charges if for any reason any claim is found to be fraudulent. Etiqa shall also reserve the right to pursue any actions at law or in equity that it deems appropriate in dealing with such fraudulent activity [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#2].

## Frequently Asked Questions

For Fatal accidents Notify MOM as soon as possible [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#frequently-asked-questions].

Employees can claim compensation within one year of accident, or up to the limitation period of the Occupational Disease even if they no longer work for the employer [src:raw/web/www.etiqa.com.sg/2026-08-25/claims-commercial-insurance-claim-work-injury-compensation.md#frequently-asked-questions].

## About Direct Etiqa CI Rider

## 2. Our Responsibilities

> This is a Non-participating rider that accelerates the Death Benefit of the Basic policy upon diagnosis of any one of the 30 critical illnesses of the Life insured. [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

> This rider is available for DIRECT- Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. whole life with Premium term of either up to Age 70 or up to Age 85. The entry Age of the Life insured for DIRECT - Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. CI rider (attachable to DIRECT- Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. whole life ) is shown below. [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

## 3.1 Critical Illness (CI) Benefit

> This rider pays 100% of the Sum insured plus accumulated bonuses (if any) that have been added to the Basic policy if the Life insured is diagnosed with a CI (except for 'Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery') for the first time during the rider policy term. [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

> We pay this Benefit only for one CI, with the exception that if the first CI claimable under this rider is 'Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery', we will only pay 10% of the Sum Insured under this rider subject to a S$25,000 maximum sum payable. This Benefit is payable once only and shall be deducted from the amount of this rider, thereby reducing the amount of the Sum Insured which may be payable herein. [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

Once the total CI Benefit is paid, the Basic policy will automatically terminate [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

Our payment on this rider will proportionately reduce the Sum insured of the Basic policy. You must continue to pay the revised Premiums to keep the Basic policy in force [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

If the Death Benefit of the Basic policy is reduced due to a claim for Total and Permanent Disability Benefit or Terminal Illness Benefit under the Basic policy, we will reduce the CI Benefit of this rider proportionally. The Premium of the rider will be reduced accordingly and You must continue to pay the revised Premiums to keep the rider in force [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

> The maximum aggregate amount payable is Two Million Singapore Dollars (SGD 2,000,000) per Life insured for all policies and riders issued by Us with CI Benefits. [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

> The 30 Critical Illnesses are listed below: [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

## 1 . Major Cancer Our Responsibilities

A malignant tumour positively diagnosed with histological confirmation and characterised by the uncontrolled growth of malignant cells with invasion and destruction of normal tissue [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

The term Major Cancer includes, but is not limited to, leukemia, lymphoma and sarcoma [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

Major Cancer diagnosed on the basis of finding tumour cells and/or tumour-associated molecules in blood, saliva, faeces, urine or any other bodily fluid in the absence of further definitive and clinically verifiable evidence does not meet the above definition [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

For the above definition, the following are excluded: [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

- a) All tumours which are histologically classified as any of the following: [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

Having any degree of malignant potential; [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

Neoplasm of uncertain or unknown behaviour; or [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

All grades of dysplasia, squamous intraepithelial lesions (HSIL and LSIL) and intra epithelial neoplasia; [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

- b) Any non-melanoma skin carcinoma, skin confined primary cutaneous lymphoma and dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans unless there is evidence of metastases to lymph nodes or beyond; - c) Malignant melanoma that has not caused invasion beyond the epidermis; - d) All Prostate cancers histologically described as T1N0M0 (TNM Classification) or below; or Prostate cancers of another equivalent or lesser classification; [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

- e) All Thyroid cancers histologically classified as T1N0M0 (TNM Classification) or below; - f) All Neuroendocrine tumours histologically classified as T1N0M0 (TNM Classification) or below; - g) All tumours of the Urinary Bladder histologically classified as T1N0M0 (TNM Classification) or below; [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

- h) All Gastro-Intestinal Stromal tumours histologically classified as Stage I or IA according to the latest edition of the AJCC Cancer Staging Manual, or below; - i) Chronic Lymphocytic Leukaemia less than RAI Stage 3; - j) All bone marrow malignancies which do not require recurrent blood transfusions, chemotherapy, targeted cancer therapies, bone marrow transplant, haematopoietic stem cell transplant or other major interventionist treatment; and - k) All tumours in the presence of HIV infection [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 2. Heart Attack of Specified Severity

Death of heart muscle due to ischaemia, that is evident by at least three of the following criteria proving the occurrence of a new heart attack: [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

- a) History of typical chest pain; - b) New characteristic electrocardiographic changes; with the development of any of the following: ST elevation or depression, T wave inversion, pathological Q waves or left bundle branch block; [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

- c) Elevation of the cardiac biomarkers, inclusive of CKMB above the generally accepted normal laboratory levels or Cardiac Troponin T or I at 0.5ng/ml and above; - d) Imaging evidence of new loss of viable myocardium or new regional wall motion abnormality. The imaging must be done by Cardiologist specified by the Company [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

- a) Angina; - b) Heart attack of indeterminate age; and - c) A rise in cardiac biomarkers or Troponin T or I following an intra-arterial cardiac procedure including, but not limited to, coronary angiography and coronary angioplasty [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

Explanatory note: 0.5ng/ml = 0.5ug/L = 500pg/ml [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 3 Stroke with Permanent Neurological Deficit

A cerebrovascular incident including infarction of brain tissue, cerebral and subarachnoid haemorrhage, intracerebral embolism and cerebral thrombosis resulting in permanent neurological deficit. This diagnosis must be supported by all of the following conditions: [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

> - a) Evidence of permanent clinical neurological deficit confirmed by a neurologist at least 6 weeks after the event; and - b) Findings on Magnetic Resonance Imaging, Computerised Tomography, or other reliable imaging techniques consistent with the diagnosis of a new stroke. [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

- a) Transient Ischaemic Attacks; - b) Brain damage due to an accident or injury, infection, vasculitis, and inflammatory disease; - c) Vascular disease affecting the eye or optic nerve; - d) Ischaemic disorders of the vestibular system; and - e) Secondary haemorrhage within a pre-existing cerebral lesion [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 4 Coronary Artery By-pass Surgery

The actual undergoing of open-chest surgery or Minimally Invasive Direct Coronary Artery Bypass surgery to correct the narrowing or blockage of one or more coronary arteries with bypass grafts. This diagnosis must be supported by angiographic evidence of significant coronary artery obstruction and the procedure must be considered medically necessary by a consultant cardiologist [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

Angioplasty and all other intra-arterial, catheter-based techniques, 'keyhole' or laser procedures are excluded [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 5 End Stage Kidney Failure

Chronic irreversible failure of both kidneys requiring either permanent renal dialysis or kidney transplantation [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 6 Irreversible Aplastic Anaemia

Chronic persistent and irreversible bone marrow failure, confirmed by biopsy, which results in anaemia, neutropenia and thrombocytopenia requiring treatment with at least one of the following: [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

- a) Blood product transfusion; - b) Bone marrow stimulating agents; - c) Immunosuppressive agents; or - d) Bone marrow or haematopoietic stem cell transplantation [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

The diagnosis must be confirmed by a haematologist [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 7 End Stage Lung Disease

End stage lung disease, causing chronic respiratory failure. This diagnosis must be supported by evidence of all of the following: [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

- a) FEV1 test results which are consistently less than 1 litre; - b) Permanent supplementary oxygen therapy for hypoxemia; - c) Arterial blood gas analyses with partial oxygen pressures of 55mmHg or less (PaO2 ≤ 55mmHg); and - d) Dyspnea at rest [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

The diagnosis must be confirmed by a respiratory physician [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 8 End Stage Liver Failure

End stage liver failure as evidenced by all of the following: [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

- a) Permanent jaundice; - b) Ascites; and - c) Hepatic encephalopathy [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

Liver disease secondary to alcohol or drug abuse is excluded [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 9 Coma

> A coma that persists for at least 96 hours. This diagnosis must be supported by evidence of all of the following: [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

> - a) No response to external stimuli for at least 96 hours; - b) Life support measures are necessary to sustain life; and - c) Brain damage resulting in permanent neurological deficit which must be assessed at least 30 days after the onset of the coma. [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

For the above definition, medically induced coma and coma resulting directly from alcohol or drug abuse are excluded [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 10 Deafness (Irreversible Loss of Hearing)

Total and irreversible loss of hearing in both ears as a result of illness or accident. This diagnosis must be supported by audiometric and sound-threshold tests provided and certified by an Ear, Nose, Throat (ENT) specialist [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

> Total means 'the loss of at least 80 decibels in all frequencies of hearing'. [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

> Irreversible means 'cannot be reasonably restored to at least 40 decibels by medical treatment, hearing aid and/or surgical procedures consistent with the current standard of the medical services available in Singapore after a period of 6 months from the date of intervention.' [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

## 11 Open Chest Heart Valve Surgery

The actual undergoing of open-heart surgery to replace or repair heart valve abnormalities. The diagnosis of heart valve abnormality must be supported by cardiac catheterization or echocardiogram and the procedure must be considered medically necessary by a consultant cardiologist [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 12 Irreversible Loss of Speech

> Total and irreversible loss of the ability to speak as a result of injury or disease to the vocal cords. The inability to speak must be established for a continuous period of 12 months. This diagnosis must be supported by medical evidence furnished by an Ear, Nose, Throat (ENT) specialist. [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

All psychiatric related causes are excluded [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 13 Major Burns

> Third degree (full thickness of the skin) burns covering at least 20% of the surface of the Life Assured's body. [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md]

## 14 Major Organ / Bone Marrow Transplantation

The receipt of a transplant of: [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

- a) Human bone marrow using haematopoietic stem cells preceded by total bone marrow ablation; or - b) One of the following human organs: heart, lung, liver, kidney, pancreas, that resulted from irreversible end stage failure of the relevant organ [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

Other stem cell transplants are excluded [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

## 15 Multiple Sclerosis

The definite diagnosis of Multiple Sclerosis, and must be supported by all of the following: [src:raw/wordings/direct-etiqa-ci-rider-policy-contract-09-nov-2023-to-12-mar-2025.md].

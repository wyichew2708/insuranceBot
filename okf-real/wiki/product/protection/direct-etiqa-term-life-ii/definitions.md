## 11. What Do We Mean With These Words?

Age means the Age at next birthday [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Accident means a sudden, unforeseen and unplanned event causing bodily injury that is violent, external and visible in nature. It is not caused by sickness, disease, gradual physical, mental changes or any other causes. The Accident must occur after the Policy issue date [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Assignee means any person to whom You have transferred Your rights as the Policy o wner and the assignment is registered with Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Basic policy means the policy as it exists, including the supplementary terms and any Endorsement made to it, without any optional supplementary contract / rider [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Benefit(s) means any payments that We will pay and/or the amount of Premium that We will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Doctor means a licensed person who is qualified by degree in western medicine to practice medicine. The license is given by the appropriate medical authority of his country of residence to practice medicine within his scope of licensing and training. This cannot be You, the Life insured, a family member or a relative [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Life insured means the person whom We provide the cover for. The Life insured does not have any right to the policy, unless he/she is also the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

> Nominee(s) is a person that You have nominated (under the Insurance Act 1966 and Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009 ) to receive the policy monies payable under the policy upon Your death. The nomination must be registered with Us. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md]

Non-participating means it does not share in any surplus or profits of the Company's fund [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Policy commencement date means the date the policy commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Policy issue date means the date We issue the policy. This is shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Policy Information Page or any Endorsement issued by Us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the policy has been transferred to another party [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Policy year / Policy anniversary is the 1-year period that starts on the Policy commencement date or any subsequent anniversary of the Policy commencement date [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Premium(s) is the amount of money that You pay to Us to keep this policy in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

- (i) Subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - (ii) Who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act 1966 . It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own Benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md]

Proposal form means the form You signed to buy this policy from Us. It includes anything written, said (and recorded) and/or any document given to Us which has information for Us to rely on before We decide to issue this policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Reinstatement date is the date We reinstate Your policy back to inforce [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

Sum insured is the amount of insurance coverage provided by the Basic policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

> Terminal illness (TI) is the conclusive diagnosis of an illness that is expected to result in the death of the Life insured within twelve (12) months. This diagnosis must be supported by a specialist and confirmed by the Company's appointed Doctor. Terminal illness in the presence of HIV infection is excluded. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md]

## Total and permanent disability (TPD) means any of the two situations: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

1. The Life insured, due to Accident or sickness, is disabled to such an extent as to be rendered totally unable to engage in any occupation, business or activity for income, remuneration or profit; and The disability must continue uninterrupted for at least six (6) consecutive months from the time when disability started; and The disability must, in the view of a Doctor appointed by the Company, be deemed permanent with no possibility of improvement in the foreseeable future [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

2. The Life insured, due to Accident or sickness, suffers total and irrecoverable loss of use of: 3. (a) the entire sight in both eyes; or 4. (b) any two limbs at or above the wrist or ankle; or 5. (c) the entire sight in one eye and any one limb at or above the wrist or ankle [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

We, Our, Us, the Company means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Registration No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

You, Your means the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md].

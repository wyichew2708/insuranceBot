## 6. What is Not Covered?

There are certain conditions under which no Benefits will be payable. These conditions are stated as exclusions. W hen there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our offer of conditional acceptance, the Policy Information Page and Endorsement [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md#p4].

When any of the exclusion happens, We will return the total Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest, less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-direct-etiqa-term-life-ii.md#p4].

## Exclusions

1) There are certain conditions which no Benefit will be payable. These conditions are stated as “exclusions” in the policy contract. The categories of exclusions that are common to all life insurers relate to: a) Suicide within one (1) year (for Death Benefit) b) Self-inflicted injury (for TPD Benefit) [src:raw/product-summaries/direct-etiqa-term-life-ii-product-summary.md#p3].

When there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our offer of conditional acceptance, the Policy Information Page and Endorsement [src:raw/product-summaries/direct-etiqa-term-life-ii-product-summary.md#p3].

When any of the exclusion happens, We will return the higher of either the total Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest, less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/product-summaries/direct-etiqa-term-life-ii-product-summary.md#p3].

2) In addition to the above common categories of exclusions, life insurers may impose other [src:raw/product-summaries/direct-etiqa-term-life-ii-product-summary.md#p3].

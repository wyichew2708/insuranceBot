## 1. Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured while the policy is in force, We will pay S$5,000 less any amounts owing to Us. When We make this payment, the policy ends. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md]

> While We assess the death claims, if the Proper claimant needs financial assistance to prepare the funeral, We may consider offering an immediate advance payment of up to S$3,000 per Life insured. This funeral expense financial aid, if given, will be deducted from the final Death Benefit to the Proper claimant. Our advance payment of this financial aid does not mean We have approved the Death Benefit claim. If the Death Benefit claim is not approved later, after Our assessment, We have the right to ask You to return this advance payment. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md]

## - a) Early and Intermediate Stage CI Benefit; and

- b) Severe Stage CI Benefit [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

> The total amount payable for CI Benefit does not exceed 100% of the Sum insured. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md]

> CI Benefit will end when We have paid 100% of the Sum insured or the Death Benefit or upon expiry of this policy, whichever is earliest. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md]

Please refer to Appendix A for the list of CIs covered and the Policy Contract for their definitions [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

## a) Early and Intermediate Stage CI Benefit

- i) the CI Benefit has not ended; 2. ii) any payment made from this Early and Intermediate Stage CI Benefit will reduce the Sum insured by the same amount paid; 3. iii) only one (1) claim is allowed for this Early and Intermediate Stage CI Benefit; [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

if more than one (1) CI covered under this Early and Intermediate Stage CI Benefit are diagnosed on the same date, We will only process the claim with the highest Benefit payout for this Early and Intermediate Stage CI Benefit; [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

When We make this payment, the Early and Intermediate Stage CI Benefit will end and the policy will continue with the remaining Sum insured for the other eligible Benefits [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

## b) Severe Stage CI Benefit

> If the Life insured is diagnosed with any of the covered severe stage CIs (except for Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery) while the policy is in force, 100% of the Sum insured will be paid in one (1) lump sum, less any claim(s) paid for Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery, Early and Intermediate Stage CI Benefit and Severe Mental Health Benefit, less any amount owing to Us. > If the Life insured is diagnosed with Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery, 10% of the Sum insured will be accelerated and paid in one (1) lump sum, subject to a maximum amount of S$25,000 per policy. This is only payable once and the Sum insured will be reduced accordingly after such payment has been made. Premiums payable remains unchanged and continue to be payable. When We make this payment under this Severe Stage CI Benefit in full, the CI Benefit will end and the policy will: [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

> - i) the Mental Health Benefit has not ended; 2. ii) a maximum of eight (8) claims can be made under this Mild Mental Health Benefit; 3. iii) the total amount payable under this Mild Mental Health Benefit and all policies issued by Us shall not exceed S$1,440 on the same Life insured; [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md]

the Waiting Period (refer to Section 8 for further information); and - v) the Survival Period (refer to Section 8 for further information) [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

> - iv) the total amount payable under this Severe Mental Health Benefit and all policies issued by Us shall not exceed S$25,000 on the same Life insured; - v) the Waiting Period (refer to Section 8 for further information); and - vi) the Survival Period (refer to Section 8 for further information). ## 5. Premium Waiver Benefit for Early and Intermediate Stage CI

Upon a successful claim of a covered early or intermediate stage CI under the Early and Intermediate Stage CI Benefit, all future Premiums for this policy will be waived starting from the next Premium due date immediately after the date of diagnosis of the early or intermediate stage CI, subject to the Maximum Claim Limit (refer to Section 2 (c) for further information) [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

## Death Benefit

> We will not pay the Death Benefit if the death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md]

We will return the total Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest, less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

## CI Benefit, Mental Health Benefit and Continuous Care Benefit

We do not pay any Benefit if the condition is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

- a) a Pre-existing condition; - b) intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; - c) effects of drug or alcohol, narcotics or psychotropic substances unless taken in accordance and compliance with the lawful directions and prescription of a registered medical practitioner; [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

- d) Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV; - e) participation in a criminal or unlawful act; or [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

- f) war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities (whether war be declared or not), armed or unarmed truce, civil war, mutiny, rebellion, any act of terrorism, revolution, insurrection, military or usurped power, riot or civil commotion, strikes [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

## CI Benefit

We will not pay any CI Benefit if: [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

- a) the date of diagnosis of Major Cancer, Heart Attack of Specified Severity, or Other Serious Coronary Artery Disease; - b) the date of diagnosis of coronary artery disease leading to performance of Coronary Artery By-pass Surgery; or - c) the date of diagnosis of the condition leading to the performance of Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment For Coronary Artery, [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

> is within ninety (90) days from the Policy issue date, latest Reinstatement date or the latest increase in Sum insured, whichever is later. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md]

## Mental Health Benefit

> We will not pay the Mild Mental Health Benefit if the date of psychiatrist consultation is within twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date, latest Reinstatement date or the latest increase in Sum insured, whichever is later. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md]

> We will not pay the Severe Mental Health Benefit if the date of diagnosis of the covered mental health condition under the Severe Mental Health Benefit is within ninety (90) days from the Policy issue date, latest Reinstatement date or the latest increase in Sum insured, whichever is later. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md]

## Continuous Care Benefit

We will not pay the Continuous Care Benefit if: [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

- a) the date of diagnosis of Major Cancer, Heart Attack of Specified Severity, or Other Serious Coronary Artery Disease; or - b) the date of diagnosis of coronary artery disease leading to performance of Coronary Artery By-pass Surgery, [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md].

> We will not pay the second claim of Continuous Care Benefit if the date of diagnosis of a subsequent covered severe stage CI is within twelve (12) months from the date of diagnosis of the first Continuous Care Benefit claim. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-for-essential-critical-secure-final.md]

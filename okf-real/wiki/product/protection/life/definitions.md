## 11 What Do We Mean With These Words?

Age means the Age at next birthday [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Aggregate sums insured means the sum of the same type of benefits under all policies covering a single Life insured with Us [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Assignee means any person to whom You have transferred Your rights as the Policy owner and the assignment is registered with Us [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Basic policy means the policy as it exists, including the supplementary terms and any Endorsement made to it, without any optional supplementary contract / rider [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Benefit(s) means any payments that We will pay and/or the amount of Premium that We will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Cash values means the monetary value the basic policy has accumulated over the lifetime of the policy, minus any outstanding amount that You owe Us [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Doctor means a licensed person who is qualified by degree in western medicine to practice medicine. The license is given by the appropriate medical authority of his country of residence to practice medicine within his scope of licensing and training. This cannot be You, the Life insured, a family member or a relative [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Effective date means the date We start to cover You. This is either the date We approve Your application for this policy or the date We receive Your first premiums in full, whichever is later [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by Us [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Expiry date means the date the policy ends and where no benefit is payable [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Face value means the notional value used to determine the non-guaranteed bonuses, the maturity Benefit and the yearly cash payout. It is not the sum insured of Your policy [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Guaranteed issuance means We issue the policy without asking for Your health declaration or medical checks at the time of application [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Instalment premium is the premium You pay on a more frequent basis, other than yearly [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Key life events is when one of the three events happens: [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

- i. the Life insured marries - ii. the Life insured becomes a parent and has a new born child, or legally adopts a child or - iii. the life insured graduates from tertiary education [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Life insured means the person whom We provide the cover for. The Life insured does not have any right to the policy, unless he/she is also the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Maturity date is the date the policy ends and where a maturity Benefit is payable [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Non-participating means it does not share in any surplus or profits of the company's fund [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

> Nominee(s) is a person that You have nominated (under the Insurance Act 1966 and Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009 ) to receive the policy monies payable under the policy upon Your death. The nomination must be registered with Us. [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md]

Paid-up means You stop paying any future Premiums and the Benefit is reduced accordingly [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Participating means it shares in the surplus or profits of the life Participating fund [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Policy commencement date means the date the policy commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page. This is the date We take as the policy anniversary . On the anniversary dates the subsequent premiums are due [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Policy issue date means the date We issue the policy. This is shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Policy Information Page or any Endorsement issued by Us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the policy has been transferred to another party [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Premium(s) is the amount of money that You pay singly or regularly to Us to keep this policy alive so You may claim for the Benefits [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Pre-existing condition means the existence of any signs or symptoms before the policy effective date or reinstatement date for which treatment, medication, consultation, advice, or diagnosis has been sought or received by the Life insured or would have caused any reasonable and sensible person to get medical advice or treatment [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act 1966 . It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own Benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased. [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md]

Proposal form means the form You signed to buy this policy from Us. It includes anything written, said (and recorded) and/or any document given to Us which has information for Us to rely on before We decide to issue this policy [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Reinstatement date is the date We reactivate Your policy to bring it back to life after it ended due to no premium payment [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Sum insured is the amount of insurance coverage provided by the basic policy [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

> Total and permanent disability is a disability that lasts continuously for at least 180 days, which the doctor considers permanent and expects it to last throughout the lifetime of the Life insured. This means one of the following: [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md]

> - i. A disability where the Life insured whose age is between 17 and 65 cannot perform any work or occupation, or - ii. A disability where the Life insured suffers from any of these: 3.  Total and irrecoverable loss of sight in both eyes; or 4.  Loss of two or more limbs, each above the wrist or ankle; or 5.  Total and irrecoverable loss of sight in one eye and loss of one limb at or above the wrist or ankle. [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md]

- iii. A disability where the Life insured cannot do at least 3 out of 6 Activities of Daily Living without physical help from another person or mechanical equipment [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

The six (6) Activities of Daily Living are: [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Washing - The ability to wash in the bath or shower (including getting into and out of the bath or shower) or wash by other means [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Dressing - The ability to put on, take off, secure and unfasten all garments and, as appropriate, any braces, artificial limbs or other surgical or medical appliances [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Feeding - The ability to feed oneself food after it has been prepared and made available [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Toileting - The ability to use the lavatory or manage bowel and bladder function through the use of protective undergarments or surgical appliances if appropriate [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Mobility - The ability to move indoors from room to room on level surfaces [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

Transferring - The ability to move from a bed to an upright chair or wheelchair, and vice versa [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

> - iv. A disability where the Life insured has to be confined to a home, a hospital or other institution in order to receive constant care and medical attention. If the Life insured is unemployed at the time of disability, then only (ii) and (iii) shall apply. If the Life insured suffers from TPD before reaching 17 years old, only definitions (ii) and (iv) shall [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md]

Waiting period the time period during which We do not pay the Benefit. This period begins from the: [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

- a) policy issue date; - b) Policy commencement date; - c) date of increase/addition of any Benefits; or - d) date of reinstatement [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

We, Our, Us, the Company means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Co. Reg. No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

You, Your means the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/general-provisions-for-life-insurance.md].

## About CI Protection Rider

## 2 Our Responsibilities

> This is a Non-participating rider that accelerates the death Benefit of the Basic policy if the Life insured is diagnosed with any one of the covered 36 severe-stage critical illnesses while the rider is in force. You have the option of accelerating the death Benefit of the Basic policy by 20%, 50%, 80% or 100%. At the rider Expiry date, if We have not paid out any claims, this rider will end and no Benefits will be payable. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md]

This rider can only be attached at the policy application of the Basic policy . This rider covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md].

This Policy Contract should be read together with the Policy Contract of the Basic policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md].

## 2.1 Critical Illness (CI) Benefit

> This rider pays the CI Benefit as an advancement of the death Benefit of the Basic policy if the Life insured is diagnosed with a covered severe-stage CI while the rider is in force. If the CI benefit payable is lesser than the death Benefit of the Basic policy, the death Benefit of the Basic policy will be reduced by the amount paid for the CI. You must continue to pay the revised Premiums to keep the Basic policy in force. If Our payment for CI Benefit is the full 100% death Benefit of the Basic policy, both the Basic policy and this rider will be terminated. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md]

> We pay this CI Benefit in full only for one of the covered severe-stage CI, except for Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery. If the severe-stage CI diagnosed is Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery, We will pay 10% of the CI Benefit of this rider, subject to a maximum amount of S$25,000 per policy. The remaining CI Benefit (if any) will be paid on the next covered severe-stage CI diagnosed that is not Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery. You must continue to pay the revised Premiums to keep the rider in force. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md]

If the death Benefit of the Basic policy is reduced due to a claim for TPD Benefit under the Basic policy, We will reduce the CI Benefit of this rider proportionally. The Premium of the rider will be reduced accordingly and You must continue to pay the revised Premiums to keep the rider in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md].

> The maximum aggregate amount payable is Two Million Singapore Dollars (S$2,000,000) per Life insured for all policies and riders issued by Us with CI Benefits. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md]

Please refer to Appendix A for the definitions of the covered critical illnesses [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md].

## 4.1 Critical Illness (CI) Benefit

We will not pay any Benefit if the CI is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md].

- A Pre-existing condition; - Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; - Effects of drug or alcohol addiction; or - Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md].

## 5.2 Who Do We Pay Benefits To

If the policy has been assigned, the Benefit amount will be paid to the Assignee. If the policy has not been assigned, the Benefit amount will be paid to either You or Your executors, administrators, Nominees or any other Proper claimant, provided We have proof, as deemed sufficient by Us, of the relationship of the person claiming the Benefit [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md].

Before We pay any Benefit, We will deduct any amount You owe on this policy from the Benefit. By paying out the Benefit, it will end Our legal responsibility on this policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-ci-protection-rider-v1-23.md].

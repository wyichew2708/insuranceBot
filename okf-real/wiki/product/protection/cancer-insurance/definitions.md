## 10. What Do We Mean With These Words?

Age means the age at next birthday [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Assignee means any person to whom you have transferred your rights as the Policy Owner and the assignment is registered with us [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Benefit(s) means any payments that we will pay when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Doctor means a licensed person who is qualified by degree in western medicine to practice medicine. The license is given by the appropriate medical authority of his country of residence to practice medicine within his scope of licensing and training. This cannot be you, the Life insured, a family member or a relative [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

*Cancer means a malignant tumour positively diagnosed with histological confirmation and characterised by the uncontrolled growth of malignant cells with invasion and destruction of normal tissue. It includes carcinoma-in-situ, a focal autonomous new growth of carcinomatous cells which have not yet infiltrated normal tissue beyond the epithelial basement membrane. The diagnosis of carcinoma- in- situ must always be positively diagnosed upon the basis of a microscopic examination of the fixed tissue, supported by a biopsy result. Clinical diagnosis does not meet this standard [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Cancer diagnosed on the basis of finding tumour cells and/or tumour-associated molecules in blood, saliva, faeces, urine or any other bodily fluid in the absence of further definitive and clinically verifiable evidence does not meet the above definition [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

For the above definition, the following are excluded: [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

- All tumours which are histologically classified as any of the following: - -Pre-malignant; - -Non-invasive; - -Having borderline malignancy; - -Having any degree of malignant potential; - -Having suspicious malignancy; - -Neoplasm of uncertain or unknown behaviour; or - -Cervical Dysplasia CIN -1, CIN-2 - Any non-melanoma skin carcinoma, skin confined primary cutaneous lymphoma and dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans unless there is evidence of metastases to lymph nodes or beyond; [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

- Malignant melanoma that has not caused invasion beyond the epidermis; - All tumours in the presence of HIV infection [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

> *The Life Insurance Association Singapore (LIA) has standard Definitions for 37 severe-stage Critical Illnesses (Version 2019). This Critical Illness does not fall under Version 2019. For Critical Illnesses that do not fall under Version 2019, the definitions are determined by the insurance company. You may refer to www.lia.org.sg for the standard Definitions (Version 2019). [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md]

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by us [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Life insured means the person whom we provide the cover for. The Life insured does not have any right to the policy, unless he/she is also the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

> Nominee(s) is a person that you have nominated (under the Insurance Act 1966 or Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009 ) to receive the policy monies payable under the policy upon the Life insured's death. The nomination must be registered with us. [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md]

Non-participating means it does not share in any surplus or profits of the Company's fund [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Policy commencement date means the date the policy commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page. This is the date we take as the policy anniversary [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Policy issue date means the date we issue the policy. This is shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Policy Information Page or any Endorsement issued by Us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the policy has been transferred to another party [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Premium(s) is the amount of money that you pay either monthly or annually to us, as shown in your Policy Information Page to keep this policy in force [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Pre-existing condition means the existence of any signs or symptoms before the policy effective date for which treatment, medication, consultation, advice, or diagnosis has been sought or received by the Life insured or would have caused any reasonable and sensible person to get medical advice or treatment [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

- a) subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict Us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - b) who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act 1966 . It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased. [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md]

Proposal form means the form you signed to buy this policy from Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. It includes anything written, said (and recorded) and/or any document given to us which has information for us to rely on before we decide to issue this policy [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

Sum insured is the amount of insurance coverage provided by this policy [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

We, Our, Us, the Company means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

You, Your means the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/cancer-insurance-policy-contract-v1-25.md].

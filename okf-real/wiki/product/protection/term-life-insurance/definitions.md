## 11. What Do We Mean With These Words?

Age means the Age at next birthday [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Accident means a sudden, unforeseen and unplanned event causing bodily injury that is violent, external and visible in nature. It is not caused by sickness, disease, gradual physical, mental changes or any other causes. The Accident must occur after the Policy issue date [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Assignee means any person to whom You have transferred Your rights as the Policy o wner and the assignment is registered with Us [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Basic policy means the policy as it exists, including the supplementary terms and any Endorsement made to it, without any optional supplementary contract / rider [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Benefit(s) means any payments that We will pay and/or the amount of Premium that We will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Doctor means a licensed person who is qualified by degree in western medicine to practice medicine. The license is given by the appropriate medical authority of his country of residence to practice medicine within his scope of licensing and training. This cannot be You, the Life insured, a family member or a relative [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by Us [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Life insured means the person whom We provide the cover for. The Life insured does not have any right to the policy, unless he/she is also the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

> Nominee(s) is a person that You have nominated (under the Insurance Act, Chapter 142 and Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009 ) to receive the policy monies payable under the policy upon Your death. The nomination must be registered with Us. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md]

Non-participating means it does not share in any surplus or profits of the Company's fund [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Policy commencement date means the date the policy commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Policy issue date means the date We issue the policy. This is shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Policy Information Page or any Endorsement issued by Us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the policy has been transferred to another party [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Policy year / Policy anniversary is the 1-year period that starts on the Policy commencement date or any subsequent anniversary of the Policy commencement date [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Premium(s) is the amount of money that You pay to Us to keep this policy in force [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act, Chapter 142 . It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own Benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md]

Proposal form means the form You signed to buy this policy from Us. It includes anything written, said (and recorded) and/or any document given to Us which has information for Us to rely on before We decide to issue this policy [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Reinstatement date is the date we reinstate your policy back to inforce [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Sum insured is the amount of insurance coverage provided by the Basic policy [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

> Terminal illness is the conclusive diagnosis of an illness that is expected to result in the death of the Life insured within twelve (12) months. This diagnosis must be supported by a specialist and confirmed by the Company's appointed Doctor. Terminal illness in the presence of HIV infection is excluded. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md]

Total and permanent disability means any of the two situations: [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

1. The Life insured, due to Accident or sickness, is disabled to such an extent as to be rendered totally unable to engage in any occupation, business or activity for income, remuneration or profit; and The disability must continue uninterrupted for at least six (6) consecutive months from the time when disability started; and The disability must, in the view of a medical examiner appointed by the Company, be deemed permanent with no possibility of improvement in the foreseeable future [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

2. The Life insured, due to Accident or sickness, suffers total and irrecoverable loss of use of: 3. (a) the entire sight in both eyes; or 4. (b) any two limbs at or above the wrist or ankle; or 5. (c) the entire sight in one eye and any one limb at or above the wrist or ankle [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

We, Our, Us, the Company means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Registration No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

You, Your means the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

Cash values means the monetary value the basic policy has accumulated over the lifetime of the policy, minus any outstanding amount that You owe Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Effective date means the date We start to cover You. This is either the date We approve Your application for this policy or the date We receive Your first premiums in full, whichever is later [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Expiry date means the date the policy ends and where no benefit is payable [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Face value means the notional value used to determine the non-guaranteed bonuses, the maturity Benefit and the yearly cash payout. It is not the sum insured of Your policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Guaranteed issuance means We issue the policy without asking for Your health declaration or medical checks at the time of application [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Instalment premium is the premium You pay on a more frequent basis, other than yearly [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Key life events is when one of the three events happens: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

- i. the Life insured marries - ii. the Life insured becomes a parent and has a new born child, or legally adopts a child or - iii. the life insured graduates from tertiary education [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Maturity date is the date the policy ends and where a maturity Benefit is payable [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Paid-up means You stop paying any future Premiums and the Benefit is reduced accordingly [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Participating means it shares in the surplus or profits of the life Participating fund [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Policy commencement date means the date the policy commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page. This is the date We take as the policy anniversary . On the anniversary dates the subsequent premiums are due [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Premium(s) is the amount of money that You pay singly or regularly to Us to keep this policy alive so You may claim for the Benefits [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Pre-existing condition means the existence of any signs or symptoms before the policy effective date or reinstatement date for which treatment, medication, consultation, advice, or diagnosis has been sought or received by the Life insured or would have caused any reasonable and sensible person to get medical advice or treatment [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Reinstatement date is the date We reactivate Your policy to bring it back to life after it ended due to no premium payment [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

> Total and permanent disability is a disability that lasts continuously for at least 180 days, which the doctor considers permanent and expects it to last throughout the lifetime of the Life insured. This means one of the following: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

> - i. A disability where the Life insured whose age is between 17 and 65 cannot perform any work or occupation, or - ii. A disability where the Life insured suffers from any of these: - Total and irrecoverable loss of sight in both eyes; or - Loss of two or more limbs, each above the wrist or ankle; or - Total and irrecoverable loss of sight in one eye and loss of one limb at or above the wrist or ankle. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

- iii. A disability where the Life insured cannot do at least 3 out of 6 Activities of Daily Living without physical help from another person or mechanical equipment [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

The six (6) Activities of Daily Living are: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Washing - The ability to wash in the bath or shower (including getting into and out of the bath or shower) or wash by other means [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Dressing - The ability to put on, take off, secure and unfasten all garments and, as appropriate, any braces, artificial limbs or other surgical or medical appliances [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Feeding - The ability to feed oneself food after it has been prepared and made available [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Toileting - The ability to use the lavatory or manage bowel and bladder function through the use of protective undergarments or surgical appliances if appropriate [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Mobility - The ability to move indoors from room to room on level surfaces [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Transferring - The ability to move from a bed to an upright chair or wheelchair, and vice versa [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

- iv. A disability where the Life insured has to be confined to a home, a hospital or other institution in order to receive constant care and medical attention [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

If the Life insured is unemployed at the time of disability, then only (ii) and (iii) shall apply [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

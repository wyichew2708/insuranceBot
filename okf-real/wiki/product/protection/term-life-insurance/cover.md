## 3 Our Responsibilities

Your policy may cover all or some of the Benefits described below  [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

You can find the Benefit(s) covered under this policy on the Policy Information Page and/or the supplementary terms [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

## 3.1 Covered Events

Depending on the Benefits, terms and conditions of Your policy, if Your policy is valid and a covered event happens, We will pay You the Sum insured/Benefits less any loan and interest You owe Us on this policy or its supplementary rider contract. The covered events of Your policy are written on the Policy Information Page and/or the supplementary terms [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

## 3.2 Guaranteed Insurability Benefit

If Your policy has a guaranteed insurability Benefit, You can choose to increase the Life insured's protection without giving Us proof of good health whenever the Life insured experiences a key life event. You can buy a new term or an endowment policy on the Life insured for a maximum of 2 key life events per life. This is the case no matter how many policies We insure him/her. You can only do so if: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

> - the Life insured is under age 50; - Your policy is issued on standard terms with neither specific conditions which We will not cover nor extra premiums which We charge You due to medical reasons and - You have paid all the premiums due under this policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

> The most You can buy for each new policy is the lower of either 25% of the original sum insured or $150,000. You have to buy the new policy within 3 months when the key life event happens. You must pay the increased premium on the new policy based on the age of the Life insured at the time of purchase. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

## 3.3 Cash Benefits

If Your policy has a cash Benefit, You can choose to [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

- i. deposit the amount with Us and earn interest at a rate We will set or - ii. receive the amount as a payout [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

> We may change the interest rate at any time by giving You 30 days' notice. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

> At the time You apply for the policy, We will ask for Your choice. If You did not state Your choice, We will assume that Your choice is to deposit the amount with Us. You may withdraw the deposits with Us at any time with no charges but the minimum withdrawal amount each time is the lesser of either $1000 or the entire deposit amount. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

If there is a claim, the claim Benefit payment will include any cash Benefits and earned interest that is deposited with Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

## 3.4 Cash Values & Paid-up Policy

If Your policy accumulates a cash value, You may [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

- i. - end Your policy and cash out the amount. The cash value will be much lower than the premiums paid if You end Your policy in the first few years; [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

- ii. convert this policy to a paid-up policy so that You do not have to pay future premiums. The coverage will be reduced [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Once it is a paid-up policy, We will stop paying cash Benefits or adding bonuses to Your policy. You also cannot apply for a policy loan [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

## 3.5 Loans

> You may take a loan on Your policy for up to 90% of its cash value. We will charge You interest on the policy loan and may change the interest rate at any time by giving You 30 days' notice. Interest accrues on a daily basis. You may repay the policy loan and interest at any time. If the policy loan plus interest is more than the cash value, this policy will end. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

We will deduct the policy loan and interest from any amount We may be due to pay to You under this policy and/or its supplementary contracts [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

## 3.6 Bonus

If this is a participating policy, a part of the premiums You pay will go into a life participating fund. We will share the profit or loss of this fund with You. Profits will be shared with You by giving You bonuses in three ways: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

We add this to the policy each year. Once added, We cannot remove it [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

This is a percentage of the total reversionary bonuses. We add this only at the time We pay a claim or when You give up Your policy for its cash value [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

This is a percentage of the total reversionary bonuses. We add this only at the policy Maturity date [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

Bonuses are not guaranteed until they are declared to You [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

## 3.7 Maturity Benefit

If Your policy has a maturity Benefit, We will pay You the amount less any loan and interest You owe Us on this policy on the Maturity date of this policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

## 5 What is Covered?

Your policy may cover all or some of the Benefits described below. You have to read the Policy Information Page and/or the supplementary terms to find out the exact coverage [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

If Your supplementary terms show this Benefit and the Life insured dies, We will pay the sum insured plus all the bonuses that We have added to Your policy (if any) less any amounts owing to Us. When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

> While We assess the death claims, if the claimant needs financial assistance to prepare the funeral, We may consider offering an immediate payment of up to $3000 per insured life. This funeral expense financial aid, if given, will be deducted from the final death Benefit to the claimant. Our advance payment of this financial aid does not mean We have approved the death claim. When the death claim is not approved later, after Our assessment, We have the right to ask You to return this advance payment. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

## 5.2 Terminal Illness Benefit

> If Your supplementary terms show this Benefit and the Life insured has a terminal illness before reaching age 71, We will pay the sum insured plus all the bonuses that We have added to Your policy (if any) less any amounts owing to Us. When We make this payment, the policy ends. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

## 5.3 Total and Permanent Disability (TPD) Benefit

> If Your supplementary terms show this Benefit and the Life insured becomes totally and permanently disabled before reaching age 71, We will pay in one lump sum the sum insured plus all the bonuses that We have added to Your policy (if any) less any amounts owing to Us. We will only provide a maximum aggregate sum insured of $4 million per insured life for all policies issued in Singapore with TPD Benefits. Our payment on TPD Benefits will proportionately reduce the sum insured of the basic policy. If Our payment for TPD benefit is 100% of the sum insured under this policy, the policy ends. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

## 6.2 Terminal Illness Benefit

We will not pay the terminal illness benefit if it is due to: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

- i. Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-harm or attempted suicide - ii. AIDS, AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of HIV except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

## 6.3 Total and Permanent Disability (TPD) Benefit

We will not pay the TPD Benefit or waive any premiums if TPD occurs due to: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

- i. Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-harm or attempted suicide - ii. Criminal acts, war (declared or not), terrorism and chemical warfare - iii. Participating in aviation (except as fare- paying passenger or member crew of a commercial airline), any dangerous or hazardous sport or hobby such as (but not limited to) steeple chasing, polo, horse racing, underwater diving, hunting, motor vehicular racing, mountaineering or potholing, or aerial sports such as skydiving, parachuting, bungee jumping - iv. Effects of drug or alcohol addiction - v. HIV infection, Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) and any AIDS related conditions [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

## 4. Terminal Illness Benefit

If the life insured has a terminal illness during the policy term, we will pay the sum insured less any amounts owing to us. When we make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

## 5. Total and Permanent Disability (TPD) Benefit

> If the life insured becomes totally and permanently disabled before reaching age 65, we will pay in one lump sum the sum insured less any amounts owing to us. We will only provide a maximum aggregate sum insured of $4 million per insured life for all policies issued in Singapore with TPD benefits. Our payment on TPD benefits will proportionately reduce the sum insured of the basic policy. If our payment for TPD benefit is 100% of the sum insured under this policy, the policy ends. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

6. Total and permanent disability definition is defined as follow: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

> For ages between 19 and 65, Total and permanent disability means any of the two situations: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

1. The life insured, due to accident or sickness, is disabled to such an extent as to be rendered totally unable to engage in any occupation, business or activity for income, remuneration or profit; and The disability must continue uninterrupted for at least 6 consecutive months from the time when disability started; and The disability must, in the view of a medical examiner appointed by the company, be deemed permanent with no possibility of improvement in the foreseeable future [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

2. The life insured, due to accident or sickness, suffers total and irrecoverable loss of use of: - a) the entire sight in both eyes; or - b) any two limbs at or above the wrist or ankle; or - c) the entire sight in one eye and any one limb at or above the wrist or ankle 7. Your policy will end when one of these events happens first: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md].

> - a) death of the life insured; - b) we paid out 100% of the death benefit of this policy; - c) premium is not paid on time; - d) at the end of the policy term (applicable for policy term of 20 years and up to age 65) - e) you choose not to renew your policy at the end of the policy term (applicable for policy term of 5 years (renewable)); or - f) your written request and our acceptance of the application to terminate the policy. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-term-life-v1-23-2024-01.md]

## 3. Our Responsibilities

This plan is a Non-participating, regular premium level term policy that provides the Sum insured upon the death, Total and permanent disability or Terminal illness of the Life insured during the policy term. This plan does not have any cash values. At the end of the policy term, if We have not paid out any claims, no Benefits will be payable [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

> This plan is available for policy term of 5 years (renewable), 20 years or up to Age 65. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md]

Your policy covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

## 3.1 Covered Events

If Your policy is valid and a covered event happens, We will pay You the Benefits less any amounts You owe Us on this policy. The covered events of Your policy are written on the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

## 5. What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefits described below while the policy is in force [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

Upon death of the Life insured, We will pay the Sum insured less any amounts owing to Us. When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

> While We assess the death claims, if the claimant needs financial assistance to prepare the funeral, We may consider offering an immediate payment of up to S$3,000 per Life insured. This funeral expense financial aid, if given, will be deducted from the final death benefit to the claimant. Our advance payment of this financial aid does not mean We have approved the death claim. If the death claim is not approved later, after Our assessment, We have the right to ask You to return this advance payment. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md]

## 5.2 Total and Permanent Disability (TPD) Benefit

> If the Life insured becomes totally and permanently disabled on or before reaching the Policy anniversary when he attains Age 65 while the policy is in force, We will pay the death Benefit in one lump sum. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md]

> The maximum aggregate amount payable is Four Million Singapore Dollars (S$4,000,000) per Life insured for all policies and riders issued by Us with TPD Benefits. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md]

If the TPD Benefit payable is lesser than death Benefit, the death Benefit will be automatically reduced by the amount paid for the TPD. The policy remains in force and the remaining death Benefit is the original death Benefit less the amount paid for TPD [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

If the TPD Benefit payable is the same as the death Benefit, the policy ends when We make this payment [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

## 5.3 Terminal Illness Benefit

If the Life insured has a Terminal illness during the policy term, We will pay the death Benefit in one lump sum. When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

## 6.1 Death Benefit

> We do not pay the death Benefit if the death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy Issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md]

## 6.2 Total and Permanent Disability (TPD) Benefit

We do not pay the TPD Benefit if the TPD is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

- a) Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; - b) Criminal acts, war (declared or not), terrorism and chemical warfare; - c) Participating in aviation (except as fare-paying passenger or member crew of a commercial airline), any dangerous or hazardous sport or hobby such as (but not limited to) steeple chasing, polo, horse racing, underwater diving, hunting, motor vehicular racing, mountaineering or potholing, or aerial sports such as skydiving, parachuting, bungee jumping; [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

- d) Effects of drug or alcohol addiction; or - e) Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

## 6.3 Terminal Illness Benefit

We do not pay the Terminal illness Benefit if the Terminal illness is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

- a) Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; - b) AIDS, AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of HIV except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

When there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our offer of conditional acceptance, the Policy Information Page and Endorsement. When any of the exclusion happens, We will return the total Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest, less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-term-life-policy-contract.md].

## Death Benefit

Upon death of the Life insured while the policy is in force, We will pay the Sum insured less any amounts owing to Us. When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/product-summaries/eprotect-term-life-product-summary-tiq-v1-23.md].

## Terminal Illness Benefit

> The maximum aggregate amount payable is Five Million Singapore Dollars (S$5,000,000) per Life insured for all policies and riders issued by Us with Terminal illness Benefit. [src:raw/product-summaries/eprotect-term-life-product-summary-tiq-v1-23.md]

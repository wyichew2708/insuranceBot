## About Advanced CI Rider

## 2 Our Responsibilities

> This is a Non-participating rider that accelerates the death Benefit of the Basic policy if the Life insured is diagnosed with any one of the covered 36 severe-stage critical illnesses while the rider is in force. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

This rider does not have any cash values. At the rider Expiry date, if We have not paid out any claims, the rider will end and no Benefits will be payable [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

Your policy covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

This Policy Contract should be read together with the Policy Contract of the Basic policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 2.1 Covered Events

If Your policy is valid and a covered event happens, We will pay You the Benefits less any amounts You owe Us on this policy. The covered events of Your policy are written on the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 2.3 Guaranteed Convertibility

When You exercise the option to convert Your Basic policy, this rider will be converted automatically to a new rider available at the point of conversion, without giving Us proof of good health if the following conditions are met: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

> - a) this rider is in force when this option is exercised; - b) All the Premiums under this rider are paid up to-date; - c) the Life insured has not reached Age 66 when this option is exercised; - d) the Life insured of the new rider must be the same as this rider; [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

- e) the Life insured is insured for the same coverage amount or less on the new rider, subject to the minimum and maximum coverage amount of the new rider. If You choose a higher coverage [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

amount for the new rider, any excess coverage amount will be subject to underwriting and any waiting period will re-start from the effective date of the conversion; [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

- f) The new rider covers the same insured events as this rider and also accelerates the death Benefit of the Basic policy; and - g) there are no previously admitted or submitted claims on this rider [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

If this rider is issued on non-standard terms, the same terms, conditions and exclusions shall continue to be applicable on the new rider. The conversion cannot be reverted once the new rider is effective [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 2.4 Option to Increase Sum Insured

This rider provides an option to increase the Life insured's protection without giving Us proof of good health whenever the Life insured experiences a key life event [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

- a) the Life insured changes marital status (for e.g. marries, divorces); - b) the Life insured becomes a parent by having a newborn child or legally adopts a child; - c) the Life insured purchases a property; - d) the Life insured or Life insured's child enrolls into tertiary education; [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

- e) the Life insured graduated from tertiary education [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

You can exercise this option for a maximum of 2 key life events per Life insured. You can only do so if the following conditions are met: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

> - a) Your rider is fully underwritten and issued on standard terms without any exclusions; - b) there are no previously admitted or submitted claims on this rider; - c) the key life event must occur before the Policy anniversary when the Life insured attains Age 50; [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

> - d) the application to exercise this option must be submitted within 90 calendar days following the occurrence of the key life event; - e) All the Premiums under this rider are paid up to-date; and - f) The total Sum insured that can be increased under this option regardless of the number of times it is exercised is the lower of either 50% of the rider's Sum insured at rider commencement date or S$200,000, per Life insured. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

> If You have more than one policy with Us which offers similar option, You can exercise this option for a maximum of 2 key life events per Life insured and the maximum aggregate coverage that can be increased under this option per Life insured is S$200,000. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

The increased Sum insured will be effective on the next Policy Anniversary immediately after the approval of Your application and the Premium payable for the increased Sum insured for the remaining term of the rider will be calculated based on the Life insured's Age at the next Policy Anniversary [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 4 What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefits described below while the policy is in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 4.1 Critical Illness (CI) Benefit

This rider pays the CI Benefit as an advancement of the death Benefit of the Basic policy if the Life insured is diagnosed with a covered severe-stage critical illness while the rider is in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

> The maximum aggregate amount payable is Two Million Singapore Dollars (S$2,000,000) per Life insured for all policies and riders issued by Us with CI Benefits. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

If the CI Benefit payable is lesser than the death Benefit of the Basic policy, the death Benefit will be reduced by the amount paid for the CI. The policy remains in force and the remaining death Benefit is the original death Benefit less the amount paid for CI [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

If the CI Benefit payable is the same as the death Benefit of the Basic policy, both the Basic policy and this rider ends when We make this payment [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

> We pay this CI Benefit in full only for one of the covered critical illnesses, except for Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery. If the critical illness diagnosed is Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery, We will pay 10% of the CI Benefit of this rider, subject to a maximum amount of S$25,000 per policy. The remaining CI Benefit (if any) will be paid on the next covered critical illness diagnosed that is not Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery. You must continue to pay the revised Premiums to keep the rider in force. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

If the death Benefit of the Basic policy is reduced due to a claim for Terminal illness Benefit under the Basic policy or Total and permanent disability Benefit under any other riders, We will reduce the CI Benefit of this rider proportionally. The Premium of the rider will be reduced accordingly and You must continue to pay the revised Premiums to keep this rider in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

> The 36 covered Critical Illnesses are listed below: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

Please refer to Appendix A for the definitions of the covered critical illnesses [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 5.1 Critical Illness (CI) Benefit

We will not pay any Benefit if the CI is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

- A Pre-existing condition; - Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; - Effects of drug or alcohol addiction; or - Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 11 Appendix A - List of Critical Illnesses Covered

Critical illness means any one of the following: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-advanced-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 1. Critical Illness (CI) Benefit

If the Life insured is diagnosed with any of the covered severe-stage CI (except for Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery) while the rider is in force, We will pay the CI Benefit in one lump sum as an advancement of the Death Benefit of the Basic policy. When We make this payment, both the rider and the Basic policy ends [src:raw/product-summaries/advanced-ci-rider-product-summary.md].

> If the Life insured is diagnosed with Angioplasty &amp; Other Invasive Treatment for Coronary Artery, We will pay 10% of the CI Benefit, subject to a maximum amount of S$25,000 per policy. This is only payable once and the CI Benefit will be reduced accordingly after such payment has been made. You must continue to pay the revised insurance charge to keep the rider in force [src:raw/product-summaries/advanced-ci-rider-product-summary.md]

Please refer to Appendix B for the definitions of the covered critical illnesses [src:raw/product-summaries/advanced-ci-rider-product-summary.md].

## CI Benefit

We do not pay any Benefit if the CI is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/product-summaries/advanced-ci-rider-product-summary.md].

- a) a Pre-existing condition; - b) intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; - c) effects of drug or alcohol addiction; or - d) Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV [src:raw/product-summaries/advanced-ci-rider-product-summary.md].

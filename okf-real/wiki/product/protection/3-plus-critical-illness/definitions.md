## 9. What Do We Mean With These Words?

Activities of Daily Living refers to Washing, Dressing, Feeding, Toileting, Mobility and Transferring. a) Washing - The ability to wash in the bath or shower (including getting into and out of the bath or shower) or wash by other means [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

b) Dressing - The ability to put on, take off, secure and unfasten all garments and, as appropriate, any braces, artificial limbs or other surgical or medical appliances. c) Feeding - The ability to feed oneself food after it has been prepared and made available [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

d) Toileting - The ability to use the lavatory or manage bowel and bladder function through the use of protective undergarments or surgical appliances if appropriate. e) Mobility - The ability to move indoors from room to room on level surfaces [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

f) Transferring - The ability to move from a bed to an upright chair or wheelchair, and vice versa. Age means the Age at next birthday. Assignee means any person to whom You have transferred Your rights as the Policy owner and the assignment is registered with Us [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

Basic policy means the policy as it exists, including the supplementary terms and any Endorsement made to it, without any optional supplementary Benefit / rider. Benefit(s) means any payments that We will pay and/or the amount of Premium that We will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

Doctor means a licensed person who is qualified by degree in western medicine to practice medicine. The license is given by the appropriate medical authority of his country of residence to practice medicine within his scope of licensing and training. This cannot be the Life insured, a family member or a relative [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by Us. Expiry date means the date the policy ends and where no Benefit is payable [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

> Life insured means the person whom We provide the cover for. The Life insured does not have any right to the policy, unless he/she is also the Policy owner. Nominee(s) is a person that You have nominated (under the Insurance Act 1966 and Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009) to receive the policy monies payable under the policy upon Your death. The nomination must be registered with Us. [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10]

Non-participating means it does not share in any surplus or profits of the Company’s fund. Policy commencement date means the date the policy commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page. Policy issue date means the date We issue the policy. This is shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Policy Information Page or any Endorsement issued by Us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the policy has been transferred to another party. Premium(s) is the amount of money that You pay to Us to keep this policy in force [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

Pre-existing condition means the existence of any signs or symptoms before the Policy issue date, the last increase in policy’s Sum insured made, whichever is later, for which treatment, medication, consultation, advice, or diagnosis has been sought or received by the Life insured or would have caused any reasonable and sensible person to get medical advice or treatment [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: a) subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict Us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or b) who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act 1966. It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own Benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased. [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10]

Proposal form means the form You signed to buy this policy from Us. It includes anything written, said (and recorded) and/or any document given to Us which has information for Us to rely on before We decide to issue this policy [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

Sum insured is the amount of insurance coverage provided by the Basic policy or optional rider (where applicable). We, Our, Us, the Company means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Registration No. 201331905K). You, Your means the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/3-plus-critical-illness-policy-contract-v1-25.md#p10].

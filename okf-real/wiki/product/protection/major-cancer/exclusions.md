## Exclusions

There are certain conditions under which no Benefits will be payable [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-major-cancer-26-oct-2020-clean.md].

> 1. We do not pay the Benefits if the Major Cancer is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: - Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first 12 months from the Rider issue date, the last increase in rider's Sum insured made or the latest rider re-activation date, whichever is later; [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-major-cancer-26-oct-2020-clean.md]

- Effects of drug or alcohol addiction; - Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) infection, Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) and any AIDS related condition, unless the HIV infection is due to blood transfusion or occupationally acquired HIV; or - A Pre-existing condition [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-major-cancer-26-oct-2020-clean.md].

> 2. The Major Cancer is diagnosed within the first 90 days waiting period. Waiting period will start from the Rider issue date, the last increase in rider's Sum insured made or the latest rider re-activation date, whichever is later. 3. The Life insured did not survive for 30 days after the date of diagnosis of the Major Cancer. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-major-cancer-26-oct-2020-clean.md]

4. When there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our offer of conditional acceptance, the policy information page and Endorsement [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-major-cancer-26-oct-2020-clean.md].

When any of the exclusion happens, We will return the total Insurance cover charge paid for this rider without interest back into the Basic policy's Account value, less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-major-cancer-26-oct-2020-clean.md].

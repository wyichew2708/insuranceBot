## About Early CI Rider

## 2 Our Responsibilities

This is a Non-participating rider that accelerates the death Benefit of the Basic policy if the Life insured is diagnosed with any one of the covered critical illnesses at the early, intermediate or severe stage, as defined in Appendix A, while the rider is in force. This rider also provides additional coverage for special conditions as defined in Appendix B and monthly payout upon diagnosis of any covered severe-stage critical illnesses [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

This rider does not have any cash values. At the rider Expiry date, if We did not admit the claim for monthly payout Benefit, this rider will end and no Benefits will be payable. If We admit the claim for monthly payout Benefit, We will continue paying the monthly payout Benefit. The rider ends upon death of the Life insured or when all the monthly payouts have been paid, whichever is earlier [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

Your rider covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

This Policy Contract should be read together with the Policy Contract of the Basic policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 2.1 Covered Events

If Your policy is valid and a covered event happens, We will pay You the Benefits less any amounts You owe Us on this policy. The covered events of Your policy are written on the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 2.3 Guaranteed Convertibility

When You exercise the option to convert Your Basic policy, this rider will be converted automatically to a new rider available at the point of conversion, without giving Us proof of good health if the following conditions are met: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

> - a) this rider is in force when this option is exercised; - b) All the Premiums under this rider are paid up to-date; - c) the Life insured has not reached Age 66 when this option is exercised; [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

- d) the Life insured of the new rider must be the same as this rider; - e) the Life insured is insured for the same coverage amount or less on the new rider, subject to the minimum and maximum coverage amount of the new rider. If You choose a higher coverage amount for the new rider, any excess coverage amount will be subject to underwriting and any waiting period will re-start from the effective date of the conversion; [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

- f) The new rider covers the same insured events as this rider and also accelerates the death Benefit of the Basic policy; and - g) there are no previously admitted or submitted claims on this rider [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

If this rider is issued on non-standard terms, the same terms, conditions and exclusions shall continue to be applicable on the new rider. The conversion cannot be reverted once the new rider is effective [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 2.4 Option to Increase Sum Insured

This rider provides an option to increase the Life insured's protection without giving Us proof of good health whenever the Life insured experiences a key life event [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

- a) the Life insured changes marital status (for e.g. marries, divorces); - b) the Life insured becomes a parent by having a newborn child or legally adopts a child; - c) the Life insured purchases a property; - d) the Life insured or Life insured's child enrolls into tertiary education; [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

- e) the Life insured graduated from tertiary education [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

You can exercise this option for a maximum of 2 key life events per Life insured. You can only do so if the following conditions are met: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

> - a) Your rider is fully underwritten and issued on standard terms without any exclusions; - b) there are no previously admitted or submitted claims on this rider; - c) the key life event must occur before the Policy anniversary when the Life insured attains Age 50; [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

> - d) the application to exercise this option must be submitted within 90 calendar days following the occurrence of the key life event; - e) All the Premiums under this rider are paid up to-date; and - f) The total Sum insured that can be increased under this option regardless of the number of times it is exercised is the lower of either 50% of the rider's Sum insured at rider commencement date or S$200,000, per Life insured. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

> If You have more than one policy with Us which offers similar option, You can exercise this option for a maximum of 2 key life events per Life insured and the maximum aggregate coverage that can be increased under this option per Life insured is S$200,000. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

The increased Sum insured will be effective on the next Policy Anniversary immediately after the approval of Your application and the Premium payable for the increased Sum insured for the remaining term of the rider will be calculated based on the Life insured's Age at the next Policy Anniversary [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 4 What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefits described below while the policy is in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 4.1 Critical Illness (CI) Benefit

This rider pays the CI Benefit as an advancement of the death Benefit of the Basic policy if the Life insured is diagnosed with a covered CI at the early, intermediate or severe stage while the rider is in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

If the Life insured is diagnosed with an early or intermediate stage of a covered CI, We will pay the CI Benefit subject to the maximum aggregate amount and waive all future Premiums for this rider starting from the next Premium due date immediately after the date of diagnosis [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

> We will only provide a maximum aggregate amount of S$2,000,000 per Life insured for early, intermediate or severe stages of CI, subject to a cap of S$350,000 per Life insured for early or intermediate stages of CI, for all policies and riders issued by Us with CI Benefits. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

Upon payment of the CI Benefit for an early or intermediate stage of a covered CI, this rider will continue with the remaining CI Benefit (if any) to be payable upon the diagnosis of a severe-stage covered CI [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

If the CI Benefit payable is lesser than the death Benefit of the Basic policy, the death Benefit of the Basic policy will be reduced by the amount paid for the CI. The policy remains in force and the remaining death Benefit is the original death Benefit less the amount paid for CI [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

If the CI Benefit payable is the same as the death Benefit of the Basic policy, both the Basic policy and the CI Benefit of this rider ends when We make this payment [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

If the death Benefit of the Basic policy is reduced due to a claim for Terminal illness Benefit under the Basic policy or Total and permanent disability Benefit under any other riders, We will reduce the CI Benefit of this rider proportionally. The Premium of the rider will be reduced accordingly and You must continue to pay the revised Premiums to keep this rider in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

Please refer to Appendix A for the list of covered CI and their definitions [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 4.2 Monthly Payout Benefit

> If the Life insured is diagnosed with a covered severe-stage CI while the rider is in force, We will pay You twelve (12) monthly payouts, starting from the Policy month immediately after the date of diagnosis. The monthly payout is equivalent to 1% of the rider's Sum insured. Before Our payment, any amounts owing to Us will first be deducted. This Benefit ends when either one of these events happens first: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

- a) Death of the Life insured; or - b) All the monthly payouts have been paid [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

Our payment on the monthly payouts will not reduce the death Benefit of the Basic policy and it shall be paid to You as an additional Benefit to any CI Benefit payable by Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 4.3 Special Condition Benefit

> If the Life insured is diagnosed with a covered special condition while the rider is in force, We will pay an additional 20% of the rider's Sum insured in one lump sum. Before Our payment, any amounts owing to Us will first be deducted. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

> We will only provide a maximum aggregate amount of S$25,000 per Life insured per special condition. Each special condition can only be paid once and a maximum of six (6) claims can be made under this Benefit, regardless of the number of times this rider is being renewed. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md]

Our payment on the special condition will not reduce the death Benefit of the Basic policy and it shall be paid to You as an additional Benefit to any CI Benefit payable by Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

Please refer to Appendix B for the definitions of the covered special conditions [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

## 5.1 Critical Illness (CI) Benefit

We will not pay any Benefit if the CI (early, intermediate or severe stages) or special condition is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

- A Pre-existing condition; - Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; - Effects of drug or alcohol addiction; or - Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-early-ci-rider-v1-23.md].

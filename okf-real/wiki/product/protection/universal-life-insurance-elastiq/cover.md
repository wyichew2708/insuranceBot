## 3. Our Responsibilities

> This is a single premium, Non-participating universal life plan denominated in Singapore dollars. It matures on the policy anniversary immediately before the Life insured attains 100 years old. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

Your policy covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

## 3.1 Covered Events

If your policy is valid and a covered event happens, we will pay you the Benefits less any amounts you owe us on this policy. The covered events of your policy are written on the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

## 3.2 Account Value

- a) any recurring Top-up(s); - b) any ad-hoc Top-up(s); and - c) accumulated interest; [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Any interest will be credited into the Account value at the end of each Policy month [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

## 3.3 Crediting Rate

> For the initial single premium, the crediting rate for the first 3 years from the Policy commencement date is guaranteed and fixed at the crediting rate determined by us on the Policy commencement date. You may refer to the Policy Illustration for the guaranteed crediting rates for the first 3 years from the Policy issue date. For subsequent years, the crediting rate will be determined by us based on the prevailing rate, subject to the minimum guaranteed crediting rate of 0% p.a. which ensures that your capital is fully guaranteed each year. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

For any Top-up(s) made, the crediting rate will be determined by us based on prevailing market conditions. We reserve the right to revise the crediting rate for Top-up(s) from time to time [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Interest is calculated based on the daily Account value and credited into the Account value at the end of each Policy month [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Any revision in crediting rates will take effect on the 1 st business day of each calendar month but any interest earned will only be credited to the Account value at the end of each Policy month [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

For reinstatement, we reserve the right to revise the crediting rate for the Account value [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

## 3.4 Top-up(s)

You can make a recurring or ad-hoc Top-up to your policy immediately after the Policy issue date, subject to our approval. We reserve the right to limit the number of Top-up(s) to the policy [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

For recurring Top-up(s), only monthly Top-up frequency is allowed [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

The Top-up(s) made is subject to the following: [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

> -  The minimum amount for each Top-up must be at least S$500; -  Top-up(s) amount must be in multiples of S$500; and -  The maximum aggregate amount for all Top-up(s) per policy is S$200,000 less the single premium paid to us. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

The Account value will increase by the amount of Top-up(s) made to the policy [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

For any Top-up(s), interest will be calculated from the date Your Top-up payment is successfully deducted from Your bank account and credited into Your Account value [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

## 3.5 Loyalty Bonus

> A non-guaranteed loyalty bonus, equivalent to 0.3% of the average monthly Account value for the past 36 Policy months, will be credited into the Account value at the end of the 3 rd Policy year and at every subsequent 3 Policy year interval (6 th , 9 th , 12 th , 15 th , 18 th Policy year and so on), as long as your policy is valid and no partial withdrawal has been made before. We reserve the right to revise the bonus rate at any time by giving you 30 days' notice. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

## 3.6 Surrender Benefit

You may surrender your policy in full or partially through partial withdrawal [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Upon full surrender, We will pay the surrender benefit in one lump sum which is equivalent to the Account value, less any amounts owing to us. You may request for a full surrender any time after the free look period [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

> We reserve the right to delay the payment of the surrender value for up to a period of 6 months from the date of your surrender application. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

## 3.7 Partial Withdrawal

> You may request for partial withdrawal(s) 90 calendar days after the Policy issue date, subject to the following: [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

> -  The withdrawal amount must be at least S$500 (or its multiples) per withdrawal; and -  After withdrawal, the average daily Account value for the Policy month must be at least S$5,000 for a waiver of the service fee. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

> If the average daily Account value for the Policy month falls below S$5,000, a service fee of S$5 will be charged onto the Account value at the end of each Policy month. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

Partial withdrawal will reduce the Account value by the withdrawal amount [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

> We reserve the right to delay the payment of the partial withdrawal amount for up to a period of 6 months from the date of your withdrawal application. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

## 3.8 Maturity Benefit

At maturity date, if the policy is still in force, the maturity benefit payable is the Account value less any amounts owing to us [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

## 3.9 Update on Policy Status

An annual policy statement will be sent to you. This document aims to keep you informed of the status of your policy such as the Account value, total Top-up(s) and total partial withdrawal(s) [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

## 3.10 Change of Life Insured

Where the Policy owner is an entity, Policy owner has the option to request for a change of Life insured under this policy after the first Policy year, subject to the following conditions: [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

-  Acceptance of the new Life insured is subject to our approval, depending on the insurability of the new Life insured and such other terms and conditions as we shall determine from time to time. The new Life insured must be a Key personnel of the entity; [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

> -  All exclusions and conditions will be applicable to the new Life insured. The exclusions and conditions to this policy will begin from the effective date of the change of Life insured; and -  The policy term will remain unchanged. The policy will mature on the policy anniversary immediately before the original Life insured attains 100 years old. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

There are no restrictions on the number of changes that can be made [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

## 4.4 Changes in Information Provided

For any changes in the information (including any changes in the residency, citizenship or tax status) provided to us when you are applying for this policy, we must be informed in writing immediately. We reserve the right to make changes to the policy based on the new information provided to us [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

## 5. What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefit described below while the policy is in force [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured while the policy is in force, we will pay 106.8% of Account value, less any amounts owing to us. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

When we make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

## Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured while the policy is in force, we will pay 106.8% of Account value, less any amounts owing to us. When we make this payment, the policy ends. [src:raw/product-summaries/elastiq-product-summary-7-nov.md]

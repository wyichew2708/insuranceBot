## 3.4 Premium-Free Period

While the Policy is in force, You are entitled to a Premium-Free Period throughout the premium payment term. The Premium-Free Period will be activated automatically when regular Premiums are missed or when You request to activate it. The applicable Premium-Free Period is determined based on the prevailing Policy year and Your selected premium payment term [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

'Premium-Free Period' refers to the number of months in which Premium shortfall charge will not be applicable if You missed paying any regular Premium [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

Please refer to the table below for the applicable Premium-Free Period [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

You are allowed to accumulate any unused Premium-Free Period within the premium payment term. Once You have fully utilised the applicable Premium-Free Period, a premium shortfall charge will apply on the regular Premium that You have missed [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

Please note that utilising the premium-free period may result in lower projected account values and potentially deviate from your financial goals. You are encouraged to pay the full regular Premium term and only utilise the premium-free period during times of financial emergencies [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

Top-up(s) is/are not allowed when You are on Premium-Free Period and recurring Top-up(s) (if any) will stop [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

## 3.7.1 Change in Regular Premium

An increase in regular Premium is not allowed [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

> While the Policy is in force, You may request for a decrease in regular Premium after 3 years of Premiums have been paid to Us i.e. 36 monthly, 12 quarterly, 6 half-yearly or 3 annual Premiums, subject to the following: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md]

> - the minimum regular Premium requirement set by Us; and - the decrease in regular Premium must not be more than 25% of the original regular Premium amount chosen at the Policy issue date. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md]

Start-up Bonus recovery charge will apply when You decrease Your Policy's regular Premium. Please refer to the Fees and Charges section for the Start-up Bonus recovery charge [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

The change in regular Premium shall take effect from the next premium due date [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

## 3.7.2 Top-up (Ad-hoc / Recurring)

While the Policy is in force, You may request to make a recurring or ad-hoc Top-up(s) to Your Policy immediately after the Policy issue date, subject to Our approval [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

> 100% of each recurring Top-up Premium and ad-hoc Top-up Premium less premium charge will be credited to Your Policy in the form of units to the Top-up Account value in accordance to the investment allocation chosen by You. The units will be purchased based on the unit price on the next Valuation day. Please refer to the Fees and Charges section for the premium charge. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md]

Where the Top-up is paid prior to the date the Top-up application is accepted by the Company, it will only be allocated and apportioned to create units in each fund following the Company's acceptance [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

## 3.7.4 Full Surrender

You may request to surrender Your Policy for its account value at any time after the free look period [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

A surrender charge will be imposed on the Regular Premium Account value when you surrender the Policy during the premium payment term. Please refer to the Fees and Charges section for the full surrender charge. There is no surrender charge on the Top-up Account [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

The surrender value consists of the following: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

- a) Account value(s) less surrender charge (if any); and - b) any Premium and/or Top-up(s) Premium you have paid but have yet to be invested in any ILP SubFund, - c) less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

We will pay You an amount equivalent to the value of the cancelled units at the unit prices of the respective ILP Sub-Fund(s) on the next Valuation day after We receive your request, less any applicable charges [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

Your coverage will be terminated once You have surrendered Your Policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

## 3.7.5 Premium Redirection

You may request to re-direct Your future regular Premium into other ILP Sub-Fund(s) of Your choice without affecting the units in Your existing selected ILP Sub-Fund(s) [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

> Premiums can be redirected to a maximum of ten (10) ILP Sub-Funds. The premium redirection is subject to a minimum allocation of 10% per selected fund, or a minimum investment amount for certain ILP SubFund(s). [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md]

Note that for Your Policy, only Shariah-compliant ILP Sub-Funds will be made available for subscription (including premium redirection and/or top-up(s)), redemption and/or fund switching [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

## 3.8.4 Determination of Unit Prices and Pricing Basis

The Company will value each ILP Sub-Fund on every Dealing Day to determine the unit price of a unit on a forward-pricing basis. The pricing of each fund is done on a single pricing basis [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

The Company will determine the unit price of each ILP Sub-Fund based on the Net Asset Value of the ILP Sub-Fund determined pursuant to clause 3.8.5. The result will be rounded down to the nearest five (5) decimal places in Singapore dollars or other currency in which the ILP Sub-Fund is denominated [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

The unit price of each ILP Sub-Fund can be obtained from the Company's website www.etiqa.com.sg. The Company may from time to time change the address of its website without notice. The Company may also make available the unit price in such other manner as it may determine in its absolute discretion from time to time [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

## 3.9 Premium Allocation to the ILP Sub-Funds

> Regular Premium Account: 100% of the regular Premium paid will be invested into Your selected ILP SubFunds. The Premium shall be invested according to the allocations indicated for Your ILP Sub-Funds. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md]

> Top-up Account: 100% of each recurring Top-up Premium and/or ad-hoc Top-up Premium paid less premium charge will be invested into Your selected ILP Sub-Funds. The Premium shall be invested according to the allocations indicated for Your ILP Sub-Funds. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md]

- Premium-Free Period will be activated automatically if regular Premium due under the Regular Premium Account are unpaid after the grace period or upon Your request to activate it. - the Policy (including attaching optional unit-deducting riders) will continue to be in force as long as the Regular Premium Account value is sufficient to cover the applicable fees and charges due [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

- optional premium-paying riders (if attached) will lapse. - Premium shortfall charge will not apply as long as within the Premium-Free Period. - Top-up(s) is/are not allowed and recurring Top-up(s) if any, will stop. - when the Regular Premium Account value is insufficient to pay for the applicable fees and charges due, the Policy (including attaching optional unit-deducting riders) will lapse [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

- Premium-Free Period will stop when You resume paying your regular Premium [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-vista-v4-0.md].

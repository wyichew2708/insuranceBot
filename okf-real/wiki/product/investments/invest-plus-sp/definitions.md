## 12 What Do We Mean With These Words?

Account value is the value of all the units in Your policy less any applicable fees and charges [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Age means the Age at next birthday [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Application means the Proposal form, any amendments, forms and documents provided by You and the Life insured [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Assignee means any person to whom You have transferred Your rights as the Policy o wner and the assignment is registered with Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Benefit(s) means any payments that We will pay and/or the amount of Premium that We will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Doctor means a licensed person who is qualified by degree in western medicine to practice medicine. The license is given by the appropriate medical authority of his country of residence to practice medicine within his scope of licensing and training. This cannot be You, the Life insured, a family member or a relative [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Fund Factsheet refers to a product document that show the performance of the Portfolio fund / ILP subfund [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Fund Summary refers to a product document that contains the list of ILP sub-fund and Portfolio funds as well as their ILP sub-funds and allocations [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

ILP refers to Investment-linked insurance plan [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

ILP sub-fund manager refers to the asset management company that manages the respective ILP subfunds [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Life insured means the person whom We provide the cover for. The Life insured does not have any right to the policy, unless he/she is also the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Maturity date is the date the policy ends and where a maturity Benefit is payable [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

> Nominee(s) is a person that You have nominated (under the Insurance Act 1966, and Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009 ) to receive the policy monies payable under the policy upon Your death. The nomination must be registered with Us. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md]

Policy commencement date means the date the policy commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Policy issue date means the date We issue the policy. This is shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Policy Information Page or any Endorsement issued by Us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the policy has been transferred to another party [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Policy year / Policy anniversary is the 1-year period that starts on the Policy commencement date or any subsequent anniversary of the Policy commencement date [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Portfolio fund refers to the list of ILP sub-funds in the policy in which the Premiums are allocated [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Portfolio fund manager refers to Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. who manages the Portfolio funds [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Premium(s) is an amount that You pay to Us to keep this policy in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Pre-existing condition means the existence of any signs or symptoms before the Policy commencement date or Reinstatement date for which treatment, medication, consultation, advice, or diagnosis has been sought or received by the Life insured or would have caused any reasonable and sensible person to get medical advice or treatment [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

- a) subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict Us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - b) who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act 1966 . It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own Benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md]

Proposal form means the form You signed to buy this policy from Us. It includes anything written, said (and recorded) and/or any document given to Us which has information for Us to rely on before We decide to issue this policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Reinstatement date is the date We reinstate Your policy back to inforce [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Top-up(s) means the subsequent additional premium(s) You make to Your policy after Your policy is issued [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

Valuation day means a business day when We carry out a valuation to determine the price of a unit of the ILP sub-funds under this policy respectively [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

We, Our, Us, the Company means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Co. Reg. No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

You, Your means the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-invest-plus-sp-v2-25.md].

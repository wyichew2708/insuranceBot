## The following are excluded

• Non-organic diseases such as neurosis and psychiatric illnesses; and • Alcohol related brain damage. 2 Idiopathic The unequivocal diagnosis of Idiopathic Parkinson’s Disease by a Parkinson’s consultant neurologist. This diagnosis must be supported by all of the Disease* following conditions: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-retirement.md#p6].

• The disease cannot be controlled with medication; and • Inability of the Life insured to perform (whether aided or unaided) at least 3 of the 6 “Activities of Daily Living” (ADLs) for a continuous period of at least six (6) months [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-retirement.md#p6].

For the purpose of this definition, “aided” shall mean with the aid of special equipment, device and/or apparatus and not pertaining to human aid. 3 Osteoporosis with Osteoporosis is a degenerative bone disease that results in loss of bone. Fractures The diagnosis must be supported by a bone density reading which satisfies the World Health Organisation (WHO) definition of osteoporosis with a bone density reading T-score of less than –2.5. There must also be a history of three (3) or more osteoporotic fractures involving either femur, wrist or vertebrae. These fractures must directly cause the Life insured's inability to perform (whether aided or unaided) at least 1 of the 6 ADLs for a continuous period of at least six (6) months [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-retirement.md#p6].

> For the purpose of this definition, “aided” shall mean with the aid of special equipment, device and/or apparatus and not pertaining to human aid. *The Life Insurance Association Singapore (LIA) has standard Definitions for 37 severe-stage Critical Illnesses (Version 2024). These Critical Illnesses fall under Version 2024. You may refer to www.lia.org.sg for the standard Definitions (Version 2024). [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-retirement.md#p6]

## 11. What Do We Mean With These Words?

Accidental / Accident means a sudden, unforeseen and unplanned event causing bodily injury that is violent, external and visible in nature. It is not caused by sickness, disease, gradual physical, mental changes or any other causes. The Accident must occur after the Policy issue date [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md#p12].

> Advance Premium refers to the second year's Premium that You have paid in advance at the time of application. The Advance Premium facility is not a bank savings account nor a bank deposit. Advance Premium will be refunded without any interest when You surrender Your policy, make a claim on the policy for the Death Benefit, TI Benefit, Accidental Death Benefit or TPD Premium Waiver Benefit or when the policy ends as a result of Termination clause 10 (e), (f) or (g), in the first policy year. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md#p12]

Age means the age at next birthday. Assignee means any person to whom You have transferred Your rights as the Policy Owner and the assignment is registered with Us. Basic policy means the policy as it exists, including the supplementary terms and any Endorsement made to it, without any optional supplementary contract / rider [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md#p12].

Benefit(s) means any payments that We will pay and/or the amount of Premium that We will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur. Doctor means a licensed person who is qualified by degree in western medicine to practice medicine [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md#p12].

The license is given by the appropriate medical authority of his country of residence to practice medicine within his scope of licensing and training. This cannot be You, the Life insured, a family member or a relative. Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by Us [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md#p12].

Face value is the notional value used to determine the Non-Guaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit and the Yearly Cash Benefit. It is not the sum insured of Your policy [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md#p12].

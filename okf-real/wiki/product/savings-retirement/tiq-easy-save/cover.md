## 3. Our Responsibilities

> This is a Regular premium, Non-participating universal life plan denominated in Singapore dollars. It matures on the Policy anniversary immediately before the Life insured attains 100 years old. The plan has a premium payment term of 2 years. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md]

Your policy covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

## 3.1 Covered Events

If Your policy is valid and a covered event happens, We will pay You the Benefits less any amounts You owe Us on this policy. The covered events of Your policy are written on the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

## 3.2 Account Value

- a) premium charge on the first year Regular premium; and - b) partial withdrawal amount and partial withdrawal charges (if any) [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

Any interest will be credited into the Account value at the end of each Policy month [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

## 3.3 Crediting Rate

> For the Account value, the crediting rate for the first 6 years from the Policy commencement date is guaranteed and fixed at the crediting rate determined by us on the Policy issue date. This crediting rate will not be lower than 2.00% p.a. You may refer to the Policy Illustration for the guaranteed crediting rates for the first 6 years from the Policy commencement date. For subsequent years, the crediting rate will be determined by us based on the prevailing rate, subject to the minimum guaranteed crediting rate of 0% [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md]

Interest is calculated based on the daily Account value and credited into the Account value at the end of each Policy month [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

> We reserve the right to revise the crediting rates at any time by giving You 30 days written notice, but any interest earned will only be credited to the Account value at the end of each Policy month. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md]

For reinstatement, We reserve the right to revise the crediting rate for the Account value [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

## 3.4 Surrender Benefit

You may surrender Your policy in full or partially through partial withdrawal [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

Upon full surrender, We will pay the surrender Benefit in one lump sum which is equivalent to the Account value less surrender charges (if any) and any amounts owing to Us. You may request for a full surrender any time after the free look period [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

> We reserve the right to delay the payment of the surrender Benefit for up to a period of 6 months from the date of the surrender request. We will exercise this right when there is a surge in withdrawals within the Portfolio during a very short period of time. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md]

We will also monitor Your Account value and exercise Market Value Adjustment (MVA) at Our discretion to protect the interest of all policyholders. This is to ensure those who remain in Our Portfolio will not be placed at a disadvantage when others leave [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

## 3.5 Partial Withdrawal

> You may request for partial withdrawal(s) after the Policy issue date, subject to the withdrawal amount must be at least S$500 (or its multiples) per withdrawal. Partial withdrawal will reduce the Account value by the withdrawal amount and partial withdrawal charges (if any). [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md]

> We reserve the right to delay the payment of the withdrawal amount for up to a period of 6 months from the date of the withdrawal request. We will exercise this right when there is a surge in withdrawals within the Portfolio during a very short period of time. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md]

We will also monitor Your Account value and exercise MVA at Our discretion to protect the interest of all policyholders. This is to ensure those who remain in Our Portfolio will not be placed at a disadvantage when others leave [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

## 3.6 Free Partial Withdrawal Benefit

> During the first 6 years from the Policy issue date, You may request for partial withdrawal(s) without paying partial withdrawal charge upon the following events: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md]

If You or Your spouse is certified by a Doctor to: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

- a) be physically or mentally incapacitated from ever continuing in any employment; - b) have a severely impaired life expectancy; - c) lack capacity within the meaning of Section 4 of the Mental Capacity Act (MCA) and the lack of capacity is likely to be permanent; or - d) be diagnosed with Terminal illness [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

We may appoint a Doctor to re-examine You or Your spouse on the certified medical condition [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

You can exercise this Benefit subject to the following conditions: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

> - a) Any of the above-specified events has to occur after the Policy issue date or latest Reinstatement date; - b) The maximum partial withdrawal amount is the lower of S$50,000 or 50% of the total Regular premiums paid (not including Premiums paid in advance); and - c) This benefit can only be exercised once throughout the policy term. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md]

## 3.7 Maturity Benefit

At maturity date, if the policy is still in force, the maturity Benefit payable is the Account value less any amounts owing to us [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

## 3.8 Update on Policy Status

Your policy status is available to view on Our TiqConnect website [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

An annual policy statement will be sent to You. This document aims to keep You informed of the status of Your policy such as the Account value, total partial withdrawal(s) and the charges deducted [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

## 5. What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefit described below while the policy is in force [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

## 1.1 Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured while the policy is in force, We will pay 101% of the Account value, less any amounts owing to Us. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md]

When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/tiq-easy-save-general-provisions.md].

## Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured while the policy is in force, We will pay 101% of Account value, less any amounts owing to Us. When We make this payment, the policy ends. [src:raw/product-summaries/tiq-easy-save-product-summary.md]

## Surrender Benefit

Market Value Adjustment (MVA) means an adjustment to Your surrender or partial withdrawal amount which is to reflect the market value fluctuations of the assets supporting Your policy. The adjustment, expressed as a percentage of the surrender or partial withdrawal amount, is decided at Our discretion [src:raw/product-summaries/tiq-easy-save-product-summary.md].

## 3.1 Surrender Benefit

A guaranteed Surrender value, less any outstanding amount owing to Us, will be payable upon surrender [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p2].

## 3.2 Maturity Benefit

On the Maturity date, if the policy is still in force, the Life insured survives and We have not paid out any claims, We will pay You a guaranteed Maturity Benefit in one lump sum, less any outstanding amount owing to Us. Please refer to the Policy Illustration for the value of the Maturity Benefit [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p2].

## 5. What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefits described below while the policy is inforce [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p3].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured while the policy is in force, We will pay 101% of the single Premium You paid on this policy, less any outstanding amount owing to Us. When We make this payment, the policy ends. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p3]

## There are certain conditions under which no Benefits will be payable

> a) Life insured commits intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date. When this happens, We will refund the Premiums paid without interest, less any amounts owing to Us from this policy. [src:raw/product-summaries/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-product-summary-tiq.md#p2]

> b) death due to Pre-existing conditions within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date. When this happens, We will refund the Premiums paid without interest, less any amounts owing to Us from this policy. [src:raw/product-summaries/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-product-summary-tiq.md#p2]

The above are stated as exclusions in the Policy Contract. You are advised to read Your Policy Contract for the full details of these exclusions [src:raw/product-summaries/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-product-summary-tiq.md#p2].

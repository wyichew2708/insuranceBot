## 3. Our Responsibilities

> This is a single Premium, Non-participating endowment plan. This plan has a policy term of 3 years and it pays 101% of the single Premium You paid on this policy upon the death of the Life insured during the policy term. A lump sum guaranteed Maturity Benefit will be paid at the end of the policy term if the Life insured survives and We have not paid out any claims. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p2]

Your policy covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p2].

## 3.1 Surrender Benefit

A guaranteed Surrender value, less any outstanding amount owing to Us, will be payable upon surrender [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p2].

## 3.2 Maturity Benefit

On the Maturity date, if the policy is still in force, the Life insured survives and We have not paid out any claims, We will pay You a guaranteed Maturity Benefit in one lump sum, less any outstanding amount owing to Us. Please refer to the Policy Illustration for the value of the Maturity Benefit [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p2].

## 4.2 Changes in Information Provided

For any changes in the information (including any changes in the residency, citizenship or tax status) provided to Us when You are applying for this policy, We must be informed in writing immediately. We reserve the right to make changes to the policy based on the new information provided to Us [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p2].

## 5. What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefits described below while the policy is inforce [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p2].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured while the policy is in force, We will pay 101% of the single Premium You paid on this policy, less any outstanding amount owing to Us. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p2]

When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-policy-contract-tiq.md#p2].

## Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured while the policy is in force, We will pay 101% of the single Premium You paid on this policy, less any outstanding amount owing to Us. When We make this payment, the policy ends. [src:raw/product-summaries/tiq-3-year-endowment-plan-product-summary-tiq.md]

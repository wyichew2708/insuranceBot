## General Definitions

Accident/Accidental means an unforeseen event of violent, accidental, external and visible nature, occurring during the Period of Insurance, which results in Bodily Injury or death [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

Act of Terrorism means an act, including but not limited to the use of force or violence and/or the threat thereof, of any person or group(s) of persons, whether acting alone or on behalf of or in connection with any organization(s) or government(s), committed for political, religious, ideological or similar purposes including the intention to influence any government and/or to put the public, or any section of the public, in fear [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

AWP Services Singapore Pte. Ltd. is a third party provider contracted by Us to provide Emergency Home Assistance service and Multi-appliances Home Protector service to our ePROTECT home pro policyholders [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

Building shall mean the private dwelling or residential flat and all domestic outbuildings, fixtures and fittings attached to the building, swimming pool, underground services, walls, gates and fences and all other domestic improvements of a structural nature at the Premise and for which You are legally responsible. All buildings are declared by You to be built of bricks, stone or concrete and roofed with concrete, slate, metal, asbestos or a composite of asbestos and other non-combustible mineral ingredient unless specially mentioned [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

> Child means any of Your unmarried dependent children aged between 1 and below 18 years or up to 25 years old if he or she is pursuing full-time education in a recognized tertiary institution and who is a member of the Household. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2]

Domestic Helper refers to a domestic maid employed by You and staying at the address shown in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

Effective Date of Insurance means the commencement date of insurance as specified on the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

Family means Your spouse and children, and Your relatives permanently living with you at the address stated in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

Fungi means any form of fungus including but not limited to all forms of mould or mildew and any mycotoxins, spores, scents, vapours, gases or substances including any by-products produced or released by it [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

Home / Dwelling means a building occupied as private dwelling (house, flat or apartment) together with its garages and outbuildings, all used for domestic purposes at the address shown in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

Household means all members of Your family and any other persons (other than paying guests or tenants) living with You permanently [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

Household Contents shall mean any moveable household item belonging to You or any member of Your Household except for the following: [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

a) Property more specifically insured under another policy. b) Motor vehicles and accessories, pedal cycles and watercraft. c) Money, deeds, bonds, bills of exchange, promissory notes, cheques, traveller’s cheques, securities for money, stamps, certificates or documents of any kind, manuscripts, medals, contact lenses, hearing aids and livestock unless specially mentioned herein [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

d) Any part of the structure or ceilings of the building(s), wallpapers and the like or external television and radio [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

antennae, aerials, aerial fittings, masts and towers [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

e) Any property the value of which is included in the Total Sum Insured on Renovation, fixtures and fittings or [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

f) Landlord’s fixtures and fittings. g) Property owned or held in trust in connection with any business profession or trade. h) Livestock [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

Injury means bodily injury caused solely and directly by an accident [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md#p2].

## General Definitions

Accident/Accidental means an unforeseen event of violent, accidental, external and visible nature, occurring during the Period of Insurance, which results in Bodily Injury or death [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Act of Terrorism means an act, including but not limited to the use of force or violence and/or the threat thereof, of any person or group(s) of persons, whether acting alone or on behalf of or in connection with any organization(s) or government(s), committed for political, religious, ideological or similar purposes including the intention to influence any government and/or to put the public, or any section of the public, in fear [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

AWP Services Singapore Pte. Ltd. is a third party provider contracted by Us to provide Emergency Home Assistance service and Multi-appliances Home Protector service to our ePROTECT home pro policyholders [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Building shall mean the private dwelling or residential flat and all domestic outbuildings, fixtures and fittings attached to the building, swimming pool, underground services, walls, gates and fences and all other domestic improvements of a structural nature at the Premise and for which You are legally responsible. All buildings are declared by You to be built of bricks, stone or concrete and roofed with concrete, slate, metal, asbestos or a composite of asbestos and other non-combustible mineral ingredient unless specially mentioned [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

> Child means any of Your unmarried dependent children aged between 1 and below 18 years or up to 25 years old if he or she is pursuing full-time education in a recognized tertiary institution and who is a member of the Household. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md]

Domestic Helper refers to a domestic maid employed by You and staying at the address shown in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Effective Date of Insurance means the commencement date of insurance as specified on the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Family means Your spouse and children, and Your relatives permanently living with you at the address stated in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Fungi means any form of fungus including but not limited to all forms of mould or mildew and any mycotoxins, spores, scents, vapours, gases or substances including any by-products produced or released by it [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Home / Dwelling means a building occupied as private dwelling (house, flat or apartment) together with its garages and outbuildings, all used for domestic purposes at the address shown in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Household means all members of Your family and any other persons (other than paying guests or tenants) living with You permanently [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Household Contents shall mean any moveable household item belonging to You or any member of Your Household except for the following: [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

- a) Property more specifically insured under another policy. - b) Motor vehicles and accessories, pedal cycles and watercraft. - c) Money, deeds, bonds, bills of exchange, promissory notes, cheques, traveller's cheques, securities for money, stamps, certificates or documents of any kind, manuscripts, medals, contact lenses, hearing aids and livestock unless specially mentioned herein [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

- d) Any part of the structure or ceilings of the building(s), wallpapers and the like or external television and radio antennae, aerials, aerial fittings, masts and towers. - e) Any property the value of which is included in the Total Sum Insured on Renovation, fixtures and fittings or Building(s) [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

- f) Landlord's fixtures and fittings. - g) Property owned or held in trust in connection with any business profession or trade. - h) Livestock [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Injury means bodily injury caused solely and directly by an accident [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Insured Perils refer to the following: [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

- a) Fire, Lightning, Thunderbolt, Subterranean Fire. - b) Domestic Explosion. - c) Aircraft and other aerial devices and/or articles dropped therefrom. - d) Impact by any road vehicle not belonging to nor under the control of the You or any member of the Household [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

> - e) Bursting or overflowing of domestic water tanks, apparatus or pipes from within the property insured or containing property insured but excluding damage thereto and loss or damage occurring whilst Your Home is left unoccupied for more than sixty (60) days. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md]

> - f) Theft accompanied by actual violent and forcible entry or any attempt to break in excluding loss or damage occurring whilst Your Home is left unoccupied for more than sixty (60) consecutive days. - g) Hurricane, Cyclone, Typhoon and Windstorm including flood or overflow of the sea occasioned thereby. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md]

> - h) Earthquake or Volcanic Eruption including flood or overflow of the sea occasioned thereby. - i) Subsidence or Landslip caused by Flood only but excluding the first $10,000 or 10% of the claim cost whichever is higher for each and every loss. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md]

> - j) Riot, Strike, Civil Commotion not amounting to a popular uprising, strike or labour disturbance. - k) Malicious persons and vandals except for loss or damage occurring while Your Home has been unoccupied for more than sixty (60) days whether consecutively or not in any one Period of Insurance and/or loss or damage caused by the willful or dishonest act of You and/or Your Household or with the connivance of You and/or Your Household. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md]

- l) Falling trees or branches but not loss or damage caused by falling or looping of trees by You or on Your behalf. - m) Smoke damage to the property insured by fire directly caused by smoke due to a sudden, unusual and faulty operation of any heating or cooking unit while in Your Home excluding damage thereto [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

- n) Accidental Damage to all electrical and electronic appliances [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Money means cash, coins, bank notes, currency notes, promissory notes, cheques, traveler cheques, money orders, postal orders, deeds, bonds, crossed banker's draft belonging to You or for which You have accepted responsibility and held for personal purposes [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Period of Insurance means the period during which the coverage under this Policy is effective, as stated in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Renovation means improvements and additions within Your Home made by You as owner or by any former owner of Your Home in the form of fixtures and fittings including flooring, built-in wardrobes and air-conditioners, but does not include any part of the building [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Schedule means documents with details of the Insured, type of cover and Period of Insurance which forms part of the policy [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Sum Insured refers to the maximum amount which You are insured as shown in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

Valuables shall mean jewellery, watches, curios, works of art, furs, antiques, stamps or coins collections and other collectible items, manuscripts, medals, items of gold, silver or other precious stones, platinum, paintings, fine glassware and crystal, tapestries, antiques and other collectible property that are kept in Your Home [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

We, Our and Us refer to Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd., (Company Reg. No. 201331905K) the Company providing this insurance [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

You/Your/Insured means the Insured named in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-policy-wording-eprotect-home-20sep2017-onwards.md].

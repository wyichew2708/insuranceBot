## About Eprotect Safety Insurance

## Section 1 - Accidental Death

> We shall pay the Benefit Amount in accordance to the schedule of benefits stated under Your plan if during the Period of Insurance, You sustain Bodily Injury which results in Accidental Death within fiftytwo (52) weeks from the date of Accident. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md]

The Accidental Death Benefit will be doubled if You are travelling in a public conveyance outside Malaysia, Singapore or Brunei at the time of Accidental Death or Injury [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

> We will not pay for the benefit under Section 1 if We have already done so under Section 10. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md]

## Section 2 - Permanent Disablement due to Accident

> If during the Period of Insurance, You sustain Bodily Injury which results in Permanent Disablement within fifty-two (52) weeks from the date of Accident, We shall pay You up to the maximum limits stated under Your plan as specified under Section 2 of the table of benefits using the scale of compensation table as shown below. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md]

## Section 4 - Hospitalisation Allowance

> We will pay compensation in accordance to the schedule of benefits stated under Your plan for each completed twenty-four (24)-hour period that You are confined in a Hospital as a result of Bodily Injury due to any one (1) Accident or as a result of contracting an Infectious Disease up to a maximum of one hundred and eighty (180) days subject to medical report and original receipt being provided. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md]

The limit stated in Section 4 is applicable to any one (1) Accident or any one (1) Infectious Disease [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

## Section 6 - Bereavement Grant

We will pay a lump sum payment in accordance to the schedule of benefits stated under Your plan in the event of Accidental Death or if You contract an Infectious Disease which directly results in death [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

## Section 7 - Mobility Expense Reimbursement

> If You sustain Bodily Injury or contract an Infectious Disease during the Period of Insurance and within twelve (12) months from the date of Accident or diagnosis of the Infectious Disease, which directly and independent of all other causes result in Permanent Disablement and You need and can operate: [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md]

- a) a self-powered, climbing wheelchair; and/or - b) a motor vehicle with the controls suitably adjusted, [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

We will reimburse the actual cost incurred up to the limits specified in the schedule of benefits stated under Section 7 of Your plan for any one (1) Accident or Infectious Disease [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

## Section 8 - Ambulance Fees

We will reimburse the actual cost incurred for the use of ambulance services in accordance to the schedule of benefits stated under Section 8 of Your plan for any one (1) Accident or Infectious Disease, subject to original receipt being provided [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

## Section 9 - Home or Office Modification Reimbursement

If You sustain Bodily Injury or contract an Infectious Disease during the Period of Insurance, which directly and independent of all other causes result in Permanent Disablement and You need a lift, necessary ramps, railings and holds installed in Your ordinary place of residence or workplace, We will reimburse the actual cost incurred up to the limits specified in the schedule of benefits stated under Section 9 of Your plan for any one (1) Accident or Infectious Disease [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

Any modifications done to Your Home or Office would need to be completed within six (6) months from the date the Medical Practitioner has confirmed Your condition to be Permanent Disablement [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

## Section 10 - Death due to Infectious Disease

> We shall pay the Benefit Amount in accordance to the schedule of benefits stated under Your plan if during the Period of Insurance, You contract an Infectious Disease and because of this directly results in Death within fifty-two (52) weeks from the date of diagnosis of the Infectious Disease. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md]

> We will not pay for the benefit under Section 10 if We have already done so under Section 1. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md]

## Section 11 - Permanent Disablement due to Infectious Disease

> If during the Period of Insurance, You contract an Infectious Disease and because of this directly results in Permanent Disablement within fifty-two (52) weeks from the date of diagnosis, We shall pay You up to the maximum limits stated under Your plan as specified under Section 11 of the table of benefits using the scale of compensation table as shown below. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md]

## 1. Sedentary Work / Off-Duty Cover

If You are employed under any of the excluded occupations stated under General Exclusion, You shall still be covered under this Policy provided that: [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

- a) You are employed to do sedentary/desk-bound duties only; or - b) You are off-duty at the time of the Accident and the Bodily Injury does not arise in the course of Your employment or any activity related to Your employment [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

## 2. Suffocation by Smoke, Poisonous Fumes, Gas or Drowning

Any result sustained by You due to suffocation by smoke, poisonous fumes, gas or drowning shall be deemed to be Bodily Injury covered under this Policy provided that such Bodily Injury does not arise in the course of Your employment or out of Your neglect, wilful or intentional act [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

## 3. Disappearance

> If You disappear and after a period of twelve (12) consecutive months Your body cannot be found, and sufficient evidence is provided that leads Us to believe that You have died as a result of an Accident, We will pay the Accidental Death Benefit. If at any time after the payment, this belief is incorrect, then the amount paid must be repaid to Us. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md]

## 4. Riot, Strike, Civil Commotion

This Policy covers death or disablement sustained by the Insured Person as a result of riot, strike, civil commotion, hijack, murder or assault. Provided that such injury does not arise out of the Insured Person's own participation, collaboration or provocation of such act [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

## 5. Peace Time Reservist Training

This Policy covers injury sustained in the course of peacetime reservist training within Singapore [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

## 9. Payment of Benefit

Any benefit payable under this Policy shall be paid to You or Your nominated beneficiary, if any, as stated in the Schedule or otherwise to Your estate. Any receipt by You or Your nominated beneficiary or estate of any Benefit payable under this Policy shall in all cases be deemed final and complete discharge of all of Our liabilities in respect of such benefit [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-safety-policy-wording-v2.md].

## 11 What Do We Mean With These Words?

> Advance Premium refers to the second year's Premium that You have paid in advance at the time of application. The Advance Premium facility is not a bank savings account nor a bank deposit. Advance Premium will be refunded without any interest when You surrender Your policy, make a claim on the policy for the Death Benefit or when the policy ends as a result of Termination clauses 10 (f), (g) or (h), in the first policy year. [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md]

Age means the age at next birthday [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Assignee means any person to whom You have transferred Your rights as the Policy Owner and the assignment is registered with Us [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Benefit(s) means any payments that We will pay and/or the amount of Premium that We will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by Us [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Life insured means the person whom We provide the cover for. The Life insured does not have any right to the policy, unless he/she is also the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Maturity date is the date when the policy matures [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

> Nominee(s) is a person that You have nominated (under the Insurance Act 1966 and Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009 ) to receive the policy monies payable under the policy upon Your death. The nomination must be registered with Us. [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md]

Non-participating means it does not share in any surplus or profits of the Company's fund [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Policy commencement date means the date the policy commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page. This is the date We take as the policy anniversary [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Policy issue date means the date We issue the policy. This is shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Policy Information Page or any Endorsement issued by Us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the policy has been transferred to another party [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Premium(s) is the amount of money that You pay to Us to keep this policy in force [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Pre-existing condition means the existence of any signs or symptoms before the Policy commencement date or Reinstatement date for which treatment, medication, consultation, advice, or diagnosis has been sought or received by the Life insured or would have caused any reasonable and sensible person to get medical advice or treatment [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

- a) subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict Us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - b) who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act 1966 . It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own Benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased. [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md]

Proposal form means the form You signed to buy this policy from Us. It includes anything written, said (and recorded) and/or any document given to Us which has information for us to rely on before We decide to issue this policy [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Reinstatement date is the date We reinstate Your policy back to in force [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Surrender value means the monetary value this policy has accumulated over the policy term of the policy, minus any outstanding amount that You owe Us [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

We, Our, Us, the Company means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Registration No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

You, Your means the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

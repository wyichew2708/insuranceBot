## 3 Our Responsibilities

This is a limited pay regular Premium, Non-participating endowment plan. This plan provides protection against the death of the Life insured during the policy term. A lump sum guaranteed Maturity Benefit will be paid at the end of the policy term if the Life insured survives and We have not paid out any claims. This plan has a policy term of five (5) years [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

You have to pay Premiums for the first two (2) policy years [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

Your policy covers the Benefit(s) described below [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

## 3.1 Surrender Benefit

A guaranteed Surrender value, less any outstanding amount owing to Us, will be payable upon surrender [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

If you have paid an Advance Premium on this policy and you surrender the policy in the first policy year, we will pay the Surrender Benefit mentioned above and refund the Advance Premium paid (without any interest). Please refer to the Policy Illustration for the value of the Surrender Benefit [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

## 3.2 Maturity Benefit

On the Maturity date, if the policy is still in force, the Life insured survives and We have not paid out any claims, We will pay You a guaranteed Maturity Benefit in one lump sum, less any outstanding amount owing to Us. Please refer to the Policy Illustration for the value of the Maturity Benefit [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

## 3.3 Loans

> Subject to Our approval, You may take a loan on Your policy of up to 90% of its total Surrender value less any amounts owing to Us. [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md]

> We will charge an interest on the policy loan and may change the interest rate at any time by giving You thirty (30) days' written notice in advance. Interest accrues on a daily basis. You may repay the policy loan and interest at any time. If the policy loan plus interest is more than the total Surrender value, this policy will end. [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md]

We will deduct the policy loan and interest from any amount We may be due to pay to You under this policy [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

## 4.3 Changes in Information Provided

For any changes in the information (including any changes in the residency, citizenship or tax status) provided to Us when You are applying for this policy, We must be informed in writing immediately. We reserve the right to make changes to the policy based on the new information provided to Us [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

## 5 What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefit described below while the policy is in force [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

> If death of the Life insured occurs in the first policy year, We will pay 101% of one (1) yearly Premium paid, less any outstanding amount owing to Us. If You have paid an Advance Premium on this policy, the Advance Premium paid (without any interest) for second policy year will be refunded. [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md]

If death of the Life insured occurs from the second policy year onwards, we will pay the higher of: [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

> - a) 101% of two (2) yearly premium paid; or - b) Surrender value; [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md]

less any outstanding amount owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

## 6.1 Death Benefit

We do not pay the Death Benefit if the death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

> - a) intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later; or - b) Pre-existing conditions within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md]

When this happens, We will refund the total Premiums paid without interest, less any amounts owing to Us from this policy [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md].

## 2. Surrender Benefit

A guaranteed Surrender value, less any outstanding amount owing to Us, will be payable upon surrender. Please refer to the Policy Illustration for the value of the Surrender Benefit [src:raw/product-summaries/enrich-aspire-vii-product-summary.md].

If you have paid an Advance Premium on this policy and you surrender the policy in the first policy year, we will pay the Surrender Benefit mentioned above and refund the Advance Premium paid (without any interest) [src:raw/product-summaries/enrich-aspire-vii-product-summary.md].

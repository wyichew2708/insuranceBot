## 3.1 Surrender Benefit

A guaranteed Surrender value, less any outstanding amount owing to Us, will be payable upon surrender. If you have paid an Advance Premium on this policy and you surrender the policy in the first policy year, we will pay the Surrender Benefit mentioned above and refund the Advance Premium paid (without any interest). Please refer to the Policy Illustration for the value of the Surrender Benefit [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md#p2].

## 3.2 Maturity Benefit

On the Maturity date, if the policy is still in force, the Life insured survives and We have not paid out any claims, We will pay You a guaranteed Maturity Benefit in one lump sum, less any outstanding amount owing to Us. Please refer to the Policy Illustration for the value of the Maturity Benefit [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md#p2].

## 5 What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefit described below while the policy is in force [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md#p3].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

> If death of the Life insured occurs in the first policy year, We will pay 101% of one (1) yearly Premium paid, less any outstanding amount owing to Us. If You have paid an Advance Premium on this policy, the Advance Premium paid (without any interest) for second policy year will be refunded. [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md#p3]

> If death of the Life insured occurs from the second policy year onwards, we will pay the higher of: a) 101% of two (2) yearly premium paid; or b) Surrender value; less any outstanding amount owing to Us. When We make this payment, the policy ends. [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md#p3]

## 6.1 Death Benefit

> We do not pay the Death Benefit if the death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: a) intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later; or b) Pre-existing conditions within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md#p3]

When this happens, We will refund the total Premiums paid without interest, less any amounts owing to Us from this policy [src:raw/wordings/enrich-aspire-vii-policy-contract.md#p3].

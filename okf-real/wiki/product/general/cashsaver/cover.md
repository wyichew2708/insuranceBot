## 3. Our Responsibilities

This is a regular Premium endowment plan designed to provide a Yearly Cash Benefit starting from the end of the second Policy year and a Maturity Benefit at the end of the policy term [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

> This plan also provides coverage against death, terminal Illness and accidental death (on or before reaching the Policy anniversary when the Life insured attains Age 80) during the policy term. It also waives the Premium of the policy if You become totally and permanently disabled (on or before reaching the Policy anniversary when You attain Age 86). [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

> You can choose Your premium payment term to be two (2) or five (5) years. Depending on Your choice of premium term, the policy terms available ranges from six (6) to fifteen (15) years. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

This is a Participating life insurance policy. It allows You to participate in the performance of the Participating fund in the form of bonuses that are not guaranteed [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

This is a guaranteed issuance policy which means that We issue this policy without asking for Your health declaration or medical checks at the time of application [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

Your policy covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

## 3.1 Yearly Cash Benefit

You will receive a guaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit starting from the end of the second Policy year until the policy matures, as long as the Life insured is alive and the policy is in force [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

> The Yearly Cash Benefit is 3.9% of the Face value. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

> You may accumulate Your Yearly Cash Benefit with Us at the prevailing interest rate. The prevailing interest rate is non-guaranteed and We may change the interest rate at any time by giving You thirty (30) days' written notice. You may withdraw the Yearly Cash Benefit accumulated with Us at any time with no charges but the minimum withdrawal amount each time is the lower of either S$1,000 or the entire amount. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

Before We make any payments, any amounts owing to Us will first be deducted [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

> If You did not state your choice of Yearly Cash Benefit payout option, the default option is to accumulate Your Yearly Cash Benefit with Us at the prevailing interest rate which is non-guaranteed. You can write in to Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. to change Your option fourteen (14) days before Your Yearly Cash Benefit payout. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

## 3.2 Surrender Benefit

Upon surrender, the Surrender Benefit is paid in one lump sum. The total Surrender Benefit payable is the sum of the following: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

- a) the guaranteed Surrender value; and - b) performance bonus (if any); and - c) Yearly Cash Benefit and Non-Guaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit accumulated with Us (if any); less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

If You have paid an Advance Premium on this policy and You surrender the policy in the first Policy year, We will pay the Surrender Benefit mentioned above and refund the Advance Premium paid (without any interest) [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

## 3.3 Maturity Benefit

Upon maturity if the policy is still in force, the Maturity Benefit payable is the sum of the following: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

- a) the guaranteed maturity value; and - b) performance bonus (if any); and - c) Yearly Cash Benefit and Non-Guaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit accumulated with Us (if any); less any amount owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

## 3.5 Bonuses

Your policy comprises of a Participating component. A part of the Premiums You pay will go into a life Participating fund. We will share the profit or loss of this fund with You [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

Profits will be shared with You by giving You bonuses in the following ways: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

## a) Non-Guaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit

The Non-Guaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit may be payable starting from the end of the second Policy year until the policy matures, as long as the Life insured is alive and the policy is in force [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

The Non-Guaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit is declared yearly which will be paid to You together with the Yearly Cash Benefit. You may accumulate both Your Yearly Cash Benefit and the NonGuaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit with Us at the prevailing interest rate which is non-guaranteed [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

## 5. What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefits described below while the policy is inforce [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

Upon death of the Life insured during the term of the policy while the policy is in force, the Death Benefit payable is the sum of the following: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

> - a) 101% of the total Premiums paid (excluding Advance Premium); and - b) performance bonus (if any); and - c) Yearly Cash Benefit and Non-Guaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit accumulated with Us (if any); [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

If You have paid an Advance Premium on this policy and death of the Life insured occurred in the first two (2) Policy years, We will pay: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

> - a) 101% of one (1) yearly Premium paid if death of the Life insured occurred in the first Policy year and refund the Advance Premium paid (without any interest); or - b) 101% of two (2) yearly Premiums paid if death of the Life insured occurred in the second Policy year; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

plus any performance bonus (if any), Yearly Cash Benefit and Non-Guaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit accumulated with Us (if any), less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

## 5.2 Terminal Illness ('TI') Benefit

If the Life insured has a TI while the policy is in force, We will pay the Death Benefit in one lump sum [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

> The maximum aggregate amount payable is Five Million Singapore Dollars (SGD 5,000,000) per Life insured for all policies and riders issued by Us with TI Benefit. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

If the TI Benefit payable is less than the Death Benefit, the Death Benefit will be automatically reduced by the amount paid for the TI. The policy remains in force and the remaining Death Benefit is the original Death Benefit less the amount paid for TI [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

If the TI Benefit payable is the same as the Death Benefit, the policy ends when We make this payment [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

## 5.3 Accidental Death Benefit

> Upon Accidental death of the Life insured on or before reaching the Policy anniversary when the Life insured attains Age 80 while the policy is in force, We will pay 50% of the total Premiums paid (excluding Advance Premium) in addition to the Death Benefit. When We make this payment, the policy ends. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

If You have paid an Advance Premium on this policy and Accidental death of the Life insured occurred in the first two (2) Policy years, We will pay: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

> - a) 50% of one (1) yearly Premium paid in addition to the Death Benefit if death of the Life insured occurred in the first Policy year and refund the Advance Premium paid (without any interest); or - b) 50% of two (2) yearly Premiums paid in addition to the Death Benefit if death of the Life insured occurred in the second Policy year. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

> The maximum aggregate amount payable for Accidental death shall not exceed Two Million Singapore Dollars (SGD 2,000,000) per Life insured for all policies and riders issued by Us with Accidental death Benefits. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

## 5.4 Total &amp; Permanent Disability ('TPD') Premium Waiver Benefit

> If You become totally and permanently disabled (on or before reaching the Policy anniversary when You attain Age 86) during the premium term while the policy is in force, We will waive all future Premiums on the policy for the remaining premium term. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

> The maximum aggregate amount payable is Four Million Singapore Dollars (SGD 4,000,000) per Life insured for all policies and riders issued by Us with TPD Benefits. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

"Totally and Permanently Disabled" and 'Total and Permanent Disability' have the same meaning [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

> Total and permanent disability is a disability that lasts continuously for at least one hundred and eighty (180) days, which the Doctor considers permanent and expects it to last throughout Your lifetime. This means one of the following: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

> - a) A disability where Your Age is between 17 and 65 cannot perform any work or occupation, or - b) A disability where You suffer from any of these: - total and irrecoverable loss of sight in both eyes; or - loss of two or more limbs, each above the wrist or ankle; or - total and irrecoverable loss of sight in one eye and loss of one limb at or above the wrist or ankle. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

- c) A disability where You cannot do at least three (3) out of six (6) Activities of Daily Living without physical help from another person or mechanical equipment [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

The six (6) Activities of Daily Living are: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

Washing - The ability to wash in the bath or shower (including getting into and out of the bath or shower) or wash by other means [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

Dressing - The ability to put on, take off, secure and unfasten all garments and, as appropriate, any braces, artificial limbs or other surgical or medical appliances [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

Feeding - The ability to feed oneself food after it has been prepared and made available [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

Toileting - The ability to use the lavatory or manage bowel and bladder function through the use of protective undergarments or surgical appliances if appropriate [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

Mobility - The ability to move indoors from room to room on level surfaces [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

Transferring - The ability to move from a bed to an upright chair or wheelchair, and vice versa [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

- d) A disability where You have to be confined to a home, a Hospital or other institution in order to receive constant care and medical attention [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

If You are unemployed at the time of disability, then only (b) and (c) shall apply [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

## 6.1 Death Benefit

We do not pay the Death Benefit if the death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

> - a) intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later; or - b) Pre-existing conditions within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

When this happens, We will refund the total Premiums paid without interest, less any amounts owing to Us from this policy [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

## 6.2 TI Benefit

We do not pay the TI Benefit if the TI is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

> - a) intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; - b) Pre-existing conditions within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later; or - c) Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md]

## 6.3 Accidental Death Benefit

We do not pay the Accidental Death Benefit if the Accidental death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

- a) intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; - b) state of an unsound mind; - c) unlawful acts, provoked assault or deliberate exposure to danger; - d) the effects of alcohol, drugs or any dependence; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

- e) illnesses, psychological conditions or eating disorders; - f) heat stroke; - g) a bad reaction to drugs or medication; - h) the effects of viruses (for example, dengue), bacteria or diseases; - i) the negative effects or complications of medical and surgical care; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

- j) treatment aimed at improving appearance, such as cosmetic surgery or any treatment relating to a previous cosmetic treatment; - k) radiation or contamination from radioactivity; - l) being in any aircraft, except as a fare-paying passenger in a commercial aircraft, or during military operations in peacetime; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-cashsaver-policy-contract.md].

## About Etiqa Or Plant Machinery

## SUM INSURED

It shall be a requirement of this Policy that the sum insured is equal to the cost of replacement of the insured items by new items of the same kind and capacity, which means their cost of replacement including, e.g., freight, customs duties and dues, if any, and cost of erection [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractor-plant-machinery-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

If the sum insured is less than the amount required to be insured, the insurers shall pay only in such proportion as the sum insured bears to the amount required to be insured. Every item if more than one shall be subject to this condition separately [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractor-plant-machinery-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

## 11. What Do We Mean With These Words?

Age means the age at next birthday [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Account value refers to sum of the single premium paid, any Top-up(s) made and the accumulated interest, less any partial withdrawal(s) [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Assignee means any person to whom you have transferred your rights as the Policy Owner and the assignment is registered with us [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Benefit(s) means any payments that we will pay and/or the amount of Premium that we will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by us [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Key personnel means a key employee, partner or proprietor on whom the continued successful operation of a business depends [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Life insured means the person whom we provide the cover for. The Life insured does not have any right to the policy, unless he/she is also the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

> Nominee(s) is a person that you have nominated (under the Insurance Act, Chapter 142 and Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009 ) to receive the policy monies payable under the policy upon your death. The nomination must be registered with us. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

Non-participating means it does not share in any surplus or profits of the Company's fund [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Policy commencement date means the date the policy commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page. This is the date we take as the policy anniversary [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Policy issue date means the date we issue the policy. This is shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Policy month is the 1-month period that starts on the Policy commencement date or any subsequent monthiversary of the Policy commencement date [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Policy Information Page or any Endorsement issued by us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the policy has been transferred to another party [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Policy year is the 1-year period that starts on the Policy commencement date or any subsequent anniversary of the Policy commencement date [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Premium(s) is the amount of money that you pay singly or regularly to us to keep this policy alive so you may claim for the benefits [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Pre-existing condition means the existence of any signs or symptoms before the policy effective date for which treatment, medication, consultation, advice, or diagnosis has been sought or received by the Life insured or would have caused any reasonable and sensible person to get medical advice or treatment [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act, Chapter 142 . It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased. [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md]

Proposal form means the form you signed to buy this policy from Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. It includes anything written, said (and recorded) and/or any document given to us which has information for us to rely on before we decide to issue this policy [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Reinstatement date is the date we reactivate your policy to bring it back to life after it ended due to the Account value being zero or less than zero and the policy has lapsed [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

Top-up(s) means the subsequent single premium(s) you make to your policy after your policy is issued (either ad-hoc or recurring) [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

We, Our, Us, the Company means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

You, Your means the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/elastiq-general-provisions-contract-7-nov.md].

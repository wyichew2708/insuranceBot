## 3.2 Surrender Benefit

Upon surrender, the surrender Benefit is paid in one lump sum. The total surrender Benefit payable is the sum of the following: a) guaranteed Surrender value; b) Surrender value of reversionary bonus (if any); c) performance bonus (if any); and d) any re-invested yearly cash Benefits and any non-guaranteed interest (if not previously withdrawn) less any outstanding amount that You still owe Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p2].

## 3.3 Guaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit

> Enrich rewards gives You a guaranteed yearly cash Benefit of 2.5% of the Face value* every year starting from the end of the 3rd Policy year so long as Your policy is valid. This guaranteed yearly cash Benefit will end one (1) year before the Maturity date. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p2]

> You have the option to either receive the amount yearly (via direct credit to Your designated bank account) or deposit the amount with Us and earn a non-guaranteed interest. While You can choose one of these options at the application stage, if You did not state Your choice of option, the default option is to reinvest the guaranteed cash Benefit. You can write in to Us to change the cash Benefit receiving option subsequently before the first cash Benefit is paid. We may change the interest rate at any time by giving You thirty (30) days’ notice. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p2]

> You may withdraw the deposit with Us at any time with no charges but the minimum withdrawal amount each time is the lesser of either S$1,000 or the entire deposit amount. Before We make any payments, any amounts owing to Us will first be deducted. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p2]

*Face value of this policy is the notional value used to determine the non-guaranteed bonuses and the guaranteed cash Benefits. It is not the sum insured of Your policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p2].

## 3.4 Maturity Benefit

On the Maturity date, if the Life insured survives and the policy is still in force, We will pay You: a) guaranteed maturity Benefit of the total Premium You paid less total yearly cash Benefits to-date (excluding interest and Automatic Premium Benefit); [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p3].

b) reversionary bonus (if any); c) performance bonus (if any); and d) any re-invested yearly cash Benefits and any non-guaranteed interest (if not previously withdrawn) less any outstanding amount that You still owe Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p3].

## 5 What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefits described below while the policy is inforce [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p4].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured, We will pay the death Benefit in one lump sum. The death Benefit payable is the sum of the following: a) 101% of the total Premiums You paid on this policy (excluding interest and Automatic Premium Benefit); [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p4]

b) reversionary bonus (if any); c) performance bonus (if any); and d) any re-invested yearly cash Benefits and any non-guaranteed interest (if not previously withdrawn), less any outstanding amount that You still owe Us. When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p4].

> While We assess the death claims, if the claimant needs financial assistance to prepare the funeral, We may consider offering an immediate payment of up to S$3,000 per Life insured. This funeral expense financial aid, if given, will be deducted from the final death Benefit to the claimant. Our advance payment [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p4]

of this financial aid does not mean We have approved the death claim. If the death claim is not approved later, after Our assessment, We have the right to ask You to return this advance payment [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p4].

## 6.1 Death Benefit

> We do not pay the death Benefit if the death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: a) Life insured commits intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p5]

> b) Pre-existing conditions within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. When there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our offer of conditional acceptance, the Policy Information Page and Endorsement. When any of the exclusion happens, We will return the higher of either the total Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest or the Surrender value (if any), less any amounts owing to Us. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md#p5]

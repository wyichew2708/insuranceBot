## 3 Our Responsibilities

> This is a regular Premium, endowment plan which gives guaranteed yearly cash Benefits from the end of 3 rd Policy year and a maturity Benefit at the end of the policy term. It also pays the sum of 101% of the total Premiums You paid on this policy and any attaching bonuses on the death of the Life insured during the policy term. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md]

> This plan has a policy term of ten (10) years but You only have to pay Premiums for the first three (3) years as this plan has an Automatic Premium Benefit that will pay the Premiums of the plan from the 4 th to 10 th Policy year. The Automatic Premium Benefit will only take effect after We have received the first three (3) years Premiums in full. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md]

This is a Participating life insurance policy. It allows You to participate in the performance of the Participating fund in the form of bonuses that are not guaranteed [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

It is a guaranteed issuance policy which means that We issue this policy without asking for Your health declaration or medical checks at the time of application [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

Your policy covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

## 3.1 Covered Events

If Your policy is valid and a covered event happens, We will pay You the Benefits less any amount You owe Us on this policy and its supplementary rider contract (where applicable). The covered events of Your policy are written on the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

## 3.2 Surrender Benefit

Upon surrender, the surrender Benefit is paid in one lump sum. The total surrender Benefit payable is the sum of the following: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

- a) guaranteed Surrender value; - b) Surrender value of reversionary bonus (if any); - c) performance bonus (if any); and - d) any re-invested yearly cash Benefits and any non-guaranteed interest (if not previously withdrawn) less any outstanding amount that You still owe Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

## 3.3 Guaranteed Yearly Cash Benefit

> Enrich rewards gives You a guaranteed yearly cash Benefit of 2.5% of the Face value* every year starting from the end of the 3 rd Policy year so long as Your policy is valid. This guaranteed yearly cash Benefit will end one (1) year before the Maturity date. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md]

> You have the option to either receive the amount yearly (via direct credit to Your designated bank account) or deposit the amount with Us and earn a non-guaranteed interest. While You can choose one of these options at the application stage, if You did not state Your choice of option, the default option is to reinvest the guaranteed cash Benefit. You can write in to Us to change the cash Benefit receiving option subsequently before the first cash Benefit is paid. We may change the interest rate at any time by giving You thirty (30) days' notice. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md]

> You may withdraw the deposit with Us at any time with no charges but the minimum withdrawal amount each time is the lesser of either S$1,000 or the entire deposit amount. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md]

Before We make any payments, any amounts owing to Us will first be deducted [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

*Face value of this policy is the notional value used to determine the non-guaranteed bonuses and the guaranteed cash Benefits. It is not the sum insured of Your policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

## 3.4 Maturity Benefit

On the Maturity date, if the Life insured survives and the policy is still in force, We will pay You: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

- a) guaranteed maturity Benefit of the total Premium You paid less total yearly cash Benefits to-date (excluding interest and Automatic Premium Benefit); - b) reversionary bonus (if any); - c) performance bonus (if any); and - d) any re-invested yearly cash Benefits and any non-guaranteed interest (if not previously withdrawn) less any outstanding amount that You still owe Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

## 3.5 Bonus

Your policy comprises of a Participating component. A part of the Premiums You pay will go into a life Participating fund. We will share the profit or loss of this fund with You. Profits will be shared with You by giving You bonuses in the following ways: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

The reversionary bonus is determined annually and credited to the policy on the following Policy anniversary. Once the reversionary bonus is declared and added/vested, it forms part of the guaranteed Benefit of the policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

This is a one-off bonus which is a percentage of the accumulated reversionary bonuses. It may be paid when You surrender the policy, makes a claim for any of the basic Benefits of the policy or when the policy matures. The amount vary depending on the number of completed years the policy has been in force with Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

Bonuses are not guaranteed until they are declared to You [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

## 3.6 Loans

> You may take a loan on Your policy of up to 90% of its total Surrender value less any indebtedness. We will charge an interest on the policy loan and may change the interest rate at any time by giving You thirty (30) days' written notice in advance. Interest accrues on a daily basis. You may repay the policy loan and interest at any time. If the policy loan plus interest is more than the total Surrender value, this policy will end. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md]

We will deduct the policy loan and interest from any amount We may be due to pay to You under this policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

## 3.7 Paid-up Policy

You may convert this policy to a Paid-up policy so that You do not have to pay future Premiums. The coverage will be reduced. After conversion to Paid-up policy, We will stop adding bonuses to Your policy. Policy loan is not allowed after conversion to Paid-up policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

## 3.8 Update on Policy Status

An annual bonus statement will be sent to You when Your policy starts to accrue bonuses in its 3 rd Policy year. This document aims to keep You informed of the performance of the fund and its future outlook and of the bonuses allocated to Your policy for that year [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

## 5 What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefits described below while the policy is inforce [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

Upon death of the Life insured, We will pay the death Benefit in one lump sum. The death Benefit payable is the sum of the following: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

> - a) 101% of the total Premiums You paid on this policy (excluding interest and Automatic Premium Benefit); - b) reversionary bonus (if any); - c) performance bonus (if any); and - d) any re-invested yearly cash Benefits and any non-guaranteed interest (if not previously withdrawn), less any outstanding amount that You still owe Us. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md]

When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

> While We assess the death claims, if the claimant needs financial assistance to prepare the funeral, We may consider offering an immediate payment of up to S$3,000 per Life insured. This funeral expense financial aid, if given, will be deducted from the final death Benefit to the claimant. Our advance payment of this financial aid does not mean We have approved the death claim. If the death claim is not approved later, after Our assessment, We have the right to ask You to return this advance payment. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md]

## 6.1 Death Benefit

We do not pay the death Benefit if the death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

> - a) Life insured commits intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. - b) Pre-existing conditions within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md]

When there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our offer of conditional acceptance, the Policy Information Page and Endorsement. When any of the exclusion happens, We will return the higher of either the total Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest or the Surrender value (if any), less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-rewards.md].

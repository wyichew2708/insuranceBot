## General Definitions

Accident / Accidental means an unforeseen event of violent, external and visible nature, occurring during the Period of Insurance, which results in Bodily Injury or death [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Benefit means the respective Benefit, as stated in the Policy, Schedule and/or Endorsement payable by Us under the terms and conditions and exclusions in respect of each event or loss covered by this Policy [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

> Bodily Injury means injury sustained by an Insured during the period of insurance and is caused by an Accident solely and independently of any other causes within fifty-two (52) weeks from the date of such Accident. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md]

Effective Date of Insurance means the commencement date of insurance as specified on the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

> Each day of Hospital Confinement is defined as a day of which the Hospital charges You for room and board upon admission as an in-patient for a minimum period of twenty-four (24) hours upon on the recommendation of a Medical Practitioner. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md]

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Family Member means Your spouse, parent, parent-in-law, grandparent, child, brother or sister [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Hospital means any institution which fully meets all of the following criteria: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

> - a) maintains permanent and full-time facilities for care of overnight resident patients; and - b) has diagnostic and therapeutic facilities for surgical and medical diagnosis and treatment and care of injured and sick persons by or under the supervision of a staff of Medical Practitioners; and - c) continuously provides twenty-four (24) hours a day nursing service supervised by qualified nurses; and - d) is not, other than incidentally, a mental institution or a place for rest or a place for the aged, drug addicts or alcoholics. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md]

> Hospital Confinement means an admission or a series of admissions from the same cause except that if admissions from the same cause are separated by twelve (12) months or more then each such admission shall constitute a separate Hospital Confinement. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md]

Infectious Disease shall mean the definitive diagnosis of any of the following infectious diseases by a qualified/registered Medical Practitioner during the period of insurance and is supported by acceptable clinical, radiological, histological and laboratory evidence: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

> 1. Hand, foot and mouth disease (HFMD) 2. Dengue fever (DHF) 3. Avian influenza or 'bird flu' due to influenza A viral strains H5N1, H9N2, H7N7, H7N9 or H1N1 4. Mumps 5. Rubella 6. Tuberculosis 7. Measles 8. Malaria 9. Anthrax infection 10. Yellow fever 11. Plague 12. Melioidosis or 'soil disease' 13. Rabies 14. Legionnaires' disease 15. Chikunguny 16. Nipah viral encephalitis 17. Japanese viral encephalitis 18. Variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (vCJD) or 'mad cow disease' 19. Severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS) 20. Middle-East respiratory syndrome coronavirus (MERS-CoV) 21. Zika virus 22. Group B Streptococcusin infection Type III GBS ST283 [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md]

Insured means the person(s) named in the Schedule whom We provide the coverage for. The Insured does not have any rights to the Policy, unless he is also the policy owner [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Legal Child means legal child(ren) including step child(ren) and / or legally adopted child(ren): [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

> - (a) who is / are at least one (1) year of age and not more than seventeen (17) years of age (or not more than twenty-five (25) years of age if studying full-time in a recognized tertiary institution) on the Effective Date of Insurance; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md]

- (b) wholly dependent on the adult Insured Person(s) or His Legal Spouse for financial support; and - (c) unmarried [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Legal Spouse means the adult Insured Person(s) legally married spouse [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Loss of Speech or Hearing means total and irrecoverable loss of speech or hearing which is beyond remedy by surgical or other treatment [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Loss of Sight means physical loss of an eye or permanent and total loss of sight, which shall be considered as having occurred: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

> - a) in both eyes if so certified by a registered fully qualified ophthalmic specialist. However, We reserve the right to seek a second opinion; or - b) in one (1) eye if the degree of sight remaining after correction is 1/60 or less on the Snellen Scale (that is seeing at one (1) foot what a person should see at sixty (60) feet). However, We reserve the right to seek a second opinion. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md]

- a) amputation of an entire hand or all four fingers and thumb from where they join the palm of the hand; or - b) total and permanent loss of use of an entire hand or all four fingers and thumb [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

- a) amputation of a foot (at or above the ankle); or - b) total and permanent loss of use of a foot [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Loss of Limb means loss or physical separation of a hand at/or above the wrist or a foot at/or above the ankle and shall include total and irrecoverable loss of use of hand, arm or leg [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Medical Practitioner means a registered and legally qualified physician, doctor or surgeon by a medical degree in western medicine and authorised by the medical licensing authority of that country to provide medical or surgical services within the scope of their license and training. This cannot be You, Your family member or relative, business partner, employer, employee or agent [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Medically Necessary shall mean a medical service which is: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

- a) consistent with the diagnosis and customary medical treatment for a covered disability; and - b) in accordance with standards of good medical practice, consistent with current standard of professional medical care and of proven medical benefits ; and - c) not for the convenience of the Insured or the physician, and unable to be reasonably rendered out of hospital (if admitted as an inpatient); and - d) not of an experimental, investigational or research nature, preventive or screening nature; and - e) for which the charges are fair, reasonable and customary for the disability [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Period of Insurance means the period starting from the Effective Date of Insurance during which the coverage under this Policy is effective [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

> Permanent Disablement means one of the items of disability listed in the scale of compensation under Section 2 and 12, and which was caused only by an Accident or contraction of an Infectious Disease, as long as: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md]

> - a) the disability lasts for twelve (12) months consecutively from the date of the Accident or date of diagnosis of the Infectious Disease; and - b) our Medical Practitioner confirms that it is not going to improve after twelve (12) months. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md]

Physiotherapist means a registered and legally qualified physiotherapist by a medical degree in western medicine and authorised by the medical licensing authority of that country to provide physiotherapy services within the scope of their license and training. This cannot be You, Your family member or relative, business partner, employer, employee or agent [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Pre-existing Illness shall mean disabilities or conditions that You have reasonable knowledge of [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

You may be considered to have reasonable knowledge of a pre-existing condition if: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

- a) You had received or is receiving treatment; or - b) medical advice, diagnosis, care or treatment has been recommended; or - c) clear and distinct symptoms are or were evident; or - d) its existence would have been apparent to a reasonable person in the circumstances [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Schedule means the information page that contains the details of the Insured, Benefit, Premium and Period of Insurance attached to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Temporary Total Disablement means a Bodily Injury which directly results in a state of incapacity that prevents you from engaging in the usual gainful employment or employment temporarily [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Traditional Chinese Medical (TCM) Practitioner means a licensed or registered Traditional Chinese Medical Practitioner with the Traditional Chinese Medicine Practitioners Board in Singapore, engaging in the practice of traditional Chinese medicine and/or acupuncture (including bonesetter or chiropractor). This cannot be You, your family member or travelling companion, partner, business partner, employer, employee or agent [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Us / We / Our means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Reg. No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

Waiting Period means the period from the Effective Date of Insurance before the Benefit(s) can be payable [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

You / Your / Insured means the Insured named in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording-final-v1-0-18052022.md].

## General Definitions

Accident / Accidental means an unforeseen event of violent, accidental, external and visible nature, occurring during the Period of Insurance, which results in Bodily Injury or death [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Act of Terrorism means an act, including but not limited to the use of force or violence and/or the threat thereof, of any person or group(s) of persons, whether acting alone or on behalf of or in connection with any organisation(s) or government(s), committed for political, religious, ideological or ethnic purposes or reasons including the intention to influence any government and/or to put the public, or any section of the public, in fear [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

> Adult means an individual who has attained the age of eighteen (18) years of age. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

Benefit means the respective Benefit, as stated in the Policy, Schedule and/or Endorsement payable by Us under the terms and conditions and exclusions in respect of each event or loss covered by this Policy [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

> Bodily Injury means any injury sustained by an Insured Person(s) during the period of insurance and is caused by an Accident solely and independently of any other causes within twelve (12) months from the date of such Accident. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

Child(ren) means legal Child(ren) including step Child(ren) and/or legally adopted Child(ren): [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

> - (a) Who is / are at least three (3) months of age and not more than eighteen (18) years of Age (or not more than twenty-five (25) years of Age if studying full-time in a recognised tertiary institution) on the Effective Date of Insurance; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

> - (b) Wholly dependent on his parent(s) for financial support; - (c) Unmarried; and - (d) Child under the age of 12 years must be accompanied by a parent or adult guardian during the trip. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

> Compulsory Quarantine means the Insured Person, under the order of a government issued quarantine order, being quarantined in a facility designated by a government authority due to being diagnosed with COVID-19 for a minimum of twenty-four (24) consecutive hours until discharged. Compulsory Quarantine does not include a period of quarantine that is the result of the closure of borders by a government or travel body, the enforcement of social distancing measures, a stay-at-home notice, or a period of isolation or social distancing required in relation to the Insured Person's departure or arrival at the Insured Person's destination Overseas or Usual Country of Residence. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

The level of cover selected by You at the time of Your insurance application: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

- (a) Entry Plan, Savvy Plan, Luxury Plan, Pre-Ex Entry Plan, Pre-Ex Savvy Plan or Pre-Ex Luxury Plan; providing different types and levels of benefits - (b) Individual Plan, Family Plan, Married Couple Plan or Group Plan; and - (c) Single Trip Policy or Annual Multi-Trip Policy as stated in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

> - (a) Insured Person or Insured Person's Travel Companion being diagnosed with COVID-19 and confirmed by a Doctor; - (b) The death of the Insured Person or their immediate family member or Travel Companion due to COVID19, as certified by a Doctor; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

> - (c) The Insured Person or Insured Person's Travel Companion serving a Compulsory Quarantine or stay home notice order from the government due to close contact with a positive COVID-19 carrier, and as a result missed the departure date of the Trip. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

Effective Date of Insurance means the commencement date of insurance as specified on the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Extreme Sports mean any sporting activity that presents a high level of inherent danger (that is, involving exceptional speed and height, high level of expertise, exceptional physical exertion or highly specialised gear or stunts) including but not limited to big wave surfing, canoeing down rapids, cliff jumping, horse jumping, racing or motor rallies, off-piste skiing, potholing, ultra-marathons, biathlons, triathlons and stunt riding [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Family Member means Your spouse, parent, parent-in-law, grandparent, child, brother or sister [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Family Plan Family Plan is only available under Non Pre-Ex Plans [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

> For Single Trip policies - Maximum of up to six (6) adults traveling with up to nine (9) children. The maximum number of Insured Person(s) under one Family plan is ten (10) persons. The adults need not be related to the child(ren). All must depart from and return to Singapore together at the same time. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

For Annual Multi-Trip Policy - The Insured Person(s) will comprise You, Your Legal Spouse and any number of Your Legal Children. The child(ren) need not be accompanied by either of the parent for any trips made during the Period of Insurance [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

- (a) A Policy issued to You in respect of the Insured Persons named in the Schedule who are travelling as a group, that is not issued under a Family Plan or a Married Couple Plan [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

- (b) The Insured Persons under the Group Plan must travel together on a Journey. - (c) Group Plan is only available under Single Return Trip Policy for Pre-Ex Plans and Non Pre-Ex Plans [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Hijack means an illegal seize of an aircraft, ship or vehicle while in transit and force it to go to a different destination or use it for the hijacker's own purposes [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Home Contents shall mean any moveable household item belonging to the Insured Person in the Insured Person's Home, except for the following: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

- (a) Property more specifically insured under another policy. - (b) Motor vehicles and accessories, pedal cycles and watercraft. - (c) Deeds, bonds, bills of exchange, promissory notes, cheques, traveller's cheques, securities for money, stamps, certificates or documents of any kind, manuscripts, medals, contact lenses, hearing aids and livestock unless specially mentioned herein [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

- (d) Any part of the structure or ceilings of the building(s), wallpapers and the like or external television and radio antennae, aerials, aerial fittings, masts and towers. - (e) Any property the value of which is included in the Total Sum Insured on Renovation, fixtures and fittings or Building(s) [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

- (f) Landlord's fixtures and fittings. - (g) Property owned or held in trust in connection with any business profession or trade. - (h) Livestock [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Home means a building occupied as private dwelling (house, flat or apartment) together with its garages and outbuildings, all used for domestic purposes at the Insured Person's residential address [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Hospital means any institution which fully meets all of the following criteria: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

- (a) Maintains permanent and full-time facilities for care of overnight resident patients; - (b) Has diagnostic and therapeutic facilities for surgical and medical diagnosis and treatment and care of injured and sick persons by or under the supervision of a staff of medical practitioners; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

> - (c) Continuously provides twenty-four (24) hours a day nursing service supervised by qualified nurses, and; - (d) Is not other than incidentally a mental institution or a place for rest or a place for the aged or for drug addicts or for alcoholics. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

> Hospital Confinement means an admission or a series of admissions from the same cause except that if admissions from the same cause are separated by twelve (12) months or more then each such admission shall constitute a separate Hospital Confinement. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

Hostage means a person taken by force to secure the taker's demand [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Illness means any sudden and unexpected deterioration in health which manifests itself while Overseas during the Period of Insurance and is not caused by Bodily Injury [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Individual Plan means a Policy issued to the Insured in respect of You as the Insured Person named in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Infectious Disease shall mean the definitive diagnosis of any of the following infectious diseases by a Medical Practitioner during the Period of Insurance and is supported by acceptable clinical, radiological, histological and laboratory evidence: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

> - 1) Hand, foot and mouth disease (HFMD) - 2) Dengue fever (DHF) - 3) Avian influenza or 'bird flu' due to influenza A viral strains H5N1, H9N2, H7N7, H7N9 or H1N1 - 4) Mumps - 5) Rubella - 6) Tuberculosis - 7) Measles - 8) Malaria - 9) Anthrax infection - 10) Yellow fever - 11) Plague - 12) Melioidosis or 'soil disease' - 13) Rabies - 14) Legionnaires' disease - 15) Chikungunya - 16) Nipah viral encephalitis - 17) Japanese viral encephalitis - 18) Variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (VCJD) or 'mad cow disease' - 19) Severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS) - 20) Middle-East respiratory syndrome coronavirus (MERS-CoV) - 21) COVID-19 - 22) Zika virus - 23) Group B Streptococcusin infection Type III GBS ST283 [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

Insured Person(s) / His / Him / He in respect of Individual Plan means the person named in the Schedule as the Insured Person; in respect of Group, Married Couple or Family Plan means the person(s) as defined under such plan [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Journey means the period of travel which commences when the Insured Person completes the immigration departure clearance procedure in Singapore on or after the departure date specified in the Schedule for the purpose of commencing such journey and ends (a) on the last day specified in the Schedule or (b) when the Insured Person completes the immigration arrival clearance procedure upon returning to Singapore after such journey, whichever is earlier [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Kidnap means to seize and detain or carry away by unlawful force for fraud and often with a demand for ransom [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Laptop Computer means the complete laptop computer including accessories or attachments that come as standard equipment with the laptop. Any handheld computers, tablets (including but not limited to Apple iPads, Samsung Galaxy tablets) or similar devices are excluded from this category [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

- (a) Natural Disaster; - (b) Epidemic or pandemic as declared by the World Health Organisation; - (c) Major industrial accident as determined by Us; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

- (d) Civil unrest, riot or commotion resulting in cancellation of scheduled Public Conveyance services or in a relevant government warning against non-essential travel; - (e) Strike resulting in the cancellation of scheduled Public Conveyance services; or - (f) Any event leading to airspace or multiple airport closures [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

- (a) A Policy issued to You in respect of the Insured Persons named in the Schedule who are travelling as a married couple. The Insured Person(s) will comprise You and Your Legal Spouse. - (b) The Insured Person(s) under the Married Couple Plan must travel together on a Journey [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

- (c) Married Couple Plan is only available under Single Return Trip Policy for Pre-Ex Plans and Non Pre- Ex Plans [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Medical Expenses means the necessary and reasonable expenses incurred and paid to a medical practitioner, physician, Hospital and/or ambulance service provider for medical, surgical, X-ray, Hospital or nursing treatment including the cost of medical supplies and ambulance hire and including the cost of dental treatment where such treatment is necessarily incurred to restore sound and natural teeth and is caused by an Accident. All treatment, including specialist treatment, must be prescribed or referred by a Medical Practitioner in order for expenses to be reimbursed under this Policy and such reimbursement will not exceed the usual level of charges for similar treatment, medical services or supplies in the location where the expenses were incurred and paid had this insurance not existed. Treatments and services including medicines must be customary for the treatment of a condition you have and cannot be experimental or elective. Medical Expenses exclude any purchase of Mobility Aid [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Medical Practitioner means a registered and legally qualified physician, doctor or surgeon by a medical degree in western medicine and authorised by the medical licensing authority of that country to provide medical or surgical services within the scope of their licence and training. This cannot be You, Your Family Member or travelling companion, partner, business partner, employer, employee or agent [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Mobility Aid means a device designed to assist walking or otherwise improve the mobility of people with mobility impairments and includes but is not limited to crutches, wheelchairs, walking frames and wheel trolleys [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Natural Disasters mean extreme weather conditions as determined by Us, including but not limited to cyclones, hurricanes, typhoons, tornadoes, fires, floods, tsunamis, volcanic eruptions, earthquakes, landslides or other convulsion of nature or by consequences of any of the occurrences mentioned above [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Non Pre-Ex Plans refers to the Entry plan, the Savvy plan and/or the Luxury plan [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Overseas means beyond the territorial limits of Singapore [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Payment Card means an ATM card, NETS card, credit card, charge card or debit card issued by a registered financial institution in Singapore for personal use only [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

Period of Insurance means the period during which the coverage under this Policy is effective, as stated in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

> Permanent Disablement means one of the items of disability listed in the scale of compensation under Section 1 in the table of cover, and which was caused only by an accident, as long as: the disability lasts for twelve (12) months consecutively from the date of the accident; and Our Medical Practitioner confirms that it is beyond improvement after twelve (12) months. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md]

Pre-Ex Plans refers to the Pre-Ex Entry plan, Pre-Ex Savvy plan and/or the Pre-Ex Luxury plan [src:raw/wordings/tiq-travel-covid-19-policy-wording-v10-2026-03.md].

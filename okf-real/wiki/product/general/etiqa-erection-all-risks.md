## About Etiqa Erection All Risks Insurance

This product is compiled from its policy documents. The wording is the contract; the sections below quote it [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-erection-all-risks-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

## Section I - Material Damage

The company hereby agree with the insured that if at any time during the period of cover the items or any part thereof entered in the schedule shall suffer any unforeseen and sudden physical loss or damage from any cause, other than those specifically excluded, in a manner necessitating repair or replacement, the company will indemnify the insured in respect of such loss or damage as hereinafter provided by payment in cash, replacement or repair (at their own option) up to an amount not exceeding in respect of each of the items specified in the schedule the sum set opposite thereto and not exceeding in any one event the limit of indemnity where applicable and not exceeding in all the total sum expressed in the schedule as insured hereby [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-erection-all-risks-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

The company will also reimburse the insured for the cost of clearance of debris following upon any event giving rise to a claim under this policy provided a separate sum therefore has been entered in the schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-erection-all-risks-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

## MEMO 3 – EXTENSION OF COVER

Extra charges for overtime, nightwork, work on public holidays, express freight are covered by this insurance only if previously and specially agreed upon in writing [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-erection-all-risks-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

## Section II – Third Party Liability

The company will indemnify the insured up to but not exceeding the amounts specified in the schedule against such sums which the insured shall become legally liable to pay as damages consequent upon [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-erection-all-risks-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

accidental bodily injury to or illness of third parties (whether fatal or not) accidental loss of or damage to property belonging to third parties [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-erection-all-risks-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

occurring in direct connection with the erection, construction or testing of the items insured under section I and happening on or in the immediate vicinity of the site during the period of cover [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-erection-all-risks-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

In respect of a claim for compensation to which the indemnity provided herein applies, the company will in addition indemnify the insured against [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-erection-all-risks-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

all costs and expenses of litigation recovered by any claimant from the insured, and all costs and expenses incurred with the written consent of the company, [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-erection-all-risks-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

provided always that the liability of the company under this section shall not exceed the limits of indemnity stated in the schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-erection-all-risks-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

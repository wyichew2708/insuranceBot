## 3.2 Exclusions

There are certain conditions under which no Benefits will be payable. These conditions are stated as exclusions [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2].

We do not provide any Benefit under this policy caused by or contributed by or related to any of the following: [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2].

1. any wilful or intentional acts of the Life Insured, suicide pacts or agreements or complications of suicide [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2].

or attempted suicide, provoked homicide or assault or self-inflicted injury; [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2].

2. any condition which is or results from intoxication by alcohol or drugs not prescribed by a Medical [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2].

3. any condition sustained whilst engaging in flying or other aerial activities other than in a fully licensed [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2].

for the purpose of any trade or technical operation in or on the aircraft; [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2].

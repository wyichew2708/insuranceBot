## 3.2 Survival Benefit

If the Life insured survives and the policy is still in force at the payout date, You will receive the survival benefit in the way You have chosen to receive it. Survival Benefit is the sum of a) total Premiums You paid on the Basic policy (not inclusive of interest and Automatic Premium Benefit); [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p2].

b) reversionary bonus (if any); and c) performance bonus (if any); less any outstanding amount that You still owe Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p2].

## Survival Benefit Receiving Option

> You have the option of receiving the survival Benefit either in a lump sum or in yearly payments of ten (10) or fifteen (15) years via direct credit to Your designated bank account. You may write in to Us to change the survival Benefit receiving option at any time twelve (12) months before the payout date. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p2]

> a) If You opt to receive the survival Benefit in one lump sum, the policy will end when We make this payment. This payment is the maturity Benefit. b) If You opt to receive the survival Benefit in ten (10) yearly payments, You will receive the amount including non-guaranteed interest* earned on the survival Benefit balance starting from the payout date and every anniversary thereafter up to the 21st Policy year, so long as Your policy is valid. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p2]

> Yearly payment on the 21st Policy year will be paid as the maturity Benefit. c) If You opt to receive the survival Benefit in fifteen (15) yearly payments, You will receive the amount including non-guaranteed interest* earned on the survival Benefit balance starting from the payout date and every anniversary thereafter up to the 26th Policy year, so long as Your policy is valid. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p2]

Yearly payment on the 26th Policy year will be paid as the maturity Benefit [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p2].

> Yearly payments cannot be deposited with Us and We may change the interest rate at any time by giving You thirty (30) days’ notice. *Interest earned each year will be spread over the remaining years. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p2]

## 3.3 Surrender Benefit

> Upon surrender, the surrender Benefit is paid in one lump sum. The total surrender Benefit payable is the sum of the following: (a) Before Policy year 12 while the policy is still in force a) guaranteed Surrender value; b) Surrender value of reversionary bonus (if any); and c) performance bonus (if any) less any outstanding amount that You still owe Us. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p3]

> (b) After Policy year 12 while the policy is still in force a) guaranteed Surrender value; and b) accumulated non-guaranteed interest (if any) less surrender charge and any outstanding amount that You still owe Us. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p3]

## 3.4 Maturity Benefit

> On the Maturity date, if the Life insured survives and the policy is still in force, We will pay You: a) The full survival Benefit if You have chosen to receive it in one lump sum; or b) The last yearly payment if You have chosen to receive in yearly payments of ten (10) or fifteen (15) years. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p3]

## 5 What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefits described below while the policy is inforce [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p5].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured, We will pay the death Benefit in one lump sum. The death Benefit payable is the sum of the following: a) Before the payout date while the policy is still in force We will pay the sum of 101% of the total Premiums You paid on the Basic policy (not inclusive of interest and Automatic Premium Benefit) and any attaching bonus, less any outstanding amount that You still owe Us. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p5]

> b) On and after the payout date while the policy is still in force We will pay 101% of the sum of the survival Benefit less all yearly payments disbursed and any non-guaranteed interest. Payout date refers to the end of the 12th Policy year and it is the date which You start to receive Your survival Benefit. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p5]

> When We make this payment, the policy ends. While We assess the death claims, if the claimant needs financial assistance to prepare the funeral, We may consider offering an immediate payment of up to S$3,000 per Life insured. This funeral expense financial aid, if given, will be deducted from the final death Benefit to the claimant. Our advance payment of this financial aid does not mean We have approved the death claim. If the death claim is not approved later, after Our assessment, We have the right to ask You to return this advance payment. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p5]

## 6.1 Death Benefit

> We do not pay the death Benefit if the death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: a) Life insured commits intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later; or b) Pre-existing conditions within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p5]

When there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p5].

offer of conditional acceptance, the Policy Information Page and Endorsement. When any of the exclusion happens, We will return the higher of either the total Premiums You paid on this policy (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest or the Surrender value (if any), less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md#p5].

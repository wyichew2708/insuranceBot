## 3 Our Responsibilities

> This is a regular Premium endowment plan that provides a survival Benefit at the end of 12 th Policy year if the Life insured survives. You have the option of receiving the survival Benefit either in a lump sum or in yearly payments of ten (10) or fifteen (15) years. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

> This plan is available for policy term of twelve (12), twenty-one (21) or twenty-six (26) years. Premium term is twelve (12) years but You only have to pay Premiums for the first seven (7) years as this plan has an Automatic Premium Benefit that will pay the Premiums from the 8 th to 12 th Policy year. The Automatic Premium Benefit will only take effect when We have received the full first seven (7) years Premiums. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

This is a Participating life insurance policy. It allows You to participate in the performance of the Participating fund in the form of bonuses that are not guaranteed [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

This is a guaranteed issuance policy which means that We issue this policy without asking for Your health declaration or medical checks at the time of application [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

Your policy covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

## 3.1 Covered Events

If Your policy is valid and a covered event happens, We will pay You the Benefits less any amount You owe Us on this policy and its supplementary rider contract (where applicable). The covered events of Your policy are written on the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

## 3.2 Survival Benefit

If the Life insured survives and the policy is still in force at the payout date, You will receive the survival benefit in the way You have chosen to receive it [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

Survival Benefit is the sum of [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

- a) total Premiums You paid on the Basic policy (not inclusive of interest and Automatic Premium Benefit); - b) reversionary bonus (if any); and - c) performance bonus (if any); [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

less any outstanding amount that You still owe Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

## Survival Benefit Receiving Option

> You have the option of receiving the survival Benefit either in a lump sum or in yearly payments of ten (10) or fifteen (15) years via direct credit to Your designated bank account. You may write in to Us to change the survival Benefit receiving option at any time twelve (12) months before the payout date. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

> - a) If You opt to receive the survival Benefit in one lump sum, the policy will end when We make this payment. This payment is the maturity Benefit. - b) If You opt to receive the survival Benefit in ten (10) yearly payments, You will receive the amount including non-guaranteed interest* earned on the survival Benefit balance starting from the payout date and every anniversary thereafter up to the 21 st Policy year, so long as Your policy is valid. Yearly payment on the 21 st Policy year will be paid as the maturity Benefit. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

> - c) If You opt to receive the survival Benefit in fifteen (15) yearly payments, You will receive the amount including non-guaranteed interest* earned on the survival Benefit balance starting from the payout date and every anniversary thereafter up to the 26 th Policy year, so long as Your policy is valid. Yearly payment on the 26 th Policy year will be paid as the maturity Benefit. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

> Yearly payments cannot be deposited with Us and We may change the interest rate at any time by giving You thirty (30) days' notice. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

*Interest earned each year will be spread over the remaining years [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

## 3.3 Surrender Benefit

Upon surrender, the surrender Benefit is paid in one lump sum. The total surrender Benefit payable is the sum of the following: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

> (a) Before Policy year 12 while the policy is still in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

- a) guaranteed Surrender value; - b) Surrender value of reversionary bonus (if any); and - c) performance bonus (if any) [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

> (b) After Policy year 12 while the policy is still in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

- a) guaranteed Surrender value; and - b) accumulated non-guaranteed interest (if any) [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

less surrender charge and any outstanding amount that You still owe Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

## 3.4 Maturity Benefit

On the Maturity date, if the Life insured survives and the policy is still in force, We will pay You: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

> - a) The full survival Benefit if You have chosen to receive it in one lump sum; or - b) The last yearly payment if You have chosen to receive in yearly payments of ten (10) or fifteen (15) years. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

## 3.5 Bonus

Your policy comprises of a Participating component. A part of the Premiums You pay will go into a life Participating fund. We will share the profit or loss of this fund with You. Profits will be shared with You by giving You bonuses in the following ways: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

The reversionary bonus is determined annually and credited to the policy on the following Policy anniversary. Once the reversionary bonus is declared and added/vested, it forms part of the guaranteed Benefit of the policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

This is a one-off bonus which is a percentage of the accumulated reversionary bonuses. It may, at Our sole discretion, be paid when You surrender the policy, make a claim for any of the basic Benefits of the policy or when the policy provides the survival Benefit. The amount will vary depending on the number of completed years the policy has been in force with Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

Bonuses are not guaranteed until they are declared to You [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

## 5 What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefits described below while the policy is inforce [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

Upon death of the Life insured, We will pay the death Benefit in one lump sum. The death Benefit payable is the sum of the following: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

> - a) Before the payout date while the policy is still in force We will pay the sum of 101% of the total Premiums You paid on the Basic policy (not inclusive of interest and Automatic Premium Benefit) and any attaching bonus, less any outstanding amount that You still owe Us. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

> - b) On and after the payout date while the policy is still in force We will pay 101% of the sum of the survival Benefit less all yearly payments disbursed and any non-guaranteed interest. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

> Payout date refers to the end of the 12 th Policy year and it is the date which You start to receive Your survival Benefit. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

> While We assess the death claims, if the claimant needs financial assistance to prepare the funeral, We may consider offering an immediate payment of up to S$3,000 per Life insured. This funeral expense financial aid, if given, will be deducted from the final death Benefit to the claimant. Our advance payment of this financial aid does not mean We have approved the death claim. If the death claim is not approved later, after Our assessment, We have the right to ask You to return this advance payment. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

## 6.1 Death Benefit

We do not pay the death Benefit if the death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

> - a) Life insured commits intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later; or - b) Pre-existing conditions within the first twelve (12) months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md]

When there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our offer of conditional acceptance, the Policy Information Page and Endorsement. When any of the exclusion happens, We will return the higher of either the total Premiums You paid on this policy (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest or the Surrender value (if any), less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-assure.md].

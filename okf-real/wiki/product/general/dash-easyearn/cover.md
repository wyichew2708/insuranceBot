## 3. Our Responsibilities

> This is a single premium, Non-participating universal life plan denominated in Singapore dollars. It matures on the policy anniversary immediately before You attain 100 years old. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

Your policy covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

## 3.1 Covered Events

If Your policy is valid and a covered event happens, We will pay You the Benefits less any amounts You owes Us on Your policy. The covered events of Your policy is written on the Certificate of Insurance [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

## 3.2 Update on Policy Status

An annual policy statement will be sent to You. This document aims to keep You informed of the status of Your policy such as the Account value, total Top-up(s) and total partial withdrawal(s) [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

## 4.2 Changes in Information Provided

For any changes in the information (including any changes in the residency, citizenship or tax status) provided to Us when You are applying for this policy, We must be informed in writing immediately. We reserve the right to make changes to the policy based on the new information provided to Us [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

## 5. What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefit described below while the policy is in force [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured while the policy is in force, We will pay 105% of Account value, less any amounts owing to Us. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

## 5.2 Account Value

> Minimum single premium to effect cover for a Life insured starts from S$100; up to a maximum Account value of S$2,000. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

- a) any ad-hoc Top-up(s); and - b) accumulated interest; [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

- a) any partial withdrawal(s); and - b) any partial withdrawal(s) charges [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

> Any interest payable will be credited into the Account value at the end of each Policy month. If the average daily Account value for the Policy month falls below S$100, no interest will be credited for the Policy month. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

## 5.3 Crediting Rate

> For the initial single premium, the crediting rate for the 1 st year from the policy issue date is guaranteed and fixed at the crediting rate determined by Us on the policy issue date. You may refer to the Policy Illustration for the guaranteed crediting rates for the 1 st year from the policy issue date. For subsequent years, the crediting rate will be determined by Us based on the prevailing rate, subject to the minimum guaranteed crediting rate of 0.0% p.a. which ensures that the Life insured's capital is fully guaranteed each year. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

For any Top-up(s) made, the crediting rate will be determined by Us based on prevailing market conditions. We reserve the right to revise the crediting rate for Top-up(s) from time to time [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

> Interest is calculated based on the daily Account value and credited into the Account value at the end of each Policy month. If the average daily Account value for the Policy month falls below S$100, no interest will be credited for the Policy month. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

Any revision in crediting rates will take effect on the 1st business day of each calendar month but any interest earned will only be credited to the Account value at the end of each Policy month [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

## 5.4 Top-up(s)

You can make an ad-hoc Top-up to Your policy immediately after the policy issue date, subject to Our approval [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

The Top-up(s) made are subject to the following: [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

> -  The minimum amount for each Top-up must be at least S$50; -  Top-up(s) amount must be in multiples of S$50; and -  The maximum aggregate amount for all Top-up(s) per policy is S$2,000 less the single premium paid to Us, and add the withdrawal amount and withdrawal charges(if any). [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

The Account value will increase by the amount of Top-up(s) made to the policy [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

## 5.5 Partial Withdrawal

You may request for partial withdrawal(s) anytime after the policy issue date, subject to the following: [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

> -  The withdrawal amount must be at least S$10 (or its multiples) per withdrawal; and -  The balance of Account value upon deduction of withdrawal amount and withdrawal charges(if) any, must be more than zero; -  After withdrawal, the average daily Account value for the Policy month must be at least S$100 to be entitled for interest to be credited at the end of the Policy month. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

Partial withdrawal will reduce the Account value by the withdrawal amount and withdrawal charges (if any) [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

> We reserve the right to delay the payment of the partial withdrawal amount for up to a period of 6 months from the date of Your withdrawal application. We will trigger this right when there is a surge in withdrawals (partial or full) within the Portfolio during a very short period of time. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

## 5.6 Fees and Charges

> A transaction fee of S$0.70 will be charge for each partial withdrawal request. However, no transaction fee is applicable for partial withdrawal request into Dash Wallet. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

## 5.7 Surrender Benefit

You may surrender Your policy in full or partially through partial withdrawal [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

Upon surrender, We will pay the surrender benefit in one lump sum which is equivalent to the Account value, less any amounts owing to Us. You may request for a full surrender any time after the free look period [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

> We reserve the right to delay the payment of the surrender value for up to a period of 6 months from the date of Your surrender request. We will trigger this right when there is a surge in withdrawals (partial or full) within the Portfolio during a very short period of time. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

## 5.8 Maturity Benefit

At maturity date, if the policy is still in force, the maturity benefit payable is the Account value less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

## 5.2 Account Value

> Minimum single premium to effect cover for a Life insured starts from S$2,000; up to a maximum Account value of S$20,000. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

> Any interest payable will be credited into the Account value at the end of each Policy month. If the average daily Account value for the Policy month falls below S$2,000, no interest will be credited for the Policy month. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

## 5.3 Crediting Rate

> Interest is calculated based on the daily Account value and credited into the Account value at the end of each Policy month. If the average daily Account value for the Policy month falls below S$2,000, no interest will be credited for the Policy month. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

## 5.4 Top-up(s)

> -  The minimum amount for each Top-up must be at least S$500; -  Top-up(s) amount must be in multiples of S$500; and -  The maximum aggregate amount for all Top-up(s) per policy is S$20,000 less the single premium paid to Us, and add the withdrawal amount and withdrawal charges(if any). [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

## 5.5 Partial Withdrawal

> -  The withdrawal amount must be at least S$100 (or its multiples) per withdrawal; and -  The balance of Account value upon deduction of withdrawal amount and withdrawal charges(if) any, must be more than zero; -  After withdrawal, the average daily Account value for the Policy month must be at least S$2,000 to be entitled for interest to be credited at the end of the Policy month. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

## Death Benefit

> Upon death of the Life insured while the policy is in force, we will pay 105% of Account value, less any amounts owing to us. When we make this payment, the policy ends. [src:raw/product-summaries/dash-easyearn-lite-product-summary.md]

## Surrender Benefit

Upon full surrender, we will pay the surrender benefit in one lump sum which is equivalent to the Account value, less any amounts owing to us. You may request for a full surrender any time after the free look period [src:raw/product-summaries/dash-easyearn-lite-product-summary.md].

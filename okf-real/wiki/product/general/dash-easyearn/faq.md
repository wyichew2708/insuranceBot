## What is Dash PET?

> Dash PET is an insurance savings plan that offers savings at high returns, with capital guaranteed and flexibility of funds. Grow your Dash PET while it takes care of your financial health. Dash PET life insureds are also covered for life protection and COVID-19 . The policy is underwritten by Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Reg. No. 201331905K). [src:raw/faq/dash-pet-12-august-2022.md]

## What are the benefits of Dash PET?

> As a Dash PET life insured, you enjoy: High returns, capital guaranteed: Earn as high as 1.6% 1 a. returns on your savings Easy requirement: Start saving from just S$50 up to S$50,000 Flexibility for partial withdrawals (Maximum withdrawals of up to 10% of your Single Premium and Top-ups in the first policy year. Surrender charges within first policy year apply.) Affordable add-on protection: Life coverage from as low as 2 cents a day with additional returns on your savings 1 Crediting rates are 1.6% p.a. for the first policy year, with capital guaranteed. Crediting rates are non-guaranteed. [src:raw/faq/dash-pet-12-august-2022.md]

## Dash PET is a yearly renewable policy. What does this mean?

> This is a yearly renewable plan and the policy term is 1 year. At the end of 1 year policy term, this policy will be renewed automatically for another 1 year at the same conditions on the expiry date before renewal, so long as the following conditions are met: this policy is in force on the expiry date before the renewal; and you (life insured) have not reached age 100 at the renewal date. Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. Insurance reserves the right to terminate this policy by giving 90 days’ notice. Upon termination, Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. will refund the account value, less any amounts owing to Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd.. [src:raw/faq/dash-pet-12-august-2022.md]

## What are the returns for Dash PET and how are they calculated?

> Dash PET life insureds will earn 1.6% p.a. returns on the account value for the first policy year. The minimum average daily account value balance is S$50 to be eligible for interests and other policy benefits. Receive up to 0.25% p.a. on the first S$10,000 of your Dash PET account value, for each active add-on protection. The returns are calculated daily based on the crediting rate. Returns will be credited on the 1st of every calendar month. [src:raw/faq/dash-pet-12-august-2022.md]

## What is Dash PET Plus?

> Dash PET Plus is a yearly renewable, optional single premium investment-linked insurance rider that is designed to meet your wealth accumulation needs. Dash PET Plus will renew yearly as long as the Dash PET policy remains in force till the policy anniversary immediately before the life insured attains 100 years old. This plan provides protection against death and terminal illness of the life insured during the policy term. Dash PET Plus allows you the flexibility to choose from portfolio funds for investment. Dash PET Plus is subject to the market performance of the assets of the ILP sub-funds. Your investment returns are subject to investment risks, including the possible loss of the principal invested. The value of the units in the Portfolio fund may fall as well as rise depending on economic / market conditions. [src:raw/faq/dash-pet-plus.md]

> To protect the interests of customers, the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) require financial institutions to assess whether customers have the financial knowledge and experience to understand the risks and features of unlisted Specified Investment Products, such as ILP or Unit Trust, prior to any purchases. To pass, you will have to meet at least one of the CKA criteria: Diploma or higher qualification in Accountancy, Actuarial Science, Business, Capital Markets, Commerce, Computational Finance, Economics, Finance, Financial Engineering, Financial Planning, Insurance, or Professional Finance – related qualifications (e.g. CFA, CPA/ACCA). A minimum of 3 consecutive years of working experience (including the provision of legal advice or possession of legal expertise) in the past 10 years in the development of, structuring of, management of, sale of, trading of, research on and analysis of investment products or the provision of training in investment products Transacted at least 6 times in a Collective Investment Scheme (e.g. Unit Trust) or Investment Linked Policy (ILP) in the last 3 years.

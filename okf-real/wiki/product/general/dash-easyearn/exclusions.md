## 6. What is Not Covered?

We will not pay the covered Benefit for the following: [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

## 6.1 Suicide

> Life insured commits intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first 12 months from the Policy issue date or 12 months from the last Top-up transaction made, whichever is later. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

When this happens, We will refund the Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to Life insured under this policy) without interest, less any amounts owing to Us from the Life insured's policy [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

> This policy becomes invalid if You commit intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first 12 months from the Policy issue date or 12 months from the last Top-up transaction made. When this happens, We will refund the Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest, less any outstanding amounts owing to Us from this policy. [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md]

## 6.4 One Cover Per Life Insured

Each Life insured is only allowed to purchase and hold one policy at any time under the same Portfolio [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

If You have more than one Certificate of Insurance under the same Portfolio, We will only pay the benefit based on the first incepted policy. Subsequent policy will be considered void and We will refund the Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under the subsequent policy) without interest, less any outstanding amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/dash-easyearn-lite-policy-terms-and-conditions.md].

## Exclusions

There are certain conditions under which no benefits will be payable: [src:raw/product-summaries/dash-easyearn-lite-product-summary.md].

> - a) Life insured commits intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first 12 months from the Policy issue date or 12 months from the last Top-up transaction made whichever is later. When this happens, we will refund the Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to you under this policy) without interest, less any amounts owing to us from this policy. [src:raw/product-summaries/dash-easyearn-lite-product-summary.md]

- b) Death due to pre-existing conditions throughout the policy term. When this happens, we will return the Account value on the date of death occurrence, less any amounts owing to us from this policy. - c) Condition(s) specific to the Life insured which we will not cover. If the Life insured suffers directly or indirectly from the excluded events, we will refund the higher of either the Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to you under this policy) without interest or the Surrender value (if any) on the date of event occurrence, less any amounts owing to us from this policy [src:raw/product-summaries/dash-easyearn-lite-product-summary.md].

The above are stated as exclusions in the policy Terms and Conditions. You are advised to read your policy Terms and Conditions for the full details of these exclusions [src:raw/product-summaries/dash-easyearn-lite-product-summary.md].

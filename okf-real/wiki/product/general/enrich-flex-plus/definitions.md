## 11 What Do We Mean With These Words?

Accidental / Accident means a sudden, unforeseen and unplanned event causing bodily injury that is violent, external and visible in nature. It is not caused by sickness, disease, gradual physical, mental changes or any other causes. The Accident must occur after the Policy issue date [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Age means the Age at next birthday [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Assignee means any person to whom You have transferred Your rights as the Policy owner and the assignment is registered with Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Basic policy means the policy as it exists, including the supplementary terms and any Endorsement made to it, without any optional supplementary contract / rider [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Benefit(s) means any payments that We will pay and/or the amount of Premium that We will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Child / Children means the biological child or legally adopted child [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Doctor means a licensed person who is qualified by degree in western medicine to practice medicine. The license is given by the appropriate medical authority of his country of residence to practice medicine within his scope of licensing and training. This cannot be You, the Life insured, a family member or a relative [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Face Value means the notional value used to determine the death benefit, Surrender value and maturity Benefit. It is not the sum insured of your policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Life insured means the person whom We provide the cover for. The Life insured at the Policy commencement date is defined as the primary Life insured. The Policy owner at the Policy commencement date may exercise the option to appoint a secondary Life Insured to this policy. The Life insured does not have any right to the policy, unless he/she is also the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Maturity date is the date the policy ends and where a maturity Benefit is payable [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

> Nominee(s) is a person that You have nominated (under the Insurance Act 1966 and Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009 ) to receive the policy monies payable under the policy upon Your death. The nomination must be registered with Us. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md]

Paid-up means You stop paying any future Premiums and the Benefit is reduced accordingly [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Participating means it shares in the surplus or profits of the life Participating fund [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Policy commencement date means the date the policy commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Policy issue date means the date We issue the policy. This is shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Policy Information Page or any Endorsement issued by Us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the policy has been transferred to another party [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Policy year / Policy anniversary is the 1-year period that starts on the Policy commencement date or any subsequent anniversary of the Policy commencement date [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Pre-existing condition means the existence of any signs or symptoms before the Policy commencement date or Reinstatement date for which treatment, medication, consultation, advice, or diagnosis has been sought or received by the Life insured or would have caused any reasonable and sensible person to get medical advice or treatment [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Premium(s) is the amount of money that You pay paid regularly to Us to keep this policy in force [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

- a) subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict Us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - b) who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act 1966 . It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own Benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md]

Proposal form means the form You signed to buy this policy from Us. It includes anything written, said (and recorded) and/or any document given to Us which has information for Us to rely on before We decide to issue this policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Reinstatement date is the date We reinstate Your policy back to inforce [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

Surrender value means the monetary value the Basic policy has accumulated over the lifetime of the policy, minus any outstanding amount that You owe Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

> Terminal illness is the conclusive diagnosis of an illness that is expected to result in the death of the Life insured within 12 months. This diagnosis must be supported by a specialist and confirmed by the Company's appointed Doctor. Terminal illness in the presence of HIV infection is excluded. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md]

We, Our, Us, the Company means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Co. Reg. No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

You, Your means the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md].

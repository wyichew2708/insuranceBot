## 6 What is Not Covered?

There are certain conditions under which no Benefits will be payable. When there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our offer of conditional acceptance, the Policy Information Page and Endorsement [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md#p6].

When any of the exclusion below happens, We will return the higher of either the total Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest, or the Surrender value (if any) less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md#p6].

All exclusions below are applicable to both the primary Life insured and secondary Life insured [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-flex-plus.md#p6].

## Exclusions

There are certain conditions under which no Benefits will be payable [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md#p3].

We do not pay the death benefit if the death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md#p3].

a) death from activity under special conditions; or b) [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md#p3].

> intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first 12 months from the Rider issue date, the last increase in rider’s Sum insured or the latest rider re-activation date, whichever is later. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md#p3]

> c) Pre-existing condition within the first 12 months from the Rider issue date, the last increase in rider’s [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md#p3]

Sum insured or the latest rider re-activation date, whichever is later [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md#p3].

We do not pay the TPD benefit if the TPD is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-harm or attempted suicide; [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md#p3].

Criminal acts, war (declared or not), terrorism and chemical warfare; c) Participating in aviation (except as fare-paying passenger or member crew of a commercial airline), any dangerous or hazardous sport or hobby such as (but not limited to) steeple chasing, polo, horse racing, underwater diving, hunting, motor vehicular racing, mountaineering or potholing, or aerial sports such as skydiving, parachuting, bungee jumping; [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md#p3].

d) Effects of drug or alcohol addiction; or e) Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) infection, Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) and [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md#p3].

When there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our offer of conditional acceptance, the policy information page and Endorsement [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md#p3].

When any of the exclusion happens, We will return the total Insurance cover charge paid for this rider without interest back into the Basic policy’s Account value, less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md#p3].

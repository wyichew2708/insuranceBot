## DEFINITIONS

Business Interruption – Financial loss incurred which arises because normal business at the location is disrupted as a result of property damage [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

Location – The Premises stated in this Policy where you conduct business [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

## DEFINITIONS

Insured Person(s) means the respective person(s) named in the Schedule as Insured Person(s) [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

Policyholder means the person(s) or entity named in the Schedule under whose name the Policy has been issued and who acts on behalf of the Insured Person(s) in making the Declarations, which forms the basis of this Contract [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

Accident or Accidental means an event, which is sudden, unforeseen or unexpected event that results in an Insured Person suffering death, Disablement or injury [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

Bodily Injury means injury resulting solely and directly from violent accidental external and visible means and does not include any medical condition, sickness or disease, or any naturally occurring condition, or the result of any gradually operating cause [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

## 2. INTERPRETATION

> (1) References to “Act” in this Policy mean the Work Injury Compensation Act 2019, as may be amended from time to time. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md]

> (2) References to “the Legislation” in this Policy mean the Work Injury Compensation Act 2019 and any regulations made thereunder, as may be amended from time to time. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md]

(3) Words used in the Policy have the meanings given by the Legislation [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

(4) References to “Terms of this Policy” mean any terms, exceptions, conditions and warranties, and any memorandum if applicable, contained in or endorsed on this Policy, which are consistent with the compulsory terms prescribed under the Act [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

(5) The Insured refers to each insured specified in the Schedule, including the Policyholder, that is participating in the insurance plan under this Policy [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

(6) The Policyholder refers to the party executing the contract for itself and on behalf of all other Insured specified in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

(7) The Insured’s risk profile is the risk of accident or disease to any employee in the Insured’s employment, taking account of the Insured’s workforce, payroll numbers and other material information required to be stated in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

(8) References to “Relevant Injury” in this Policy mean death or personal injury — [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

(a) sustained by an employee that is caused by an accident that — [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

arises out of or in the course of the employee’s employment with the Insured and [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

> occurs during the Period of Insurance; or (b) that results from a disease contracted in the circumstances mentioned in section 10(1) of the Act in respect of the employee’s employment with the Insured during the Period of Insurance. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md]

(9) References to “the employee’s employment with the Insured” in this Policy include work done by the employee for another person while the employee’s services are temporarily lent or let on hire by the Insured to that other person (as mentioned in section 3(2) of the Act) [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md].

> (10) References to “earnings” have the meaning given by the Act. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md]

> (11) References to “actual Annual Earnings” in this Policy mean the total of the monthly earnings paid by the Insured (as well as by other employers and known to the Insured) during the 12 months immediately before the Commencement Date of the Policy. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md]

> (12) References to “estimated Annual Earnings” in this Policy mean an estimate of the actual Annual Earnings. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md]

## DEFINITION OF PLATE GLASS

Fixed glass in windows, doors, partitions, shop front, showcases including fixed mirrors for which the Insured is responsible but excluding the frames, frameworks and/or fixtures. The glass insured is considered as plain and/or tempered and of ordinary glazing quality and without embossing, silvering, lettering, bending or ornamental work of any kind [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-autolab-insurance-package-policy-wording-wic-clean.md#p23].

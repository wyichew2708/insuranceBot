## 6 What is Not Covered?

There are certain conditions under which no Benefits will be payable. These conditions are stated as exclusions. When any of the exclusion below happens, We will return the higher of either the total Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest, or the Surrender value (if any) less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-enrich-income.md#p7].

## General Exclusions

The Company will not indemnify the Insured in respect of loss, damage or liability directly or indirectly caused by or arising out of or aggravated by [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities (whether war be declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, mutiny, riot, strike, lock-out, civil commotion, military or usurped power, a group of malicious persons or persons acting on behalf of or in connection with any political organisation, conspiracy, confiscation, commandeering, requisition or destruction or damage by order of any government de jure or de facto or by any public authority; [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

nuclear reaction, nuclear radiation or radioactive contamination; wilful act or wilful negligence of the Insured or of his representatives; cessation of work whether total or partial [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

In any action, suit or other proceeding where the Company allege that by reason of the provisions of Exclusion a) above any loss, destruction, damage or liability is not covered by this insurance the burden of proving that such loss, destruction, damage or liability is covered shall be upon the Insured [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

## Special Exclusions To Section I

The Company shall not, however, be liable for [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

the deductible stated in the Schedule to be borne by the Insured in any one occurrence; consequential loss of any kind or description whatsoever including penalties, losses due to delay, lack of performance, loss of contract; loss or damage due to faulty design; [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

## Special Exclusions To Section II

The Company will not indemnify the Insured in respect of [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

the deductible stated in the Schedule to be borne by the Insured in any one occurrence; [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

the expenditure incurred in doing or redoing or making good or repairing or replacing anything covered or coverable under Section I of this Policy; [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

damage to any property or land or building caused by vibration or by the removal or weakening of support or injury or damage to any person or property occasioned by or resulting from any such damage (unless especially agreed upon by endorsement); [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

bodily injury to or illness of employees or workmen of the Contractor(s) or the Principal(s) or any other firm connected with the project which or part of which is insured under Section I, or members of their families; loss of or damage to property belong to or held in care, custody or control of the Contractor(s), the Principal(s) or any other firm connected with the project which or part of which is insured under Section I, or an employee or workman of one of the aforesaid; [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

any accident caused by vehicles licensed for general road use or by waterborne vessels or aircraft; any agreement by the Insured to pay any sum by way of indemnity or otherwise unless such liability would have attached also in the absence of such agreement [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md].

## SPECIAL EXCLUSIONS FOR WET RISKS

This Endorsement forms part of Section 1 and is subject otherwise to the terms, provisions, conditions and exclusions contained in the policy of insurance or endorsed thereon [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

## WEAPONS EXCLUSION CLAUSE

This clause shall be paramount and shall override anything contained in this insurance inconsistent therewith: [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

In no case shall this insurance cover loss damage liability or expense directly or indirectly caused by or contributed to by or arising from [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

ionising radiations from or contamination by radioactivity from any nuclear fuel or from any nuclear waste or from [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

the radioactive, toxic, explosive or other hazardous or contaminating properties of any nuclear installation, [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

reactor or other nuclear assembly or nuclear components thereof [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

any weapon or device employing atomic or nuclear fission and/or fusion or other like reaction or radioactive force [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

the radioactive, toxic, explosive or other hazardous or contaminating properties of any radioactive matter. The exclusion in this sub-clause does not extend to radioactive isotopes, other than nuclear fuel, when such isotopes are being prepared, carried, stored, or used for commercial, agricultural, medical, scientific or other similar peaceful purposes [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

any chemical, biological, bio-chemical or electromagnetic weapon [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

## TRANSMISSION & DISTRIBUTION LINES EXCLUSION CLAUSE

This policy does not cover all transmission and distribution, including wire, cables, poles, pylons, standard towers and any equipment of any type of which may be attendant to such installation of any description. This exclusion includes but not limited to transmission or distribution of electrical power, telephone or telegraph signal and all communication signals whether audio or visual [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

> This exclusion applies to both above and below ground equipment except that which is within 1,000 meters of the insured’s premises or as defined in the Schedule and not more than 11 kilovolts. [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8]

This exclusion applies both to physical or damage to the equipment and all business interruption, consequential loss and/or other contingent losses related to transmission and distribution lines [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

## Cyber Loss Absolute Exclusion Clause

1. Notwithstanding any provision to the contrary within this contract, this contract excludes any Cyber Loss. 2. Cyber Loss means any loss, damage, liability, expense, fines or penalties or any other amount directly or indirectly [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-contractors-all-risks-policy-wording.md#p8].

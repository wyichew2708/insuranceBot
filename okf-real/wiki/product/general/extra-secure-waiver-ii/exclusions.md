## 4 What is Not Covered?

There are certain conditions under which no Benefits will be payable. These conditions are stated as exclusions [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

## 4.1 Critical Illness (CI)

We will not pay any Benefit if the CI is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

- A Pre-existing condition; - Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; - Effects of drug or alcohol addiction; or - Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

## 4.2 Waiting Period

We will not pay any Benefit if: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

- Major Cancer, Heart Attack of Specified Severity, as well as Other Serious Coronary Artery Disease is diagnosed; or - The date of diagnosis of Coronary Artery disease leading to the performance of Coronary Artery By-Pass Surgery is; [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

> within ninety (90) days from the: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md]

- Rider issue date; - Rider commencement date; or - Latest Reinstatement date [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

When there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our Letter of Conditional Acceptance. When any of the exclusion happens, We will return the total Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under this rider) without interest, less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

## 6.7 Prohibited Person Limitation and Exclusion

If You are or any relevant person is found to be a Prohibited Person: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

- a) We are entitled not to accept the application; and - b) if any policy is issued, We are entitled to end/terminate the policy, not pay any Benefits or not allow any transaction to be carried out under the policy. We will not refund any unutilised Premium when the policy is ended/terminated [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

You will need to inform Us immediately if there is any change in Your or any relevant person's identity, status or identity documents [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

Our decision in respect of this exclusion will be final [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

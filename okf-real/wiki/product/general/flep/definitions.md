## General Definitions

Accident / Accidental means an unforeseen event of violent, external and visible nature, occurring during the Period of Insurance, which results in Bodily Injury and/or Sickness [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Activation Period means the period needed to incept your coverage [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Benefit means the respective Benefit, as stated in the Policy, Schedule and/or Endorsement payable by Us under the terms and conditions and exclusions in respect of each event or loss covered by this Policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Bodily Injury means damage caused to the body by an external force suffered during the period of insurance and which is caused by an Accident solely and independently of any other causes. This shall not include any medical conditions, diseases, or viral infections, even if these conditions resulted from, or are connected with the accident directly or indirectly [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Day Surgery means a surgery which is carried out by a surgeon but not on an inpatient basis [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Downgrade means a decrease in the level of Benefits and /or the removal of coverage of Your Policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Effective Date of Insurance means the inception date of insurance as specified on the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Excess Period means the period of time you have to be out of work due to Sickness or Bodily Injury before any Benefit will start accumulating and any claim payment will be made [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Family Member means the insured member's husband or wife, children, parents, siblings, parents-inlaw, brothers-in-law, sisters-in-law, grandparents, grandparent-in-law, daughters-in-law, sons-in-law or grandchildren [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Hospital means any institution in Singapore which fully meets all of the following criteria: [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

> - (a) maintains permanent and full-time facilities for care of overnight resident patients; and - (b) diagnostic and therapeutic facilities for surgical and medical diagnosis and treatment and care of injured and sick persons by or under the supervision of a staff of Medical Practitioners; and - (c) continuously provides twenty-four (24) hours a day nursing service supervised by qualified nurses; and - (d) is not, other than incidentally, a mental institution or a place for rest or a place for the aged, drug addicts or alcoholics. [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md]

> Inpatient means a day of which the Hospital makes a room and board charge for admission of a minimum period of twenty-four (24) hours on the recommendation of a registered Medical Practitioner. [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md]

Hospitalisation Leave means the period of hospitalisation as certified by the Medical Practitioner of a hospital [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Infectious Disease shall mean the definitive diagnosis of any of the following infectious diseases by a Medical Practitioner during the Period of Insurance and is supported by acceptable clinical, radiological, histological and laboratory evidence: [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

- 1) Hand, foot and mouth disease (HFMD) - 2) Dengue fever (DHF) [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

> - 3) Avian influenza or 'bird flu' due to influenza A viral strains H5N1, H9N2, H7N7, H7N9 or H1N1 - 4) Mumps - 5) Rubella - 6) Tuberculosis - 7) Measles - 8) Malaria - 9) Anthrax infection - 10) Yellow fever - 11) Plague - 12) Melioidosis or 'soil disease' - 13) Rabies - 14) Legionnaires' disease - 15) Chikungunya - 16) Nipah viral encephalitis - 17) Japanese viral encephalitis - 18) Variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (vCJD) or 'mad cow disease' - 19) Severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS) - 20) Middle-East respiratory syndrome coronavirus (MERS-CoV) - 21) Zika virus - 22) Group B Streptococcusin infection Type III GBS ST283 [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md]

Insured means the individual (or individuals) named in the schedule of lives as the person (or people) insured under this policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Medical Practitioner means a registered and legally qualified physician, doctor or surgeon by a medical degree in western medicine and authorised by the medical licensing authority in Singapore to provide medical or surgical services within the scope of their licence and training. This cannot be You, Your family member, relatives, business partner, employer, employee or agent [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Medically Necessary means a medical service which is: [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

- (a) consistent with the diagnosis and customary medical treatment for a covered disability; and - (b) in accordance with standards of good medical practices, consistent with current standard of professional medical care and of proven medical benefits; and - (c) not for the convenience of the Insured or the Medical Practitioner, and unable to be reasonably rendered out of a hospital (if admitted as an inpatient); and - (c) not of an experimental, investigational or research nature, preventive or screening nature [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

- (d) not an elective treatment or a matter of personal choice [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Own Account Workers refers to those workers who, working on their own account, hold the type of job defined as a 'self-employment job' and have not engaged on a continuous basis any 'employees' to work for them [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Outpatient means treatment by a registered medical practitioner and does not require a day admission or an overnight stay at a hospital [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Period of Insurance means the period of cover as shown in the schedule [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Policyholder means the owner of this policy named in the schedule [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

Pre-existing Medical Condition shall mean disabilities or conditions that Insured have reasonable knowledge of. Insured may be considered to have reasonable knowledge of a pre-existing medical condition if: [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

- (a) Insured had received or is receiving treatment; or - (b) medical advice, diagnosis, care or treatment has been recommended; or - (c) clear and distinct symptoms are or were evident; or - (d) its existence would have been apparent to a reasonable person in the circumstances [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

> Pre-existing medical conditions will be covered after 12 months of continuous cover in this policy. [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md]

Renewal Date means one (1) day after the end of the existing Period of Insurance. The Policy is renewable at Our discretion [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md].

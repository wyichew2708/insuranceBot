## General Definitions

Accident / Accidental means an unforeseen event of violent, external and visible nature, occurring during the Period of Insurance, which results in Bodily Injury and/or Sickness [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Activation Period means the period needed to incept your coverage [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Benefit means the respective Benefit, as stated in the Policy, Schedule and/or Endorsement payable by Us under the terms and conditions and exclusions in respect of each event or loss covered by this Policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Bodily Injury means damage caused to the body by an external force suffered during the period of insurance and which is caused by an Accident solely and independently of any other causes. This shall not include any medical conditions, diseases, or viral infections, even if these conditions resulted from, or are connected with the accident directly or indirectly [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Day Surgery means a surgery which is carried out by a surgeon but not on an inpatient basis [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Downgrade means a decrease in the level of Benefits and /or the removal of coverage of Your Policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Effective Date of Insurance means the inception date of insurance as specified on the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Excess Period means the period of time you have to be out of work due to Sickness or Bodily Injury before any Benefit will start accumulating and any claim payment will be made [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Family Member means the insured member’s husband or wife, children, parents, siblings, parents-in- law, brothers-in-law, sisters-in-law, grandparents, grandparent-in-law, daughters-in-law, sons-in-law or grandchildren [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Hospital means any institution in Singapore which fully meets all of the following criteria: [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

> maintains permanent and full-time facilities for care of overnight resident patients; and diagnostic and therapeutic facilities for surgical and medical diagnosis and treatment and care of injured and sick persons by or under the supervision of a staff of Medical Practitioners; and continuously provides twenty-four (24) hours a day nursing service supervised by qualified nurses; and is not, other than incidentally, a mental institution or a place for rest or a place for the aged, drug addicts or alcoholics. [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2]

> Inpatient means a day of which the Hospital makes a room and board charge for admission of a minimum period of twenty-four (24) hours on the recommendation of a registered Medical Practitioner. [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2]

Hospitalisation Leave means the period of hospitalisation as certified by the Medical Practitioner of a hospital [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Infectious Disease shall mean the definitive diagnosis of any of the following infectious diseases by a Medical Practitioner during the Period of Insurance and is supported by acceptable clinical, radiological, histological and laboratory evidence: 1) Hand, foot and mouth disease (HFMD) 2) Dengue fever (DHF) [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

> 3) Avian influenza or ‘bird flu’ due to influenza A viral strains H5N1, H9N2, H7N7, H7N9 or H1N1 4) Mumps 5) Rubella 6) Tuberculosis 7) Measles 8) Malaria 9) Anthrax infection 10) Yellow fever 11) Plague 12) Melioidosis or ‘soil disease’ 13) Rabies 14) Legionnaires’ disease 15) Chikungunya 16) Nipah viral encephalitis 17) Japanese viral encephalitis 18) Variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (vCJD) or ‘mad cow disease’ 19) Severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS) 20) Middle-East respiratory syndrome coronavirus (MERS-CoV) 21) Zika virus 22) Group B Streptococcusin infection Type III GBS ST283 [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2]

Insured means the individual (or individuals) named in the schedule of lives as the person (or people) insured under this policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

Medical Practitioner means a registered and legally qualified physician, doctor or surgeon by a medical degree in western medicine and authorised by the medical licensing authority in Singapore to provide medical or surgical services within the scope of their licence and training. This cannot be You, Your family member, relatives, business partner, employer, employee or agent [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-flep.md#p2].

## Exceptions

This policy does not provide indemnity against any claim or claims: [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-management-corporation-errors-and-ommission-policy-wording.md].

> 1. for libel or slander 2. based upon or attributable to any insured gaining any personal profit or advantage or receiving any remuneration to which they were not legally entitled 3. arising out of seepage pollution or contamination or the breach of any statute regulation or ordinance prohibiting or controlling emissions of effluents of any kind or from any enforcement action proceeding brought under or pursuant to any such statute regulation or ordinance 4. for bodily injury to or sickness, disease or death of any person or for loss or damage to any tangible property including the loss of use or any consequential loss 5. based upon or attributable to payments, commission, gratuities, benefit or any other favour to or for the benefit of any 6. (a) political group 7. (b) government or armed service official 8. (c) insured employee or any person having an ownership interest in any customer of the management corporation or their agent representative or member of their family or any other entity with which they are affiliated 6. brought about or contributed to by any dishonesty or fraud by any insured provided however that 10. (a) this policy shall provide as indemnity against all costs, charges and expenses reasonably incurred in successfully defending any action brought in respect of such claims by any third party 11. (b) for the purpose of this Exception, the dishonesty or fraud of any insured shall not be imputed to any other insured 7. based upon or attributable to any failure or omission on the part of the management corporation to effect and maintain adequate insurance 8. ensuing from any loss, destruction, consequential loss or legal liability of whatsoever nature caused by or contributed to either directly or indirectly by ionizing radiations or contamination by radioactivity from any nuclear fuel, weapon or waste 9. for pending or prior litigation or any development thereof 10. which the insured had notified as claims or circumstances which might give rise to a claim to any previous insurance [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-management-corporation-errors-and-ommission-policy-wording.md]

or for which indemnity is available under any policy [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-management-corporation-errors-and-ommission-policy-wording.md].

## General Definitions

You/Your/Insured means the Insured named in the Schedule. Us/We/Our means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Reg. No. 201331905K). Accident/Accidental means an event of violent, accidental, external and visible nature, occurring during the Period of Insurance, which shall independently of any other cause be the sole cause of Bodily Injury or death [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-personal-accident-with-rider-plan-policy-22032016.md#p2].

Benefit means the respective Benefit, as stated in the Policy, Schedule and/or Endorsement payable by Us under the terms, exclusions and conditions of this Policy in respect of each event or loss covered by this Policy. Bodily Injury means injury sustained by an Insured from an Accident [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-personal-accident-with-rider-plan-policy-22032016.md#p2].

> Effective Date of Insurance means the effective date on the Schedule. Each day of Hospital Confinement is defined as a day of which the Hospital makes a charge for room and board by You for admission as an in-patient for a minimum period of twenty-four (24) hours on the recommendation of a Medical Practitioner. [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-personal-accident-with-rider-plan-policy-22032016.md#p2]

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to this Policy. Family Member means an Insured’s spouse, parent, parent-in-law, grandparent, child, brother or sister [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-personal-accident-with-rider-plan-policy-22032016.md#p2].

## General Definitions

Accident / Accidental means an unforeseen event of violent, external and visible nature, occurring during the Period of Insurance, which results in Bodily Injury or death [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording.md#p2].

Benefit means the respective Benefit, as stated in the Policy, Schedule and/or Endorsement payable by Us under the terms and conditions and exclusions in respect of each event or loss covered by this Policy [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording.md#p2].

> Bodily Injury means injury sustained by an Insured during the period of insurance and is caused by an Accident solely and independently of any other causes within fifty-two (52) weeks from the date of such Accident. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording.md#p2]

Effective Date of Insurance means the commencement date of insurance as specified on the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording.md#p2].

Each day of Hospital Confinement is defined as a day of which the Hospital charges You for room [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording.md#p2].

> and board upon admission as an in-patient for a minimum period of twenty-four (24) hours upon on the recommendation of a Medical Practitioner. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording.md#p2]

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/tiq-personal-accident-policy-wording.md#p2].

## Eligibility

> To be eligible under this Insurance, You or Your Legal Spouse named in the Schedule must be a Singapore Citizen, or a permanent resident of Singapore, or a foreigner with valid Work Permit or Employment Pass or Dependant's Pass or Long-Term Visit Pass of at least seventeen (17) years of age and not more than seventy (70) years of age on the Effective Date of Insurance. The policy shall be renewed up to the maximum age of seventy-five (75) years subject to our satisfaction on Your state of health. [src:raw/wordings/tiq-pa-policy-wording-v2-280823.md]

> For Your child(ren) to be eligible under this Insurance with 20% of the sum insured/benefits under You or Your Legal Spouse's selected plan, the following conditions must be met: [src:raw/wordings/tiq-pa-policy-wording-v2-280823.md]

> - a) The child(ren) must be Your Legal Child; - b) The child(ren) must be at least one (1) year of age and not more than seventeen (17) years of age; or up to twenty-five (25) years for full time students in a recognised tertiary institution; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-pa-policy-wording-v2-280823.md]

- c) The child(ren) must be wholly dependent on You or Your Legal Spouse for financial support; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-pa-policy-wording-v2-280823.md].

- d) The child(ren) must be unmarried; and - e) The plan type of Your child(ren)'s coverage is (are) the same as Your or Your Legal Spouse's plan [src:raw/wordings/tiq-pa-policy-wording-v2-280823.md].

## ELIGIBILITY

The Policyholder must hold an in force GIGANTIQ policy. The Policyholder named in the Schedule must be a Singapore citizen, a permanent resident of Singapore or a foreigner with a valid Work Pass, Student Pass, Dependant’s Pass or Long-Term Visit Pass [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-cyber-insurance.md].

> The Policyholder named in the Schedule must be at least eighteen (18) years of age on Effective Date of Insurance. [src:raw/wordings/policy-wording-cyber-insurance.md]

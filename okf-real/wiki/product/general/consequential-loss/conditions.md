## Conditions

This policy shall be voidable in the event of misrepresentation, misdescription or non-disclosure of any material particulars [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-consequential-loss-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

No payment in respect of any premium shall be deemed to be payment to the company unless a printed form of receipt for the same signed by an official or duly appointed agent of the company shall have been given to the insured [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-consequential-loss-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

The insured shall give notice to the company of any insurance or insurances already effected or which may subsequently be effected, covering the subject matter of this insurance or any part thereof, and unless such notice be given and the particulars of such insurance or insurances be stated in or endorsed on this policy by or on behalf of the company before the occurrence of any loss, all benefit under this policy shall be forfeited [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-consequential-loss-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

Immediately upon any fall or displacement :- [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-consequential-loss-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

Of any building damage to which might give rise to a claim under this policy; Of any part of such building; Of the whole or any part of any range of buildings or of any structure of which such buildings forms part [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-consequential-loss-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

The insurance under this policy shall cease in respect of loss resulting from damage to such building or property therein [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-consequential-loss-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

If any claim whatsoever be made under this policy arising from damage whether occurring before, during or after such fall or displacement the insured shall produce such proof as may reasonably be required that the loss neither in origin nor extent, directly or indirectly, proximately or remotely, is occasioned by or contributed to by any fall or displacement as aforesaid, and neither in origin nor extent, directly or indirectly, proximately or remotely, arises out of or in connection with any such fall or displacement [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-consequential-loss-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

The fall or displacement as aforesaid is of the whole or a substantial or important part of such building or impairs the usefulness of such building or any part thereof or leaves such building or any part thereof or any property contained therein subject to increased risk of fire or is otherwise material; [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-consequential-loss-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

The fall or displacement is not caused by fire, loss resulting from destruction or damage by which is covered by this policy or would be covered if such building, range of buildings or structure were included in the premises to which this policy refers [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-consequential-loss-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

In any action, suit or other proceeding, the burden of proving that any fall or displacement is caused by fire as aforesaid shall be upon the insured [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-consequential-loss-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

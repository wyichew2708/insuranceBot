## About Etiqa Management Corporation Errors Ommission Insurance

## 1. PROSECUTION AND CRIMINAL PROCEEDING (LEGAL DEFENCE)

The company will indemnify the insured against all costs, charges and expenses and legal representation arising out of the prosecution (criminal or otherwise) of any insured. Provided always that: [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-management-corporation-errors-and-ommission-policy-wording.md].

- (a) this extension will only apply to prosecution arising out of a wrongful act committed by an insured whilst acting in their capacity as members of the council of the management corporation and notified to the company during the period of insurance - (b) the company will not be liable to pay any penalties, fines made against any insured - (c) the company will be entitled, if they so desired, to nominate a lawyer to represent the insured - (d) no costs charges and expenses other than those incurred with the written consent of the company shall be payable hereunder - (e) prosecution in respect of any wrongful act is conducted or notice of intended prosecution given during the period of insurance and is notified to the company during the period of insurance [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-management-corporation-errors-and-ommission-policy-wording.md].

## 2. DEATH INCOMPETENCY OR INSOLVENCY

The estate heirs legal representation or assigns of deceased incompetent or insolvent insured shall be indemnified in like manner to the insured provided they shall observe and be subject to all terms conditions and exceptions of this policy insofar as they can apply [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-management-corporation-errors-and-ommission-policy-wording.md].

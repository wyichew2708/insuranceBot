## 3 Our Responsibilities

This plan is a Non-participating limited premium payment term policy that provides financial protection against death, Total and permanently disability and Terminal illness for the period of the policy term. This plan does not have any cash values. At Expiry date, if We have not paid out any claims, the policy will end and no benefits will be payable. The Sum insured of this plan reduces on a monthly basis based on the selected interest rate and policy term from Policy commencement date onwards [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

> This plan is available for policy term of 6 to 40 years or up to Age 75, whichever is earlier. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md]

## 3.1 Covered Events

If Your policy is valid and a covered event happens, We will pay You the Benefits less any amount You owe Us on this policy and its supplementary rider contract (where applicable). The covered events of Your policy are written on the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

## 5 What is Covered?

Your policy covers the Benefits described below while the policy is inforce [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

## 5.1 Death Benefit

The Sum insured of this plan reduces on a monthly basis based on the selected interest rate and policy term from Policy commencement date onwards [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

Upon death of the Life insured, We will pay the reducing Sum insured less any amounts owing to Us. When We make this payment, the policy ends [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

> While We assess the death claims, if the claimant needs financial assistance to prepare the funeral, We may consider offering an immediate payment of up to S$3,000 per Life insured. This funeral expense financial aid, if given, will be deducted from the final death benefit to the claimant. Our advance payment of this financial aid does not mean We have approved the death claim. If the death claim is not approved later, after Our assessment, We have the right to ask You to return this advance payment. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md]

## 5.2 Total and Permanent Disability (TPD) Benefit

> If the Life insured becomes totally and permanently disabled on or before reaching the Policy anniversary when he attains Age 70 while the policy is in force, We will pay the death Benefit in one lump sum. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md]

> The maximum aggregate amount payable is Four Million Singapore Dollars (SGD 4,000,000) per Life insured for all policies and riders issued by Us with TPD Benefits. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md]

If the TPD Benefit payable is lesser than death Benefit, the death Benefit will be automatically reduced by the amount paid for the TPD. The policy remains in force and the remaining death Benefit is the original death Benefit less the amount paid for TPD [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

If the TPD Benefit payable is the same as the death Benefit, the policy ends when We make this payment [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

## 5.3 Terminal Illness Benefit

> If the Life insured has a Terminal illness on or before reaching the Policy anniversary when he attains Age 70 while the policy is in force, We will pay the death Benefit in one lump sum. When we make this payment, the policy ends. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md]

## 6.1 Death Benefit

We do not pay the death Benefit if the death is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

> - a) Life insured commits intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide within the first 12 months from the Policy issue date or the latest Reinstatement date, whichever is later. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md]

## 6.2 Total Permanent Disability (TPD) Benefit

We do not pay the TPD Benefit if the TPD is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

- a) Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; - b) Criminal acts, war (declared or not), terrorism and chemical warfare; - c) Participating in aviation (except as fare-paying passenger or member crew of a commercial airline), any dangerous or hazardous sport or hobby such as (but not limited to) steeple chasing, polo, horse racing, underwater diving, hunting, motor vehicular racing, mountaineering or potholing, or aerial sports such as skydiving, parachuting, bungee jumping; [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

- d) Effects of drug or alcohol addiction; or - e) Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS), AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

## 6.3 Terminal Illness Benefit

We do not pay the Terminal illness Benefit if the Terminal illness is directly or indirectly, wholly or partly caused by: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

- a) Intentional acts (sane or insane) such as self-inflicted injuries, suicide or attempted suicide; or [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

- b) AIDS, AIDS-related conditions or infection in the presence of HIV except HIV due to blood transfusion and occupationally acquired HIV [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

When there is condition(s) specific to the Life insured which We will not cover, We will state them on Our Letter of Conditional Acceptance. When any of the exclusion happens, We will return the total Premiums paid (less any amounts previously paid to You under this policy) without interest, less any amounts owing to Us [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-for-eprotect-mortgage-v1-23.md].

## To be eligible under this Policy

> (a) The Insured Person(s) and/or Policy Owner named in the Schedule must be a Singapore citizen, a permanent resident of Singapore or a foreigner with a valid Work Pass, Student Pass, Dependant’s Pass or Long-Term Visit Pass. (b) The Policy Owner named in the Schedule must be at least eighteen (18) years of age on the Effective Date of Insurance. [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md#p4]

(c) The Insured Person(s) and/or Policy Owner should not be from any country under Our Zone country exclusions [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md#p4].

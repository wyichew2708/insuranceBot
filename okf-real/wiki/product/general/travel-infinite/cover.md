## Family Plan

For Single Trip Policy - Maximum of two (2) adults traveling with eight (8) Children. The two (2) adults need not be related to the Child(ren). All must depart from and return to Singapore together at the same time [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

For Annual Multi-Trip Policy - The Insured Person(s) will comprise You, Your Spouse and any number of Your Children. The Child(ren) need not be accompanied by either of the parent for any trips made during the Period of Insurance [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Golf Equipment means golf clubs and golf bags, golf shoes and non-motorised golf trolley [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

- (a) A Policy issued to You in respect of the Insured Persons named in the Schedule who are travelling as a group, that is not issued under a Family Plan. - (b) The Insured Persons under the Group Plan must travel together on a Journey [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

- (c) Group Plan is only available under Single Trip Policy for Classic Plan, Deluxe Plan or Suite Plan [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

- (a) Flying (including hot-air ballooning, hang-gliding, gliding and micro-lighting) other than as a [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

fare- paying passenger in a licensed passenger aircraft; [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

- (b) Equestrian activities; - (c) Hunting or shooting; - (d) Martial arts, boxing, wrestling or judo; - (e) Motor sports, rallies or competitions; - (f) Motorcycling (including motor tri-cycling and motor quadri-cycling) whether as the rider or as a passenger) - i) On a public highway unless You are wearing a crash helmet and the rider has the appropriate license to do so; [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

- ii) Not on a public highway; - (g) Mountaineering, abseiling or rock climbing requiring the use of ropes and/or guides; - (h) Organised team football (including American, Australian and Association football), ice hockey, hockey, lacrosse, hurling, shinty or rugby; [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

> - (n) Rafting, canoeing or kayaking in white-water rapids; - (o) Any form of swimming at a depth of 30 metres or more; - (p) Any form of swimming using breathing apparatus other than a snorkel unless You are a qualified diver and accompanied by a fellow diver or You are unqualified but accompanied by a qualified instructor; [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md]

- (q) Water-skiing; - (r) Winter sports; - (s) Yachting [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Hijack means an illegal seize of an aircraft, ship or vehicle while in transit and force it to go to a different destination or use it for the hijacker's own purposes [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Hospital means any institution which fully meets all of the following criteria: [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

- (a) Maintains permanent and full-time facilities for care of overnight resident patients; - (b) Has diagnostic and therapeutic facilities for surgical and medical diagnosis and treatment and care of injured and sick persons by or under the supervision of a staff of Medical Practitioners; [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

> - (c) Continuously provides twenty-four (24) hours a day nursing service supervised by qualified nurses, and - (d) Is not other than incidentally a mental institution or a place for rest or a place for the aged or for drug addicts or for alcoholics. [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md]

Hospital Confinement means an admission or a series of admissions from the same cause except that if admissions from the same cause are separated by twelve [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

> (12) months or more then each such admission shall constitute a separate Hospital Confinement. [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md]

Hospitality Expenses means customary food and beverages purchased at the golf club house for the purpose of celebrating the hole-in-one scored by You [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Hostage means a person taken by force to secure the taker's demand [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Household Contents means household furniture and furnishing, clothing and personal effects belonging to You or to members of Your family or domestic servants permanently residing with You and fixtures and fittings which You own (or for which You are responsible) not being landlord's fixtures and fittings and excluding deeds, bonds, bills of exchange, promissory notes, cheques, travellers' cheques, securities for money, documents of any kind, cash, currency notes [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Illness means any sudden and unexpected deterioration in health which manifests itself while Overseas during the Period of Insurance and is not caused by bodily Injury [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Individual Plan means a Policy issued to the Insured in respect of You as the Insured Person named in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Injury means physical bodily injury to You as a result of an Accident during Your Trip and occurring solely, directly and independently of any other cause [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Insolvency means the inability of an individual or entity to pay its debt resulting in the total cessation of their operations due to either: [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

- (a) Insolvency, with or without filing of a bankruptcy or similar petition; or - (b) Abscondment with monies belonging to the organisation by an owner or employee who has prior convictions of any fraudulent or dishonest act, or is under investigation on a charge of fraudulent or dishonest act [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Insured Person(s) in respect of an Individual Plan means the person(s) named in the Policy Schedule as the Insured(s); in respect of a Family Plan means the person(s) stated in the Family Plan and Group Plan [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Jewellery means objects such as rings, bracelets, brooches, necklaces, bangles, ear rings, lockets that are worn on the body as decoration which have inclusions of precious metals i.e. gold and silver or precious or semi-precious stones [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Journey means the period of travel which commences when the Insured Person completes the immigration departure clearance procedure at the Usual Country of Residence on or after the departure date specified in the Schedule for the purpose of commencing such journey and ends (a) on the last day specified in the Schedule or (b) when the Insured Person completes the immigration arrival clearance procedure for returning to the Usual Country of Residence after such journey, whichever is earlier [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Schedule for the purpose of commencing such journey and ends (a) on the last day specified in the Schedule or (b) when the Insured Person completes the immigration arrival clearance procedure for returning to the Usual Country of Residence after such journey, whichever is earlier [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Kidnap means to seize and detain or carry away by unlawful force for fraud and often with a demand for ransom [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Laptop Computer means the complete Laptop Computer including accessories or attachments that come as standard equipment with the laptop. Licensed Traditional Chinese Medical Practitioner (including a Chinese acupuncturist or bonesetter) means duly registered and practicing within the scope of his license pursuant to the laws of the country in which such practice is maintained, excluding You or any of Your relatives unless otherwise approved by Us [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

> Loss of Hearing means permanent irrecoverable loss of hearing where: total loss of hearing in one or both ears to the extent that the hearing loss in one or both ears is greater than ninety-five (95) decibels across all frequencies using a pure tone audiogram that has lasted fifty-two (52) consecutive weeks and that in the opinion of an expert Medical Practitioner will not be recovered. [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md]

> - (a) The disability lasts for twelve (12) months consecutively from the date of the Accident; and - (b) Our Medical Practitioner confirms that it is not going to improve after twelve (12) months. [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md]

Personal Property and Baggage means personal goods belonging to You which are taken on the Journey or acquired by You and carried on Your person or hand- carried or check-in as accompanied baggage with the carrier during the Journey [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Pre-existing Condition means any medical condition for which: [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

> - (a) The Insured Person(s) received medical treatment, diagnosis, consultation or prescribed drugs within a twelve (12) month period immediately prior to the Effective Date of Insurance; or - (b) Medical advice or treatment was recommended by a Medical Practitioner within a twelve (12) month period immediately prior to the Effective Date of Insurance; or - (c) A reasonable person in the circumstances would be expected to be aware of within a twelve (12) month period immediately prior to the Effective Date of Insurance. [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md]

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

- (i) Subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - (ii) Who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Policyholder / Policy Owner refers to the named owner of this Policy as shown in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

Public Conveyance means any land, water or air conveyance operating under a license in the country the Insured Person(s) is in for the transportation of fare paying passengers and which has fixed, established and regular schedules and routes only [src:raw/wordings/travel-infinite-policy-wording.md].

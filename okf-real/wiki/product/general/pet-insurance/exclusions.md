## In addition to the General Exclusions, We will not pay for

1. Hereditary and congenital Conditions (unless specified under Free Policy Extensions 3); [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p6].

2. Sickness caused by endoparasites (such as worms) or ectoparasites (such as ticks, fleas, skin mites); [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p6].

3. Sickness relating to hip dysplasia; [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p6].

5. Applicable to dogs - treatment of infectious canine hepatitis (canine adenovirus), parvovirus; canine [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p6].

distemper; parainfluenza and all forms of kennel cough; [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p6].

6. Applicable to cats – treatment of panleukopaenia, chlamydia, leukaemia (FeLV), feline immuno deficiency virus (FIV), feline infectious peritonitis (FIP), viral rhinotracheitis, calicivirus, herpes virus and all forms of cat flu; [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p6].

7. Any declared pandemic disease that causes widespread sickness affecting dogs or cats; or [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p6].

8. Dental care - dental procedures, dental disease, gingivitis, treatment of teeth fractures, teeth cleaning/scaling, orthodontics, cosmetic dental restoration, temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disease, enamel hypolasia; teeth hygiene or appearance, removal of deciduous or fractured teeth or any oral disease [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p6].

## In addition to the General Exclusions, We will not pay for

1. Damage to teeth and gums unless accompanied by Accidental Bodily Injury arising from the same [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p8].

2. More than one (1) incident of swallowing a foreign object that causes a blockage or obstruction requiring [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p8].

surgical or endoscopic removal per Period of Insurance; or [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p8].

resulting from a confirmed snake bite) and inter-vertebral disc disease (IVDD) [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording.md#p8].

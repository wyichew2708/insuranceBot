## 11. What Do We Mean With These Words?

Age means the age at next birthday [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Application means the proposal form, any amendments, forms and documents provided by You and the Life insured [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Assignee means any person to whom You have transferred Your rights as the Policy Owner and the assignment is registered with Us [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Basic policy means the D a s h P E T policy as it exists, including the supplementary terms and any Endorsement made to it, without any optional supplementary contract / rider [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Benefit(s) means any payments that We will pay and/or the amount of Premium that We will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Doctor means a licensed person who is qualified by degree in western medicine to practice medicine. The license is given by the appropriate medical authority of his country of residence to practice medicine within his scope of licensing and training. This cannot be You, the Life insured, a family member or a relative [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by Us [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Fund Factsheet refers to a product document that show the performance of the Portfolio fund [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Fund manager refers to UOB Asset Management Ltd who manages the Portfolio fund [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Fund Summary refers to a product document that contains the list of Portfolio fund as well as their ILP sub-funds and allocation [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Group Policy Owner is SingCash Pte. Ltd. (Company Registration No. 201106360E). The Group Policy Owner has full rights on the rider [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

ILP refers to Investment-linked insurance plan [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

ILP sub-fund manager refers to the asset management company that manages the respective ILP sub-funds [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Life insured means the person whom We provide the cover for. The Life insured does not have any right to the rider, unless he/she is also the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Maturity date is the date the rider ends and where a maturity Benefit is payable [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

> Nominee(s) is a person that You have nominated (under the Insurance Act 1966 and Insurance ( Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009 ) to receive the policy monies payable under the rider upon Your death. The nomination must be registered with Us. [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md]

Portfolio fund refers to the list of ILP sub-funds in the rider in which the Premiums are allocated [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Policy month is the 1-month period that starts on the Rider commencement date or any subsequent monthiversary of the Rider commencement date [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Endorsement issued by Us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the rider has been transferred to another party [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Policy year / Policy anniversary is the 1-year period that starts on the Rider commencement date or any subsequent anniversary of the Rider commencement date [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Pre-existing condition means the existence of any signs or symptoms before the Rider commencement date for which treatment, medication, consultation, advice, or diagnosis has been sought or received by the Life insured or would have caused any reasonable and sensible person to get medical advice or treatment [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Premium(s) is an amount that You pay to Us to keep this rider in force [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

- a) subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict Us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - b) who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act 1966 . It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own Benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased. [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md]

Proposal form means the form You signed to buy this rider from Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. It includes anything written, said (and recorded) and/or any document given to Us which has information for Us to rely on before We decide to issue this rider [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Rider account value is the value of all the units in Your rider less any applicable fees and charges [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Rider commencement date means the date the rider commences, as shown in the Endorsement [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Rider issue date means the date We issue the rider. This is shown in the Endorsement [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

> Terminal illness is the conclusive diagnosis of an illness that is expected to result in the death of the Life insured within twelve (12) months. This diagnosis must be supported by a specialist and confirmed by the Company's appointed Doctor. Terminal illness in the presence of HIV infection is excluded. [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md]

Top-up(s) means the subsequent premium(s) You make to Your rider after Your rider is issued [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

Valuation day means a business day when We carry out a valuation to determine the price of a unit of the ILP sub-funds under this rider respectively [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

We, Our, Us, the Company means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Registration No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

You, Your means the Life insured [src:raw/wordings/dash-pet-plus-policy-contract-v3-26-02042026.md].

## General Definitions

Accident / Accidental means an unforeseen event of violent, external and visible nature, occurring during the Period of Insurance [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

> Accidental Death means death of Your Pet occurring as a direct result of an Accident and the death occurs within ninety (90) days of the Accident. [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md]

Act of God means an event outside of human control caused exclusively by flood, earthquake, hurricane, typhoon or windstorm, whose effects could not be prevented by the exercise of reasonable care and foresight [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Benefit means the respective Benefit, as stated in the Policy, Schedule and/or Endorsement payable by Us under the terms and conditions and exclusions in respect of each event or loss covered by this Policy [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

> Bodily Injury means injury sustained by Your Pet during the Period of Insurance and is caused by an Accident solely and independently of any other causes within fifty-two (52) weeks from the date of such Accident. [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md]

Clinical Examination means an examination performed by a Veterinarian involving physical examinations and comprehensive blood tests (if applicable) on Your Pet [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Clinical Symptom(s) means any manifested anomalies in, or deviation from the regular healthy state or function of Your Pet, including behavioural traits. This includes any anomaly that is readily detectible by a thorough and complete clinical examination by a Veterinarian [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Co-insurance means the percentage portion of the claim amount You need to pay Us after the Deductible is applied. This is subject to Goods &amp; Services Tax (GST) if applicable [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Condition(s) means any manifestations of Clinical Symptoms consistent with a diagnosis or diagnoses, regardless of the number of incidents or areas of the body affected [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Deductible means the first portion of any claim for which You are liable to pay before the Co-insurance has been applied. This is subject to Goods &amp; Services Tax (GST) if applicable [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Diagnostic Test(s) means tests used to determine the overall health of Your Pet, which can be used as a way to detect certain abnormalities. It can also validate the current health of Your Pet, or help to evaluate older pets more thoroughly for potential health problems [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Effective Date of Insurance means the commencement date of insurance as specified on the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Known Events means any situation or incident which threatens or affects Your Pet's health or any situation that You were aware of or could reasonably have been expected to know before You applied for cover under this Policy [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Medically Necessary means medical services provided by a Veterinarian to treat Your Pet which are: [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

- a) consistent with the diagnosis and symptoms; - b) appropriate and in accordance with generally accepted veterinary practice standards; - c) not for the convenience of You, Your Veterinarian or other providers; - d) not of an experimental, investigational or research nature, preventive or screening nature; [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

- e) consistent with the most appropriate supply or level of services which can safely be provided to Your Pet; and - f) for which the charges are fair, reasonable and customary [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Neutering/Neutered means orchidectomy or surgical removal of both testicles [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Period of Insurance means the period starting from the Effective Date of Insurance during which the coverage under this Policy is effective [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Pet means the microchipped domestic cat or dog named in the Schedule who is insured under this Policy and is owned for companionship [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Policy means this insurance policy issued by Us, including any information provided or declaration made by You, the Schedule, the Table of Benefits and any Endorsements We have issued [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Pre-Existing Medical Condition shall mean disabilities or conditions that You have reasonable knowledge of. You may be considered to have reasonable knowledge of a pre-existing condition if: [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

- a) Your Pet has received or is receiving treatment; or - b) medical advice, diagnosis, care or treatment has been recommended; or - c) clear and distinct symptoms are or were evident; or - d) its existence would have been reasonably apparent under the circumstances [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

- (i) Subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - (ii) Who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Schedule means the information page that contains the details of Your Pet, Benefit, premium and Period of Insurance attached to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

Sickness means any disease or worsening physical health not caused by an Accident, any changes to Your Pet's normal healthy state, any condition other than Your Pet's normal healthy state, for which Your Pet requires treatment by a Veterinarian [src:raw/wordings/pet-insurance-policy-wording-v1-25-january-2024-final.md].

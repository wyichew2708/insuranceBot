## What Do We Mean With These Words?

This rider will end when any of the following events happens first: [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

> - a) Termination of the Basic policy; - b) Premium is not paid on time; - c) After We give You notice that the rider cannot be renewed; - d) We paid out 100% of the rider Benefit subject to the maximum aggregate amount; [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2]

- e) Upon expiry of this rider; or - f) Your written request and Our acceptance of the application to terminate the rider. If You write to Us to terminate Your rider, there will not be any prorated refund of Premium and Your rider will terminate from the Premium due date immediately following the date We accept Your written request for termination [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

## 10. What Do We Mean With These Words?

Age means the Age at next birthday [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Assignee means any person to whom You have transferred Your rights as the Policy o wner and the assignment is registered with Us [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Basic policy means the policy as it exists, including the supplementary terms and any Endorsement made to it, without any optional supplementary Benefit / rider [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Benefit(s) means any payments that We will pay and/or the amount of Premium that We will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Doctor means a licensed person who is qualified by degree in western medicine to practice medicine. The license is given by the appropriate medical authority of his country of residence to practice medicine within his scope of licensing and training. This cannot be the Life insured, a family member or a relative [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by Us [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Expiry date means the date the policy ends and where no Benefit is payable [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Life insured means the person whom We provide the cover for. The Life insured does not have any right to the policy, unless he/she is also the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

> Nominee(s) is a person that You have nominated (under the Insurance Act 1966 and Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009 ) to receive the policy monies payable under the policy upon Your death. The nomination must be registered with Us. [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2]

Non-participating means it does not share in any surplus or profits of the Company's fund [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Policy commencement date means the date the policy commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Policy issue date means the date We issue the policy. This is shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Policy Information Page or any Endorsement issued by Us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the policy has been transferred to another party [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Pre-existing condition means the existence of any signs or symptoms before the Policy issue date, the last increase in policy's Sum insured made, whichever is later, for which treatment, medication, consultation, advice, or diagnosis has been sought or received by the Life insured or would have caused any reasonable and sensible person to get medical advice or treatment [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Premium(s) is the amount of money that You pay to Us to keep this policy in force [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

- a) subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict Us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - b) who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/heart-and-neurological-disorder-rider-policy-contract-v1-23.md#p2].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act 1966 . It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own Benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased.

## GENERAL DEFINITIONS

Accident means a sudden, unforeseen and fortuitous event, and Accidental shall have a corresponding meaning [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Benefit means the respective benefit, as stated in the Policy Schedule, payable by the Company under the terms and conditions of this Policy in respect of each event or loss covered by this Policy [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Bodily Injury means injury sustained by a Policyholder or Dependant resulting solely, directly and independently of all other causes from an Accident [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Civil Commotion means a disturbance, commotion or disorder created by civilians usually against a governing body or the policies thereof [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Doctor / Medical Practitioner means a legally registered medical practitioner or surgeon and includes a Chinese acupuncturist, bonesetter, chiropractor or physiotherapist who is licensed and practising within the scope of his license pursuant to the laws of the country in which such practice is maintained. Doctor shall not include the Policyholders or any of their relatives unless otherwise approved by the Company [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Excess an Excess is the first amount of any covered loss or expense incurred by the Policyholder which shall be borne solely by the Policyholder. The Company shall only be liable to pay the remaining amount of such loss or expense incurred after deducting the Excess amount. In the event that the loss or expense incurred by the Policyholder is less than or equal to the amount stated as Excess in the policy, the Company will have no liabilities whatsoever [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Inception Date means the date this Policy first comes into force, as specified in the Policy Schedule [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Loss of Limb means loss by complete physical severance of a hand at or above the wrist or of a foot at or above the ankle [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Loss of Sight means total and irrecoverable loss of all sight in any eye rendering the Policyholder absolutely blind in that eye and beyond remedy by surgical or other treatment [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Loss of Speech and Hearing means total and irrecoverable loss of speech and hearing which is beyond remedy by surgical or other treatment [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Loss of Use means loss in terms of physical incapacity or disability in all aspects of daily living and not only in terms of professional or occupational incapacity or disability of the Policyholder [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Medical Expenses means Usual, Reasonable and Customary Medical Expenses necessarily and reasonably incurred in the medical or surgical treatment of Bodily Injury covered by this Policy [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

Period of Insurance means five (5) years from the Inception Date [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

> Permanent Disablement means disablement that results solely, directly and independently of all other causes from Bodily Injury and which occurs within three hundred and sixty-five (365) consecutive days of the Accident in which Bodily Injury was sustained, and: [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2]

> falls into one of the categories listed in the Policy Schedule; or is a disablement which, having lasted for a continuous and uninterrupted period of at least three hundred and sixty-five {365) consecutive days, is at the expiry of that period, beyond hope of improvement. [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2]

> Permanent Total Disablement means disablement that results solely, directly and independently of all other causes from Bodily Injury and which occurs within three hundred and sixty-five (365) consecutive days of the Accident in which Bodily Injury was sustained which, having lasted for a continuous and uninterrupted period of at least three hundred and sixty-five (365) consecutive days, will in all probability entirely prevent the Policyholder from engaging in employment of any and every kind for the remainder of his life and from which there is no hope of improvement. [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2]

Policy Schedule means the Policy Schedule which is attached to, incorporated in and forms part of this Policy [src:raw/wordings/policywordings-eprotect-family.md#p2].

## Conditions

This Policy and the Schedule shall be read together as one contract and any word or expression to which a specific meaning has been attached in any part of this Policy or of the Schedule shall bear such specific meaning wherever it may appear [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-fidelity-guarantee-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

## CANCELLATION

The Company may cancel this Policy by sending seven days notice by registered letter to the Insured at its last known address and in such event the Insured shall become entitled to the return of a proportionate part of the premium corresponding to the unexpired Period of Insurance. The Policy may be cancelled at any time by the Insured by giving seven days’ notice to the company and provided no claim has arisen during the then current period of insurance the Insured shall be entitled to a return of premium subject to the Company’s short period rates for the period the Policy has been in force [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-fidelity-guarantee-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

## ARBITRATION

If any difference shall arise as to the amount to be paid under this Policy (liability being otherwise admitted) such difference shall be referred to an arbitrator to be appointed in accordance with the statutory provisions on arbitration for the time being in force [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-fidelity-guarantee-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

Where any difference is by this condition to be referred to arbitration the making of an award shall be a condition precedent to any right of action against the Company [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-fidelity-guarantee-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

Unless any such action or suit be commenced within six months of the making of an award the Company shall not be liable to make any payment in excess of the amount of the award [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-fidelity-guarantee-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

## COMMENCEMENT OF ARBITRATION OR COURT ACTION

If the Company shall offer an amount in settlement or disclaim liability for any claim under this Policy and such claim shall not within twelve calendar months from the date of such offer or disclaimer have been referred to arbitration under the provision contained in the Policy or where liability is in dispute, been made subject to pending court action then the claim shall for all purposes be deemed to have been abandoned and shall not then be recoverable under this policy [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-fidelity-guarantee-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

## It shall be a condition precedent to the Company’s liability under this policy that 

the Insured has complied with all the terms of the policy; and the statements and answers in the : Proposal; and (i) any claim form; and (ii) (iii) any notice of change in circumstance or given separately in support of any of them (whether by the insured or any other person), are true and correct; and if the Policy covers the interest of any party other than the Insured, that party also complies with the same terms and conditions of the Policy where applicable [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-fidelity-guarantee-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

## SUBROGATION

After the company has accepted a claim either in whole or in part, the Company shall be fully subrogated to the position of the Insured and shall be able to exercise for its own benefit any legal right of recovery held by the Insured. The Insured must, at the Company’s expense, fully co-operate in the enforcement of this right by the Company [src:raw/wordings/etiqa-fidelity-guarantee-policy-wording-2023-02.md].

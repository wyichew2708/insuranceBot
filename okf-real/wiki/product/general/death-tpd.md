## Death Benefit

Upon death of the Life insured while the rider is in force, We will pay the Sum insured less any amounts owing to Us. When We make this payment, the rider ends [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md].

## Total and Permanent Disability (TPD) Benefit

> If the Life insured becomes totally and permanently disabled before reaching Age 66 while the rider is in force, We will pay the Sum insured less any amounts owing to Us. When We make this payment, the rider ends. [src:raw/product-summaries/product-summary-death-tpd-26-oct-2020-clean.md]

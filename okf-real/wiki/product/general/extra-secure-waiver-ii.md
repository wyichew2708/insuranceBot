## About Extra Secure Waiver II

## 2 Our Responsibilities

This is a regular Premium Non-participating rider that waives the Premium of the Basic policy and any attaching riders if the Life insured is diagnosed with a covered severe-stage critical illness. At the rider Expiry date, if We have not paid out any claims, this rider will end and no Benefits will be payable [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

This rider covers the Benefits described below [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

This Policy Contract should be read together with the Policy Contract of the Basic policy [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

## 2.1 Critical Illness (CI) Premium Waiver Benefit

> This rider allows You to continue the Basic policy and any attaching riders without paying Premiums from the next Premium due date following the Life insured's diagnosis of a severe-stage CI for the first time during the policy term. The Premium will be waived up to earlier of the Policy anniversary before the Life insured turns Age 86 or the end of the Premium payment term of Your policy. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md]

> The 37 covered Critical Illnesses are listed below: [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md]

> The maximum aggregate amount payable is Two Million Singapore Dollars (S$2,000,000) per Life insured for all policies and riders issued by Us with CI Benefits. [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md]

Please refer to Appendix A for the definitions of the covered critical illnesses [src:raw/wordings/policy-contract-extra-secure-waiver-ii-t.md].

## General Definitions

Accident/Accidental means an unforeseen event of violent, external and visible nature, occurring during the Period of Insurance, which results in Bodily Injury and/or sickness [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Activation Period means the period needed to incept your policy coverage [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Authorized Representative / Intermediary refers to GIGACOVER Pte Ltd [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Benefit means the respective Benefit, as stated in the Policy, Schedule and/or Endorsement payable by Us under the terms and conditions and exclusions in respect of each event or loss covered by this Policy [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Bicycle means a non-motorised pedal bicycle or tricycle to be used by one person [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Bodily Injury means injury sustained by an Insured during the Period of Insurance and is caused by an Accident solely and independently of any other causes [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Downgrade means a decrease in the level of benefits and / or the removal of coverage of your Policy [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

> E-Scooter means a wheeled vehicle that is not a 'non -compliant personal mobility device' under the Active Mobility Act 2017 that - [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md]

- (a) is built to transport people only (with or without carry -on baggage); and - (b) is propelled by an electric motor attached to the vehicle or by human power or both, but does not include a bicycle, power -assisted bicycle, motorcycle or skateboard [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Excess Period is the period of time you have to be out of work due to illness or injury before any benefit will start accumulating and any claim payment will be made [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Hospital means any institution in Singapore which fully meets all of the following criteria: [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

> - (c) maintains permanent and full-time facilities for care of overnight resident patients; and - (d) diagnostic and therapeutic facilities for surgical and medical diagnosis and treatment and care of injured and sick persons by or under the supervision of a staff of Medical Practitioners; and - (e) continuously provides 24 hours a day nursing service supervised by qualified nurses; and - (f) is not, other than incidentally, a mental institution or a place for rest or a place for the aged, drug addicts or alcoholics. [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md]

> Hospital Confinement is defined as a day of which the Hospital charges You for admission as an inpatient for a minimum period of twenty-four(24) hours upon on the recommendation of a Medical Practitioner. [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md]

Medical Certificate means a document issued by a Medical Practitioner in Singapore verifying your sickness and/or injury and thus unfit to perform work [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Medical Practitioner means a registered and legally qualified physician, doctor or surgeon by a medical degree in western medicine and authorised by the medical licensing authority in Singapore to provide medical or surgical services within the scope of their licence and training. This cannot be You, Your family member, relatives, business partner, employer, employee or agent [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Medically Necessary means a medical service which is: [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- (a) consistent with the diagnosis and customary medical treatment for a covered disability; and - (b) in accordance with standards of good medical practice, consistent with current standard of professional medical care and of proven medical benefits ; and [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- (c) not for the convenience of the Insured or the physician, and unable to be reasonably rendered out of hospital (if admitted as an inpatient); and - (d) not of an experimental, investigational or research nature, preventive or screening nature [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

No Claim Discount (NCD) refers to premium discount for the next Period of Insurance (POI) if no claim has occurred. NCD is only eligible to weekly and monthly policies. You are entitled for NCD provided the following conditions are met: [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- (a) Your policy is renewed in four (4) consecutive weeks, or - (b) Your policy is renewed in one (1) consecutive month, and - (c) No claim has been made under this Policy prior to the Renewal Date [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Own Account Workers refers to those workers who, working on their own account, hold the type of job defined as a 'self-employment job' and have not engaged on a continuous basis any 'employees' to work for them [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- (a) If you are paying a weekly premium, one (1) week from the Commencement Date or the latest Renewal Date whichever is later; - (b) If you are paying a monthly premium , one (1) month from the Commencement Date or the latest Renewal Date whichever is later; [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- (c) If you are paying annual premium, one (1) year from the Commencement Date or the latest Renewal Date whichever is later [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Pre-existing Illness shall mean disabilities or conditions that You have reasonable knowledge of. You may be considered to have reasonable knowledge of a pre-existing condition if: [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- (a) You had received or is receiving treatment; or - (b) medical advice, diagnosis, care or treatment has been recommended; or - (c) clear and distinct symptoms are or were evident; or - (d) its existence would have been apparent to a reasonable person in the circumstances [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Renewal Date means one (1) day after the end of the Period of Insurance. The Policy is renewable at Our discretion [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Schedule means the information page that contains the details of the Insured, Benefit, Premium and Period of Insurance attached to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Self-Employed Persons (SEP) refers to workers whom operate their own trade or business. They generally have significant autonomy over what assignments to accept and how, when and where they work. They are sometimes known loosely as 'Freelancers' or 'Independent Contractors'. More formally, they are known as 'Own Account Workers' [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Sickness shall mean illness or disease contracted and commencing whilst this Policy is in force [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Upgrade shall mean an increase in a level of benefit and/or the addition of coverage of Your Policy. It is subject to five (5) days Activation Period for the benefit upgrade from the last day of Your existing policy. There will be no change to the existing coverage during the activation period and a prorated premium may be chargeable. Any Upgrade is also subject to Pre-existing illness exclusion [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

> Upgrade / Downgrade Effective Date shall mean 12.01am Singapore Time on the date We agree to provide an Upgrade/Downgrade of Your Policy and which in Your Policy Schedule recording that Upgrade or Downgrade, as the context might require. [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md]

Us/We/Our means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Reg. No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

You/Your/Insured means the Insured named in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md].

## General Definitions

Accident/Accidental means an unforeseen event of violent, external and visible nature, occurring during the Period of Insurance, which results in Bodily Injury and/or sickness [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

Activation Period means the period needed to incept your policy coverage [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

Authorized Representative / Intermediary refers to GIGACOVER Pte Ltd [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

Benefit means the respective Benefit, as stated in the Policy, Schedule and/or Endorsement payable by Us under the terms and conditions and exclusions in respect of each event or loss covered by this Policy [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

Bicycle means a non-motorised pedal bicycle or tricycle to be used by one person [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

Bodily Injury means injury sustained by an Insured during the Period of Insurance and is caused by an Accident solely and independently of any other causes [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

Downgrade means a decrease in the level of benefits and / or the removal of coverage of your Policy [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

> E-Scooter means a wheeled vehicle that is not a “non‑compliant personal mobility device” under the Active Mobility Act 2017 that — [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2]

is built to transport people only (with or without carry‑on baggage); and is propelled by an electric motor attached to the vehicle or by human power or both, but does not include a bicycle, power‑assisted bicycle, motorcycle or skateboard [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to this Policy [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

Excess Period is the period of time you have to be out of work due to illness or injury before any benefit will start accumulating and any claim payment will be made [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

Hospital means any institution in Singapore which fully meets all of the following criteria: [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

(c) maintains permanent and full-time facilities for care of overnight resident patients; [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

> and diagnostic and therapeutic facilities for surgical and medical diagnosis and treatment and care of injured and sick persons by or under the supervision of a staff of Medical Practitioners; and continuously provides 24 hours a day nursing service supervised by qualified nurses; [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2]

and is not, other than incidentally, a mental institution or a place for rest or a place for the aged, drug addicts or alcoholics [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

> Hospital Confinement is defined as a day of which the Hospital charges You for admission as an in- patient for a minimum period of twenty-four(24) hours upon on the recommendation of a Medical Practitioner. [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2]

Medical Certificate means a document issued by a Medical Practitioner in Singapore verifying your sickness and/or injury and thus unfit to perform work [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

Medical Practitioner means a registered and legally qualified physician, doctor or surgeon by a medical degree in western medicine and authorised by the medical licensing authority in Singapore to provide medical or surgical services within the scope of their licence and training. This cannot be You, Your family member, relatives, business partner, employer, employee or agent [src:raw/wordings/gigacover-flip-insurance-policy-wording.md#p2].

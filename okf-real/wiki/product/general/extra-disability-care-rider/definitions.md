## 10 What Do We Mean With These Words?

Basic policy means the policy as it exists, including the supplementary terms and any Endorsement made to it, without any optional supplementary contract / rider [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

Benefit(s) means any payments that We will pay and/or the amount of Premium that We will waive when certain events defined in this policy occur [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

Doctor means a licensed person who is qualified by degree in western medicine to practice medicine. The license is given by the appropriate medical authority of his country of residence to practice medicine within his scope of licensing and training. This cannot be You, the Life insured, a family member or a relative [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

Endorsement means any written change to the policy which is issued and properly authorised by Us [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

Expiry date means the date the rider ends and where no benefit is payable [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

Hospital means a legally constituted establishment which: [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

- is licensed as a Hospital under the relevant national laws and regulations of the country it operates in; - has registered nurses and at least one Doctor stationed at all times; - mainly operates to diagnose and treat injuries or illnesses on an in-patient basis; [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

- has organised facilities for diagnosis, treatment and major surgery; and - is not primarily a nursing facility, clinic, nursing home, convalescence home, psychiatric facility, drug and alcohol rehabilitation facility, preventative medicine facility, hospice care or a similar establishment [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

Institution means any legally approved constituted establishment under the relevant national laws and regulations of the country it operates in that provide residential care for individuals who are fit for discharge from Hospital [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

Life insured means the person whom We provide the cover for. The Life insured does not have any right to the policy, unless he/she is also the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

> Nominee(s) is a person that You have nominated (under the Insurance Act 1966 and Insurance (Nomination of Beneficiaries) Regulations 2009 ) to receive the policy monies payable under the policy upon Your death. The nomination must be registered with Us. [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md]

Non-participating means it does not share in any surplus or profits of the Company's fund [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

Policy owner is the person named as the owner in the Policy Information Page or any Endorsement issued by Us. The Policy owner has full rights on the policy, unless the policy has been transferred to another party [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

Premium(s) is the amount of money that You pay to Us to keep this policy in force [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

- a) subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict Us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - b) who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

> Proper claimant(s) has the meaning in the Insurance Act 1966 . It means a person who claims to be entitled to the sums in question as executor of the deceased, or who claims to be entitled to that sum (whether for his own Benefit or not) and is the widower, widow, parent, child, brother, sister, nephew or niece of the deceased. [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md]

Reinstatement date is the date we reinstate your rider back to in force [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

Rider commencement date means the date the rider commences, as shown in the Policy Information Page [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

We, Our, Us, the Company means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Registration No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

You, Your means the Policy owner [src:raw/wordings/extra-disability-care-rider-policy-contract.md].

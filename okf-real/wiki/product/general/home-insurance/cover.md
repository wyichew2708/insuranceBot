## Section 1 -Renovation

We will pay You for physical loss or damage to the Renovation caused by any of the Insured Perils on a First Loss basis. Our maximum liability during any one policy year in respect of this section shall not exceed in the aggregate the Benefit Limit stated in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

## Section 2 -Home Contents

We will pay You for physical loss or damage to Home Contents, caused by any of the Insured Perils on a First Loss basis. Our maximum liability during any one policy year in respect of this section shall not exceed in the aggregate of the Benefit Limit stated in the Schedule and including the following: [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

> - a) Moveable household goods, Valuables and personal belongings, including Money up to S$750 owned by, or the legal responsibility of your or a member of your family in your Home. - b) Tenant's fixtures and fittings. - c) Visitors' personal effects up to S$2,500 when in your Home un less otherwise insured. [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md]

> - d) Office equipment and office furniture used by you or your family for business or professional purpose up to S$5,000 in your home, unless otherwise insured, that is owned by, or the legal responsibility of you or a member of your family. [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md]

## Section 3 -Personal Legal Liability (Worldwide)

(a) We will pay up to the limit stated in Your Schedule for any one occurrence and in aggregate in respect of which You or any member of Your Household become legally liable to pay as compensation in Your or their capacity as a private individual or as occupiers of Your Home for: [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- (i) Accidental death or bodily Injury to third party; - (ii) loss or damage to property which does not belong to You nor is under the charge or control of You or any member of Your Household occurring anywhere in the world during the Period of Insurance [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

(b) We will also pay, up to the limit stated in Your Schedule; [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- (i) all legal cost and expenses of litigation recovered against You or any member of the Household by any claimant; - (ii) all costs and expenses of legal defence incurred by You or any member of the Household with Our written consent [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

## 15. Geographical Coverage

For Sections 1-2, the insurance coverage is limited to loss occurring within Singapore [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

## 18. Sum Insured for Building, Renovations and/or Contents Cover

You must insure Your Building, Renovation and/or Contents for an amount that represents the full replacement value [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- a) For Building and Renovation, this is the estimated cost of rebuilding or reconstruction, if the Building and Renovation were completely destroyed. - b) For Contents, this is the cost to replace the item on a like-for-like basis based on current market prices [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

If You do not insure Your Building, Renovation and/or Contents for the right amount, We will consider that You have under-insured them [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

## Section C: Family Accidental Death Protection

> If You, Your legal spouse or Child suffers death due to Injury during the Period of Insurance occurring anywhere in the world, We will pay You or Your legal personal representative the benefits as described below if the death occurs within ninety (90) days of the Injury. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-home-policy-wording.md]

> Our maximum liability is $50,000 in the aggregate during the Period of Insurance subject to the following limits: [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-home-policy-wording.md]

> - (a) You and Your spouse at $15,000 each - (b) Your Child(ren) at $10,000 each [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-home-policy-wording.md]

> - (a) the deceased was more than 70 years old at the time the Injury occurred. - (b) Death is consequent upon: - i. suicide or any attempted suicide; - ii. self-inflicted injury; - iii. the effects of intoxicating liquor or illegal drugs; [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-home-policy-wording.md]

- iv. pregnancy, childbirth; - v. any kind of disease or illness; [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-home-policy-wording.md].

- vi. pre-existing physical or mental defects or infirmity including insanity. 2. (c) death is caused while You, Your spouse or Child(ren) were engaging in or taking part in: - i. any naval, military or air force service or operation; [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-home-policy-wording.md].

- ii. air travel except as a fare-paying passenger in a fully licensed passenger carrying aircraft; - iii. any trade, technical or sporting activity or as crew, all in connection with an aircraft; - iv. any kind of race (other than on foot or swimming) or trial of speed or reliability, - v. dangerous sports such as parachuting, hang gliding, mountaineering, rock climbing; [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-home-policy-wording.md].

- vi. winter sports; - vii. sports in a professional capacity [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-home-policy-wording.md].

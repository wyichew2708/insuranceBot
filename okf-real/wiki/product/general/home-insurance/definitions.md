## General Definitions

Accident / Accidental means a sudden, unforeseen and fortuitous event, occurring during the Period of Insurance that is the only cause of Injury or property damage [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Building shall mean the private Home or residential flat and all domestic outbuildings, including any fixtures and fittings attached to the building, building structure (but not the foundations), swimming pool, walls, gates and fences and all other domestic improvements of a structural nature at the premise and for which You are legally responsible. All buildings are to be built of bricks, stone or concrete and roofed with concrete, slate, metal, asbestos or a composite of asbestos and other non-combustible mineral ingredient unless otherwise allowed for by Us and specifically mentioned in your Policy [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Burglary shall mean unlawful or forcible entry or attempted entry of a Building with the intent to commit a felony or theft that causes damage to the Building [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Endorsement means written evidence of an agreed change to the terms of this Policy [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Family means your spouse and Children, and your relatives permanently living with you at the address stated in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

First Loss means that the sum insured represents only a portion of the full value of the Home Contents at risk [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Fungi means any form of fungus including but not limited to all forms of mould or mildew and any mycotoxins, spores, scents, vapours, gases or substances including any by-products produced or released by it [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Home Contents shall mean any moveable household item belonging to You or any member of Your Household except for the following: [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- a) Property more specifically insured under another policy. - b) Motor vehicles and accessories, pedal cycles and watercraft. - c) Deeds, bonds, bills of exchange, promissory notes, cheques, traveller's cheques, securities for - d) Money, stamps, certificates or documents of any kind, manuscripts, medals, contact lenses, - e) Hearing aids and livestock unless specially mentioned herein [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- f) Any part of the structure or ceilings of the building(s), wallpapers and the like or external. - g) Television and radio antennae, aerials, aerial fittings, masts and towers. - h) Any property the value of which is included in the Total Sum Insured on Renovation, fixtures and fittings or Building(s) [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- i) Landlord's fixtures and fittings. - j) Property owned or held in trust in connection with any business profession or trade. - k) Livestock [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

HDB means Housing &amp; Development Board [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Home/Dwelling means a building occupied as private dwelling (house, flat or apartment) together with its garages and outbuildings, all used for domestic purposes at the address shown in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Household means all members of Your Family living with You permanently [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

> Injury means bodily injury sustained by an Insured Person during the Period of Insurance and is caused by an Accident solely and independently of any other causes within fifty-two (52) weeks from the date of such Accident. [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md]

Insured Perils refer to the following: [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- (a) Fire, lightning, thunderbolt, subterranean fire. - (b) Domestic explosion. - (c) Aircraft and other aerial devices and/or articles dropped therefrom. - (d) Impact by any road vehicle not belonging to nor under the control of You or any member of the Household [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

> - (e) Bursting or overflowing of domestic water tanks, apparatus or pipes from within the insured Home or surrounding the Insured Home but excluding damage thereto and loss or damage occurring whilst Your Home is left unoccupied for more than sixty (60) consecutive days. [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md]

> - (f) Burglary accompanied by actual violent and forcible entry or any attempt to break in excluding loss or damage occurring whilst Your Home is left unoccupied for more than sixty (60) consecutive days. - (g) Hurricane, cyclone, typhoon and windstorm including flood or overflow of the sea occasioned thereby. [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md]

> - (h) Earthquake or volcanic eruption including flood or overflow of the sea occasioned thereby. - (i) Subsidence or landslip caused by flood only but excluding the first S$10,000 or 10% of the claim cost whichever is higher for each and every loss. [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md]

> - (j) Riot, strike, civil commotion not amounting to a popular uprising, strike or labour disturbance. - (k) Loss or damage to your Home caused by vandalism to your Home except for loss or damage occurring while Your Home has been unoccupied for more than sixty (60) days whether consecutively or not in any one Period of Insurance and/or loss or damage caused by the wilful or dishonest act of You and/or Your Household or with the connivance of You and/or Your Household. [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md]

- (l) Falling trees or branches but not loss or damage of the falling or looping of trees caused directly or indirectly by You or your Household [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- (m) Smoke damage to the insured Home by fire directly caused by smoke due to a sudden, unusual and faulty operation of any heating or cooking unit in Your Home excluding damage thereto. - (n) Accidental Damage to all electrical and electronic appliances in your Home [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Money means cash, coins, bank notes, currency notes, promissory notes, cheques, traveller cheques, money orders, postal order s, deeds, bonds, crossed banker's draft belonging to You or for which You have accepted responsibility and held for personal purposes [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Period of Insurance refers to the period of cover as shown in the Policy Schedule [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

- (i) subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this Policy; or - (ii) who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Robbery shall mean taking or attempting to take anything of value from the care, custody, or control of a person or persons by force or threat of force or violence and/or by putting the victim in fear [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Renovation means improvements and additions made within Your Home by You as owner or by any former owner of Your home in the form of fixtures and fittings which include flooring, built-in wardrobes and kitchen cabinets but does not form any part of the Building [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Schedule means the documents with details of the Insured Person, type of cover and Period of Insurance which forms part of the policy [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Sum Insured refers to the maximum amount which You are insured as shown in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Theft shall mean the physical removal of an object that is capable of being stolen without the consent of the owner and with the intention of depriving the owner of it permanently [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Valuables mean jewellery, watches, curios, works of art, furs, antiques, stamps or coins collections and other collectable items, manuscripts, medals, items of gold, silver or other precious stones, platinum, paintings, fine glassware and crystal, tapestries, antiques and other collectable property that are kept in Your Home [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

Visitors means someone who visits your Home or a person who is invited by you to your Home excluding third party contractors who comes to your Home to do repair work [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

We, Our, Us, and Insurer refer to Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd., (Company Reg. No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

You, Your, Yours and Insured Person refer to the individual named in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/complimentary-home-insurance-policy-wording.md].

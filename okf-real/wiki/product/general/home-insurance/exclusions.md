a) Notwithstanding any provision to the contrary within this contract, this contract excludes any Cyber Loss. b) Cyber Loss means any loss, damage, liability, expense, fines or penalties or any other amount directly or indirectly caused by: i. the use or operation of any Computer System or Computer Network; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-home-policy-wording-v9-20-oct-2023-final.md#p15].

ii. the reduction in or loss of ability to use or operate any Computer System, Computer Network or Data; iii. access to, processing, transmission, storage or use of any Data; iv. inability to access, process, transmit, store or use any Data; [src:raw/wordings/tiq-home-policy-wording-v9-20-oct-2023-final.md#p15].

v. any threat of or any hoax relating to 2.1 to 2.4 above; vi. any error or omission or Accident in respect of any Computer System, Computer Network or Data. c) Computer System means any computer, hardware, software, application, process, code, programme, information technology, communications system or electronic device owned or operated by the Insured or any other party. This includes any similar system and any associated input, output or data storage device or system, networking equipment or back up facility [src:raw/wordings/tiq-home-policy-wording-v9-20-oct-2023-final.md#p15].

d) Computer Network means a group of Computer Systems and other electronic devices or network facilities connected via a form communications technology, including the internet, intranet and virtual private networks (VPN), allowing the networked computing devices to exchange Data. e) Data means information used, accessed, processed, transmitted or stored by a Computer System [src:raw/wordings/tiq-home-policy-wording-v9-20-oct-2023-final.md#p15].

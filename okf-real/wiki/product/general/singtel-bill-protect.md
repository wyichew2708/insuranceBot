## About Singtel Bill Protect Insurance

This product is compiled from its policy documents. The wording is the contract; the sections below quote it [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md].

## Sum Insured (SGD)

> Twelve (12) times of Your last Eligible Singtel Bill, up to a limit of S$1,200 in one (1) lump sum Six (6) times of Your last Eligible Singtel Bill at the point of claim, up to a limit of S$600 paid in six (6) equal monthly instalments [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md]

## 1. Accidental Death Benefit

> We will pay an amount equivalent to twelve (12) times of Your last Eligible Singtel Bill in one lump sum, up to a limit of S$1,200 if during the Period of Insurance, You sustain Bodily Injury which results in Accidental death within twelve (12) months from the date of Accident. [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2]

If a successful claim is made on the Accidental Death Benefit, We will pay the Accidental Death Benefit to Your estate [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2].

When We make the payment for Accidental Death Benefit, the policy ends [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2].

## 2. Retrenchment Benefit

> The Retrenchment Benefit will only be available if the Retrenchment Date occurs after ninety (90) days from the Commencement Date. [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2]

> We will pay You an amount equivalent to six (6) times of Your last Eligible Singtel Bill at the point of claim, up to a limit of S$600 paid in six (6) equal monthly instalments if during the Period of Insurance, You get retrenched and remained unemployed for at least thirty (30) consecutive days from the Retrenchment Date, before reaching Age 65. [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2]

> If a successful claim is made on the Retrenchment Benefit, We will pay You the Retrenchment Benefit which will be credited by the Group Policy Owner into Your designated Singtel billing account(s) to pay off six (6) months of unpaid Eligible Singtel Bill, up to the limit of S$600 provided that Your designated Singtel bill account(s) is/are still active. [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2]

When We make the payment for Retrenchment Benefit, the policy ends [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2].

If a claim has been submitted for the Accidental Death Benefit while we are processing the claim for Retrenchment Benefit, We will process the claim for Accidental Death Benefit only and reject the claim for Retrenchment Benefit [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p2].

termination or suspension due to wilful or deliberate misconduct or unlawful behaviour; [src:raw/product-summaries/singtel-bill-protect-product-summary-v1-23.md#p3].

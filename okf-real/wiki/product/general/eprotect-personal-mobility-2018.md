## About Eprotect Personal Mobility 2018 Insurance

This product is compiled from its policy documents. The wording is the contract; the sections below quote it [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md].

## Benefits Table

> We shall pay the Benefit Amount in accordance to the Schedule of benefits stated under Your plan if during the Period of Insurance, You sustain Bodily Injury while riding on, mounting onto or dismounting from Your Bicycle or Mobility Device which results in Accidental Death within fifty two (52) weeks from the date of Accident. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md]

## Benefit 2 - Permanent Disablement due to Accident $150,000

> If during the Period of Insurance, You sustain Bodily Injury while riding on, mounting onto or dismounting from Your Bicycle or Mobility Device which results in Permanent Disablement within fifty-two (52) weeks from the date of Accident, We shall pay the relevant Benefit Amount in accordance to the Schedule of benefits stated under Your plan as specified below: [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md]

> (a) Permanent Total Paralysis or complete insanity or injuries resulting in $150,000 being permanently bedridden. (b) Permanent Total Loss of sight of both eyes or use of both hands and $150,000 both feet or combination of these. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md]

> (e) Permanent Total Loss of sight of one eye or use of one limb. $75,000 [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md]

> (g) Permanent Total Loss of use of a shoulder, elbow, hip, knee, ankle or $22,500 wrist. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md]

## Benefit 3 – Medical Expense Reimbursement (any one Accident)

> $3,000 If You sustain Bodily Injury during the Period of Insurance while riding on, mounting onto or dismounting from Your Bicycle or Mobility Device which requires medical treatment at a certified / registered clinic or hospital, We will reimburse the medical expenses incurred up to the limits specified in the Schedule of benefits stated under Your plan. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md]

> For medical treatment by a certified / registered Traditional Chinese Medical Practitioner, We will reimburse the medical expenses incurred up to $100 per visit and $1,000 per Accident. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md]

## Conditions applicable to Benefit 3

Total Benefit claimed for both Outpatient Medical Expenses and Inpatient Medical Expenses in connection with the same Accident shall not exceed the limits specified in the schedule of benefits stated under Your plan. The limit stated in Benefit 3 is applicable to any one Accident [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md].

> Deductible of $200 per Accident shall apply for Benefit 3 except for treatment by Traditional Chinese Medical Practitioner. For the avoidance of doubt, in the event You become entitled to a refund or reimbursement of all or part of such expenses from any other source, or if there is in place any other insurance against the events covered under Benefit 3, We will only be liable for the excess of the amount recoverable from such other source of insurance. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md]

## Optional Coverage

Geographical Limit Extension Territorial other than specified in the left [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md].

## Unless otherwise specified, We shall not pay any Benefit whilst You are overseas

(a) if the event which may give rise to claim occurs on the purpose of business, study or vacation for a period exceeding three (3) consecutive months in any one annual Period of Insurance; or (b) to seek medical treatment (even though such treatment shall be upon the requirement of attending Medical Practitioner) if such treatment is available locally [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md].

## 7. Payment of Benefit

Any Benefit payable under this Policy shall be paid to You or Your nominated beneficiary, if any, as stated in the Schedule or otherwise to Your estate. Any receipt by You or Your nominated beneficiary or estate of any Benefit payable under this Policy shall in all cases be deemed final and complete discharge of all of Our liabilities in respect of such Benefit [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-personal-mobility-policy-wording2018.md].

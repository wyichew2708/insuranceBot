## Exclusions

> There are certain conditions (such as death from suicide or pre-existing conditions within 12 months from the date of issue of the policy or the date of the last reinstatement of the policy, whichever is later) under which no benefits will be payable. When this happens, we will return the total premiums you paid without interest, less any expenses incurred by us on your policy. [src:raw/product-summaries/eeasy-savepro-product-summary-15-year-policy-term.md#p6]

These are stated as exclusions in the policy contract. You are advised to read your policy contract for the full details of these exclusions [src:raw/product-summaries/eeasy-savepro-product-summary-15-year-policy-term.md#p6].

## Summary of Cover

The extent of the insurance You have depends on the type of cover You have taken up. The table below shows a summary of each type of cover [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

2. Gap Cover (applicable for Comprehensive cover only) [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

3. Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. Package (applicable for Comprehensive cover only) [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## Section I - Your Car

If Your Car and its Accessories and spare parts are accidently damaged, lost or stolen, or damaged by fire during the Period of Insurance, We may at Our option: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (a) Repair or replace any part of the Car or any Accessory or spare part damaged or stolen; or - (b) Pay in cash the amount of loss or damage [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

The Accessories and spare parts must be on the Car at the time of damage, loss or theft [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

We will not pay more than the Market Value of the part of the Car, the Accessories or spare parts damaged or lost plus the reasonable costs of fitting parts [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 1. Injury and Damage

With all types of cover, We will pay all the amounts that You are legally required to pay to others in compensation for: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (a) Death or bodily injury; - (b) Loss of or damage to property; [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

As a result of an Accident occurring during the Period of Insurance and arising out of the use of Your Car [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 2. Personal Accident Benefits (for You only)

We pay the compensation for physical disability or death set out in the scale of benefits if You suffer bodily injury that: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (a) Is the direct and immediate result of an Accident - (i) involving Your Car; or - (ii) When travelling in, or getting into or out of Your Car during the Period of Insurance; and - (b) Is caused by accidental, violent, external and visible means [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

> The physical disability or death must occur within three (3) calendar months of the Accident and be independent of any other cause (except associated medical or surgical treatment). It must not arise directly or indirectly out of intentional self-injury, suicide or attempted suicide, physical defect or infirmity. The Accident must not have happened while You were under the influence of intoxicating liquor or drugs. You must be between eighteen (18) and sixty-five (65) years of age at the time of the injury. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

We pay the compensation to You or, in the event of death, to Your legal personal representative. Once We pay the benefit, We have no further liability to You or Your legal personal representative. If You hold more than one (1) motor policy with Us, compensation is payable under one (1) policy only. If the insured is a company, We pay these benefits only if an individual is named by endorsement in the Policy as being entitled to receive them [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

> If more than one of the items 1-9 listed in the scale of benefits are suffered in the same Accident, We pay for the item that gives the highest benefit. The maximum amount We pay in total during a Period of Insurance is $50,000. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

## Other Benefits

9. Total and Permanent Loss of Sight in One Eye Together with Total Loss by Physical Severance of One Hand (At or Above the Wrist) or One Foot (At or Above the Ankle) [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 3. Drive Less Save More (DLSM)

We will reward You if Your mileage is low with a Rebate on Your Car insurance premium at the end of each month throughout the Policy period, provided You submit Your Mileage Data on monthly basis [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## Optional Benefits

> 1. NCD Protector (applicable for 30% onwards) [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

> If You are entitled to 30%, 40% or 50% NCD, in the event there is one (1) claim made against Your policy within the Period of Insurance, You will be allowed to retain Your NCD. If a second or more claims are made, the normal NCD rules will apply and Your entitlement will be reduced by 30% NCD on renewal with Us. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

- (a) in the event You decide to switch insurer, the protected NCD is not transferable to the new insurer; and - (b) the NCD Protector does not waive Our rights to cancel or refuse to renew Your Policy at the end of the Period of Insurance [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

> In the event of total loss or constructive total loss of Your Car as covered under Section I of this Policy, if the Market Value at the time of loss of Your Car is below the Outstanding Car loan (at the time of loss) for the purchase of Your Car, We will pay for the difference between the Outstanding Car loan and the Market Value at the time of loss up to S$80,000. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

'Outstanding Car loan' refers to the outstanding loan amount owed by You to the finance company or hire purchase company for the purchase of Your Car as at the date of total loss of Your Car but excluding any loan interest or overdue interest, penalties or fines and/or any amounts due in arrears and payable by You before the total loss of Your Car. This is provided always that the total loss or constructive total loss of Your Car is not due to flood [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

We will not pay under this benefit if the outstanding amount due to the lessor or hire purchase owner has already been discharged [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## (b) Courtesy Car Benefit

> We will arrange for a courtesy rental Car for Your use from an available fleet of vehicles provided by Mova Automotive Pte. Ltd. for the period of the repair of Your Car at Your preferred workshop if You have purchased a Comprehensive Any Workshop plan, or at any of Our Authorised Workshops up to a maximum of ten (10) days, if the period of repairs recommended by our appointed surveyor or loss adjuster exceeds four (4) days. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

The make and model of the courtesy rental Car being provided will be determined by Us, subject to vehicle availability [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

This benefit is not applicable for: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (i) any accident repair not authorised by Our appointed surveyor or loss adjuster; or - (ii) a Windscreen claim; or - (iii) a total loss claim; or - (iv) when the repairs of Your Car is delayed due to unavailability of replacement parts; or - (v) any amount beyond the repair period recommended by Our appointed surveyor or loss adjuster even if the repairs take longer. If Your Car is repaired sooner than expected, We will only take into account for the number of days it actually took to repair Your Car [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## (d) Enhanced Personal Accident Benefits

We pay the compensation for physical disability or death set out in the scale of benefits if You, an Authorised Driver or the passenger(s) suffer bodily injury that: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (i) is the direct and immediate result of an Accident; - (a) involving Your Car; or - (b) when travelling in, or getting into or out of Your Car during the Period of Insurance; and [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (ii) is caused by accidental, violent, external and visible means [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

If more than one of the items 1-9 listed in the scale of benefits are suffered in the same Accident, We pay for the item that gives the highest benefit [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## Scale of Benefits

> The maximum amount We pay in total during a Period of Insurance is S$100,000 for You, S$20,000 for an Authorised Driver and S$10,000 per passenger multiplied by the legal seating capacity of Your Car. If the number of persons (including the driver) in Your Car at the time of occurrence exceeds the legal seating capacity of Your Car, We shall only be liable for a pro rata proportion of the compensation which otherwise would be payable. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

Note: Section 2 under 'Other Benefits' of this Policy will no longer be applicable. We will pay under this benefit instead. This benefit is only applicable provided that You have a claim which is paid or payable by Us according to the Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 1. Authorised Workshops Scheme

In the event Your Car is involved in an Accident and gives rise to a claim, Your Car must be brought to an Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. authorised workshop selected and approved by Us for repairs. Failure to do so, We reserve the right to decline liability under Section I of the Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 3. Loss of Use Benefit

> We will pay You a daily transport allowance of S$50 up to a maximum of five (5) days, if the period of repairs recommended by our appointed surveyor or loss adjuster exceeds three (3) days. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

- (a) any transport allowance for any accident repair not authorised by Our appointed surveyor or loss adjuster; or - (b) a Windscreen claim; or - (c) a total loss claim; or - (d) when the repairs of Your Car is delayed due to unavailability of replacement parts; or - (e) any amount beyond the repair period recommended by Our appointed surveyor or loss adjuster even if the repairs take longer. If Your Car is repaired sooner than expected, We will only pay You for the number of days it actually took to repair Your Car [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

> The total amount payable under this benefit shall not exceed S$250 in respect of any one (1) Accident and will only be paid to You after We have finally settled Your repair bill with the Authorised Workshop. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

In the event that the Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. Package is added to Your plan, this benefit shall cease to be applicable, and the Courtesy Car Benefit shall be applicable in its place [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 2. Agreement Between You and Us

Your insurance cover is a legal contract between You and Us. We agree to give You the insurance set out on the Policy for the premium paid by You [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

The proposal form and any other information which You give Us to obtain the insurance are relied on by Us in deciding whether or not to insure You [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

The insurance is only for the cover for which You have a certificate of insurance and only for the Period of Insurance indicated on the certificate. It is subject to the conditions contained in the Policy and certificate of insurance [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

If two (2) or more persons are named as the Insured on the Policy, each of them is responsible both individually and together for: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (a) the completeness and accuracy of information in all proposal forms, statements, claims or documents given by any one of them to Us; and - (b) compliance with the conditions of the Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 4. Duty of Care

- (a) keep Your Car, its accessories and spare parts in good condition and take reasonable care to safeguard them from loss or damage; - (b) allow Us access to examine Your Car or interview any person or employee You permit to drive it; [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (c) comply with all the conditions set out in Your Policy and certificate of insurance; - (d) comply with any relevant laws in the region where You are driving; - (e) comply promptly with requirements of public authorities; - (f) tell us immediately: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (i) if there is any material change in the Car or in the nature of the risk - (ii) if You no longer have an interest in the Car - (iii) if You take out any other insurance which covers the Car or liability against similar risks; [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (g) make sure that any person You allow to drive or be in charge of Your Car understands Your duties under the Policy and complies with its conditions as far as they apply to him [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## General Definitions

Accessories refers to all audio, video and other standard equipment fitted into the Car by the manufacturer or distributor at the time Your Car was originally bought [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Accident refers to an unexpected and unintentional event that is violent, visible and external in relation to Your Car [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Authorised Driver refers to any person(s) that holds a valid and relevant class of driving license and has been granted permission by You to drive Your Car [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Authorised Repair Limit refers to the limit You are allowed to authorise for a repair in the event Your Car is damaged and/or lost in an Accident and the damage and/or loss is covered by Your Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Car refers to the vehicle insured under this Policy as shown in the schedule or certificate of insurance [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Market Value refers to the cost of replacing Your Car with one of the same make and model, of similar condition and age as currently available immediately before the date of the loss or Accident [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Mileage Data refers to information on Your Car odometer mileage reading submitted by You through Our Platform [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Named Driver refers to any person named in the Policy under 'persons or classes of persons entitled to drive" [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

No Claim Discount (NCD) refers to a discount in Your premium when there is no claim lodged by You or made against You by any third party [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Period of Insurance refers to the period during which Your Car is insured under this Policy as shown in the schedule or certificate of insurance [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Policy refers to this Policy, Your application form, Your declarations, the Schedule, the certificate of insurance and any Endorsements We have issued under this Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Platform refers to a website and any other platform notified and provided by Us [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (i) Subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - (ii) Who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Rebate refers to rebate given to You if Your mileage driven during the Period of Insurance falls within the low mileage category, as per the Rebate Table [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Rebate Table refers to the Rebate given to You as a percentage of basic premium paid. Basic premium paid is calculated based on basic premium less No Claim Discount (NCD) less discount for direct purchases (if any). The Rebate payable will depend on the mileage driven per year as per the Rebate Table below: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Annual mileage driven (kilometres driven) Percentage Rebate with no additional premium [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Windscreen refers to the front, side, rear and quarter glass, but not the sunroof or any glass roof of the Car [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Unnamed Driver refers to any person who is not named in the Policy under 'persons or classes of persons entitled to drive' [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Us / We / Our refers to Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Reg. No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

You / Your / Insured refers to the policyholder named in the Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 1. Meaning of Words

The Policy, Schedule, Endorsement and certificate of insurance should be read together as one contract. Any word or expression which has a specific meaning has the same meaning whenever that word or expression is used in the Policy, Schedule, Endorsement or certificate of insurance [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

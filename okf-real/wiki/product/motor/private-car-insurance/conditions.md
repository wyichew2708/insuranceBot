## 4. Nine (9) Months Warranty For Repairs

You will be granted a nine (9) months warranty of the repairs carried out on Your vehicle, only if it is repaired at any of Our Authorised Workshops. This warranty is only applicable provided that You have a claim which is paid or payable by Us under Section I of this Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md#p18].

## 3. Duty of Disclosure

The law requires You to tell Us everything You know (or could reasonably be expected to know) that is relevant to Our decision to give You insurance and on what terms. You must tell Us these things before We cover You and every time You renew a Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md#p19].

You must tell Us immediately if any of the information You have given Us changes. If You do not give Us all relevant information or if You mislead Us, We may: (a) refuse to pay a claim or part of it; and/or (b) cancel the Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md#p19].

## (a) Cancellation by You

> You can cancel a Policy at any time by giving Us seven (7) days’ notice in writing. If You cancel, We refund 80% of the pro-rata premium for the unexpired period. If You cancel the Policy before the inception date, an administrative fee of S$25 (before GST) will be charged and we will refund you the remaining premium paid. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md#p20]

## However, You will not receive any refund if

> - Your premium is less than S$25 (before GST); - any claim has been made or has arisen under this Policy (including claim for Windscreen); - we have paid one or more claims under or in connection with current period of this Policy; [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md#p20]

## (b) Cancellation by Us

We may cancel a Policy at any time by giving You seven (7) days’ notice. We refund the premium less an amount to cover the period for which You were insured. If someone else is the legal owner of Your Car and You have told Us of their names and their contact details, We will advise them when We cancel the Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md#p20].

If You owe any premium, You must pay it promptly to Us [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md#p20].

(a) Cash for the premium is handed over to Us or the intermediary; (b) A credit or debit card transaction for the premium is approved by the issuing bank; (c) A payment through an electronic medium including the internet is approved by the relevant party; [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md#p22].

(d) A credit in favour of Us or the intermediary is made through an electronic medium including the internet. 2.

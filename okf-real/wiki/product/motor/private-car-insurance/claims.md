## 1. What You Must Do

> - (a) If Your Car is involved in an Accident, regardless of the severity and whether the Accident would lead to a claim being made against Us, You must call any of Our Authorised Workshops and report the Accident within 24 hours of the Accident or by the next working day (excluding Saturdays, Sundays and Public Holidays). [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

- (b) If Your Car is stolen, or is involved in a criminal act, You must give Us and to the police immediate notice of the event and co-operate with Us in taking action against the person involved in such theft or criminal act [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (c) If You receive any letter, claim, court document, police summons or any form of communication related to an Accident involving Your Car, You must immediately inform Us before You respond. - (d) You must give Us any information and help that We may need in handling a claim. This may include attending court to give evidence [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

> - (e) If You are making a claim on Us for damage to Your Car, You must write to Us within fourteen (14) days of the event or within fourteen (14) days of when You would reasonably be expected to know of the event. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

These conditions as a whole are very important and if You do not abide by them after an occurrence of an Accident, theft or involvement in a criminal act as set out above, the following consequences will result: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (a) We shall not pay for any damage to Your Car or pay anyone who claims against You for losses caused to them for that particular occurrence; and - (b) You shall lose all or part of Your NCD as set out below: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

'Non-reporting NCD' - Means the % of the NCD that would be deducted from Your NCD entitlement because of not reporting or being late in reporting an Accident [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 2. What You Must Not Do

When there may be a claim against Us, You must not, without Our written consent: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

- (a) leave Your Car unattended without taking proper precautions to prevent further loss or damage in the event of an Accident - (b) drive Your Car after it is damaged before necessary repairs have been carried out (We will not be liable for any further damage if You do) - (c) carry out repairs to Your own Car beyond the Authorised Repair Limit or dispose of any damaged property until We have had the opportunity to inspect it - (d) admit liability to anyone else - (e) negotiate, pay or settle a claim with anyone else [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 3. What We May Do

If an event happens that causes loss, damage or injury, We may: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

> - (a) adjust Your claim before repairing Your Car, if the repair costs is more than S$300; - (b) consider Your claim even if You do not send Your Car to the reporting centre at Our Authorised Workshop as required, if You: [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

- (i) send Your Car to the reporting centre at Our Authorised Workshop for inspection and report the Accident as soon as possible before it is repaired elsewhere; and - (ii) write to give Us the reason for not reporting and/or sending Your Car to the reporting centre at Our Authorised Workshop as required - (c) represent You at any inquest or official inquiry [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

If We pay Your claim, We have the right to take legal action in Your name against any person responsible for the loss, damage or injury. We take this action at Our own expense. You must not do anything which limits Our rights to do so [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

We are not obliged to continue to conduct the defence or settlement of a claim against You by another person for damage to his property once We have paid up to the limit of liability shown on the Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 4. Other Insurance

If You make a valid claim for damage or loss, We are liable to contribute only a pro rata amount if You have other insurance covering the same damage or loss. This Policy does not provide cover for other persons if they have cover under any other insurance Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## 6. What Can Affect Your Entitlement

If You do not comply with any condition of Your Policy, it may affect Your right to claim under the Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## NCD After a Claim Occurs

If more than one (1) Car is shown as Insured in the Policy Schedule and a claim has been made for one of them, then Your current NCD is reduced only for that Car. The NCD for the other Car(s) is(are) not affected [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Your NCD is not affected by any notice or information You give or are required to give to Us unless You make a claim on the policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## Endorsement No. M1P - Excess All Claims

It is hereby understood and agreed that notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in this Policy the Insured in respect of each and every event shall be responsible for the first amount stated under 'excess' in the Schedule of the Policy (or any less expenditure which may be incurred) of any expenditure for which provision is made thereunder (including any payments in respect of costs and expenses) and of any expenditure by Us in the exercise of Our discretion [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

If the expenditure incurred by Us shall include the amount for which the Insured is responsible hereunder such amount shall be repaid by the Insured to Us forthwith [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

For the purposes of this endorsement the expression 'event' shall mean an event or series of events arising out of one cause in connection with any one (1) motor vehicle in respect of or in connection with which indemnity is granted under this Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Subject otherwise to the terms of this Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

## Endorsement No. M2(A)P - Excess - Own Damage Claims

It is hereby declared and agreed that notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in this Policy the Insured in respect of each and every event shall be responsible for the first amount stated under 'excess' in the Schedule of the Policy (or any less expenditure which may be incurred) of any expenditure for which provision is made thereunder [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

For the purpose of this endorsement the expression 'event' shall mean an event or series of events arising out of one cause in connection with Your Car. Subject otherwise to the terms of this Policy [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

Endorsement No. M2U - Unnamed Driver Excess (Applicable if there is a claim under Section I of this Policy) [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md].

> In addition to the excess amount specified in the Policy Schedule, an additional excess of S$1,000 shall apply for Unnamed Driver(s) when, at the time of an accident, the Motor Vehicle is driven by or in the charge of any person other than the Named Drivers listed in the Policy Schedule. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

> This additional excess applies only to Unnamed Driver(s) who are aged twenty-seven (27) years or older , and seventy-five (75) years or younger , or who have a minimum of two (2) years of driving experience in Singapore. [src:raw/wordings/private-car-insurance-policy-wording-v19-2026-02.md]

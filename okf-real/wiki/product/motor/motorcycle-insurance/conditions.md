## Important Notice

> In accordance to Section 23(5) of the Insurance Act (Cap. 142), we would remind You that You must disclose to Us fully and faithfully all the facts You know or could reasonably be expected to know, otherwise You may not receive any benefit from this Policy. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md]

You must be the registered owner of Your Vehicle as per the Land Transport Authority (LTA)’s records during the Period of Insurance with Us, otherwise You may not receive any benefit from this Policy [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md].

## Geographical Excess for Stolen Vehicle

> It is hereby declared and agreed that notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in this policy, the Insured shall be responsible for the amount of up to 50% of the prevailing value of the stolen Motorcycle capped at S$2,500 if the Motorcycle is stolen outside Singapore. [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p6]

2. Every notice communication to be given or made under this policy shall be delivered in writing to the [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p8].

3. ## Payment Before Cover Warranty (Applicable To Individual Policyholders Only)

1. The premium due must be paid to the Insurer (or the intermediary through whom this Policy was effected) on or before the inception date (“the inception date”) or the renewal date of the coverage [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p12].

Payment shall be deemed to have been effected to the Insurer or the intermediary when one of the following acts takes place: [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p12].

(a) Cash or honoured cheque for the premium is handed over to the Insurer or the intermediary; (b) A credit or debit card transaction for the premium is approved by the issuing bank; (c) A payment through an electronic medium including the internet is approved by the relevant party; [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p12].

(d) A credit in favour of the Insurer or the intermediary is made through an electronic medium including [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p12].

In the event that the total premium due is not paid to the Insurer (or the intermediary through whom this Policy was effected) on or before the inception date or the renewal date, then the insurance shall not attach and no benefits whatsoever shall be payable by the Insurer. Any payment received thereafter shall be of no effect whatsoever as cover has not attached [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p12].

## General Excess

It is hereby understood and agreed that notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Policy, the Insured in respect of each and every event shall be responsible for the amount specified in the Schedule under the heading “Excess” (or any less expenditure which may be incurred) of any expenditure for which provision is made hereunder [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p12].

The said expenditure incurred by the Company shall include any payments in respect of claims costs and expenses and/or expenditure incurred by the Company in the exercise of its discretion under Condition 5 forthwith [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p12].

If the expenditure incurred by the Company shall include the amount for which the Insured is responsible hereunder such amount shall be repaid by the Insured to the Company forthwith [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p12].

For the purpose of the Endorsement the expression “event” shall mean an event or series of events arising out of one cause in connection with any one motorcycle in respect of or in connection with which indemnity is granted under this Policy [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p12].

## Personal Data Use

Any information collected or held by Us whether contained in your application or otherwise obtained may be used and/or disclosed to our associated individuals/companies or any independent third parties (within or outside Singapore) for any matters relating to your application, any policy issued and to provide advice or information concerning products and services which we believe may be of interest to you and to communicate with you for any purpose. Your data may also be used for audit, business analysis and reinsurance purposes [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p13].

## Policy Owners’ Protection Scheme

This policy is protected under the Policy Owners’ Protection Scheme which is administered by the Singapore Deposit Insurance Corporation (SDIC). Coverage for your policy is automatic and no further action is required from you. For more information on the types of benefits that are covered under the scheme as well as the limits of coverage, where applicable, please contact us or visit the General Insurance Association (GIA) or SDIC web-sites (www.gia.org.sg or www.sdic.org.sg) [src:raw/wordings/eprotect-motorcycle-policy-wording-v5-2025-10.md#p14].

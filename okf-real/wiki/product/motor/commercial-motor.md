## Section I – Loss Or Damage

1. The Company will indemnify the Insured against loss of or damage to the Motor Vehicle and [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

its accessories and spare parts whilst thereon [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

(a) by accidental collision or overturning or collision or overturning consequent upon [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

mechanical breakdown or consequent upon wear and tear [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

(b) by fire, external explosion, self-ignition or lightning or burglary, housebreaking or theft [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

(d) whilst in transit (including the processes of loading and unloading incidental to such [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

road rail, inland waterway, lift or elevator [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

(ii) direct sea route across the straits between the island of Penang and the mainland or [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

across the straits between Changi Point, Singapore and Tanjong Berlungkor, Johore [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

2. At Our own option the Company may pay in cash the amount of the loss or damage or may repair, reinstate or replace the Motor Vehicle or any part thereof or its accessories or spare parts. The liability of the Company shall not exceed the value of the parts lost or damaged and the reasonable cost of fitting such parts. The Insured’s estimate of value stated in the Schedule shall be the maximum amount payable by the Company in respect of any claim for loss or damage [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

If the Motor Vehicle is disabled by reason of loss or damage insured under this Policy, the Company will subject to the Limits of Liability bear the reasonable cost of protection and removal to the nearest repairers and of delivery within the country where the loss or damage was sustained [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

4. The Insured may authorise the repair of the Motor Vehicle necessitated by damage for which [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

the Company may be liable under this Policy provided that:- [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

the estimated cost of such repair does not exceed the Authorised Repair Limit [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

a detailed estimate of the cost is forwarded to the Company without delay [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p2].

is not entitled to indemnity under any other policy.is not entitled to indemnity under any other policy [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p3].

## Section III – Towing Disabled Vehicles

This Policy shall be operative whilst the Motor Vehicle is being used for the purpose of towing any one disabled mechanically propelled vehicle and the Company will indemnify the Insured in terms of Section II in respect of liability in connection with such towed vehicle provided that [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p4].

(a) such towed vehicle is not towed for reward [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p4].

the Company shall not be liable by reason of this Section in respect of damage to such towed vehicle or property being conveyed thereby [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p4].

## Optional Benefits

The Company will extend to cover third party liabilities for any loss or damage to property, bodily injury or death arising from any Accident that happens while the Equipment is being used [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p13].

The limits of indemnity in respect of any one claim or a series of claims arising from any Accident are: [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p13].

> (a) Up to an aggregate of S$100,000 for damage to property unless otherwise stated in the Schedule. (b) Unlimited for bodily injury or death to any person. [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p13]

> An excess of S$1,000 will apply to each claim or a series of claims arising out of a single Accident from the use of the Equipment. [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p13]

Subject otherwise to the terms and conditions of this Policy [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md#p13].

## Airside Extension

> It is hereby declared and agreed that the above is extended to cover the Motor Vehicle whilst being driven within the airside of Singapore Changi Airport. The limit of the amount under third party property damage and third party bodily injury in respect of any one claim or series of claims arising out of one event is limited to S$100,000 and unlimited respectively.

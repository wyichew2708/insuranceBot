## About Commercial Motor Insurance

## Section I - Loss Or Damage

1. The Company will indemnify the Insured against loss of or damage to the Motor Vehicle and its accessories and spare parts whilst thereon 2. (a) by accidental collision or overturning or collision or overturning consequent upon mechanical breakdown or consequent upon wear and tear 3. (b) by fire, external explosion, self-ignition or lightning or burglary, housebreaking or theft 4. (c) by malicious act 5. (d) whilst in transit (including the processes of loading and unloading incidental to such transit) by 6. (i) road rail, inland waterway, lift or elevator 7. (ii) direct sea route across the straits between the island of Penang and the mainland or across the straits between Changi Point, Singapore and Tanjong Berlungkor, Johore [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

2. At Our own option the Company may pay in cash the amount of the loss or damage or may repair, reinstate or replace the Motor Vehicle or any part thereof or its accessories or spare parts. The liability of the Company shall not exceed the value of the parts lost or damaged and the reasonable cost of fitting such parts. The Insured's estimate of value stated in the Schedule shall be the maximum amount payable by the Company in respect of any claim for loss or damage [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

3. If the Motor Vehicle is disabled by reason of loss or damage insured under this Policy, the Company will subject to the Limits of Liability bear the reasonable cost of protection and removal to the nearest repairers and of delivery within the country where the loss or damage was sustained [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

> 4. The Insured may authorise the repair of the Motor Vehicle necessitated by damage for which the Company may be liable under this Policy provided that:- 11. (a) the estimated cost of such repair does not exceed the Authorised Repair Limit. [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md]

a detailed estimate of the cost is forwarded to the Company without delay [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

## Section II - Liability To Third Parties

1. The Company will subject to the Limits of Liability indemnify the Insured in the event of Accident caused by or arising out of the use of the Motor Vehicle or in connection with the loading or unloading of the Motor Vehicle against all sums including claimant's costs and expenses which the Insured shall become legally liable to pay in respect of: [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

death of or bodily injury to any person 3. (b) damage to property 2. In terms of and subject to the limitations of and for the purposes of this Section the Company will indemnify any Authorised Driver who is driving the Motor Vehicle provided that such Authorised Driver 5. (i) shall as though he were the Insured observe, fulfil and be subject to the Terms of this Policy insofar as they can apply 6. (ii) is not entitled to indemnity under any other policy.is not entitled to indemnity under any other policy [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

3. In the event of the death of any person entitled to indemnity under this Section the Company will in respect of the liability incurred by such person indemnify his personal representatives in terms of and subject to the limitations of such Section provided that such representatives shall as though they were the Insured observe, fulfil and be subject to the Terms of this Policy insofar as they can apply [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

4. The Company will pay all costs and expenses incurred with its written consent. 5. In the event of an Accident involving indemnity under this Section to more than one person the Limits of Liability shall apply to the aggregate amount of indemnity to all persons indemnified and such indemnity shall apply in priority to the Insured [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

> 6. The Company may at its own option 11. (a) arrange for representation at any inquest or fatal inquiry in respect of any death which may be the subject of indemnity under this Section. 12. (b) undertake the defence of proceedings in any Court of Law in respect of any act or alleged offence causing or relating to any event which may be the subject of indemnity under this Section. [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md]

## Airside Extension

> It is hereby declared and agreed that the above is extended to cover the Motor Vehicle whilst being driven within the airside of Singapore Changi Airport. The limit of the amount under third party property damage and third party bodily injury in respect of any one claim or series of claims arising out of one event is limited to S$100,000 and unlimited respectively. [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md]

Notwithstanding this extension, the Policy does not cover any loss or damage to aircraft and its cargo and any bodily injury to passengers on aircraft and aircrews. Accordingly, all forms of aviation liabilities are absolutely excluded, be it directly or indirectly [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

This extension does not cover any Accident, loss or damage or liability of any nature arising out of, on and/or in connection with any area which any aircraft and/or parts of aircraft are in any way transported and/or moved, whether propelled by its own power or otherwise [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

Subject otherwise to the terms and conditions of this Policy [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

Personal Accident Benefit to Unnamed Passengers (Other Than The Insured and His Paid Driver or Attendant) [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

We pay the compensation for physical disability or death set out in the scale of benefits if you suffer bodily injury that: [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

- (a) is the direct and immediate result of an Accident: - i. involving your Motor Vehicle; or - ii. when travelling in, or getting into or out of, any other Motor Vehicle; and - (b) is caused by accidental, violent, external and visible means [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

> The physical disability or death must occur within three (3) calendar months of the Accident and be independent of any other cause (except associated medical or surgical treatment). It must not arise directly or indirectly out of intentional self-injury, suicide or attempted suicide, physical defect or infirmity. The person must be between age 18 and 65 at the time of the injury. If more than one of items 1-9 listed in the Scale of Benefits are suffered in the same Accident, We will pay for the item that gives the highest benefit. [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md]

We pay the compensation to You or, in the event of death, to Your legal personal representative. Once We pay the benefit, We have no further liability to You or Your legal personal representative. If You hold more than one motor policy with Us, compensation is payable under one policy only [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

If the Insured is a company, We pay these benefits only if the Insured has approved for the compensation to be payable directly to the injured person or his legal personal representative whose receipt shall be a full discharge in respect of the injury to such person [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

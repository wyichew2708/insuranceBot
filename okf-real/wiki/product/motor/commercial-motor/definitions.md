## General Definitions

Accident refers to an unexpected and unintentional event that is violent, visible and external in relation to the Motor Vehicle [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

Authorised Driver refers to any person holding a valid and relevant class of driving licence and has been given permission by You to drive the Motor Vehicle [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

Authorised Repair Limit refers to the limit You are allowed to authorised for a repair in the event Your Motor Vehicle is damaged and/or lost in an Accident and the damage and/or loss is covered by Your Policy [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

Endorsement refers to written evidence of an agreed change to the Policy [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

Equipment refers to any plant, machinery, equipment or tool (and its accessories and parts) which is installed on or fixed to the Motor Vehicle for purposes other than to help drive the Motor Vehicle [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

Motor Vehicle refers to the vehicle stated in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

No Claim Discount refers to a discount in Your premium when there is no claim lodged by You or made against You by any third party [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

Period of Insurance refers to the period during which the coverage under the Policy is effective, as stated in the Schedule [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

Policy refers to Your application, this document, Your Schedule, the certificate of insurance and any subsequent Endorsements; all of which should be read together as one contract [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

Schedule refers to the document that contains details of the Policyholder, Your Motor Vehicle, any Authorised Driver, and any terms and conditions specific to Your Policy [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

Us / We / Our / Company / Insurer refers to Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Reg. No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

You / Your / Insured refers to the Policyholder named in Your Schedule and certificate of insurance [src:raw/wordings/commercial-motor-policy-wording-v4.md].

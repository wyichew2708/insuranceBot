## General Definitions

Wherever the following words are used in this policy or in the schedule, they shall have the same meanings as given below: [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Accident means Any injury resulting solely and directly from unexpected, external, violent and visible source and does not include sickness or any naturally occurring medical condition or degenerative process [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Active Service shall mean for an employee to be: [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

- (a) employed by the policyholder on a full-time permanent basis; - (b) actively working on a day which is one of the policyholder's scheduled work days; - (c) performing in the customary manner all the regular duties of his/her employment with the policyholder on a full-time basis that day, either at one of the policyholder's business establishments or at a location that the policyholder's business requires him/her to be present [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

An employee will be considered in Active Service on a day that is not one of the policyholder's scheduled workdays only if he/she was performing in the customary manner all the regular duties of his/her employment on the preceding scheduled workday [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

> Any One Disability means all complications and conditions arising from the same Illness or Injury provided always that if there is any recurrence or relapse of such complications or conditions and the first consultation with a Physician or General Practitioner for the recurrence or relapse is made 30 days after the latest discharge from Hospital, it shall be considered a new disability. [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md]

Co-insurance means the percentage out of an eligible claim which has to be borne by the Insured Person before the relevant benefits are payable [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Country of Residence means mean the Republic of Singapore unless otherwise stated in the policy [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Day Surgery means Surgery that is scheduled and performed and is carried out by a Surgeon but not on an inpatient basis [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Effective Date means the policy commencement date or date of Insured Person's first enrolment into the policy, whichever is later [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Employee means a person employed on a full-time basis by the policyholder [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Endorsement Any written statement or notice issued and signed by Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Reg. No. 201331905K) to confirm and record changes to the terms and conditions of the policy [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Hospital means a registered institution licensed as a hospital by the Ministry of Health, for the care and treatment of persons who are injured or ill and which: [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

> - (a) provides organised facilities for diagnosis, treatment and major surgery; - (b) provides 24-hour nursing services by registered or graduate nurses and under the supervision of one or more Physicians at all times; - (c) is not primarily a clinic, a mental hospital, a place for alcoholics or drug addicts, a nursing, rest or convalescent home or a home for the aged or primarily for rehabilitation, or similar establishment. [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md]

This Policy covers treatment at Singapore Government Restructured Hospitals (SGRH) only. SGRH refers to Singapore General Hospital, Kandang Kerbau Hospital, Tan Tock Seng Hospital, Khoo Teck Puat Hospital, Changi General Hospital and National University Hospital and any other Newly inclusions to the SGRH listing as published in the current SGRH website https://www.google.com/search?q=singapore+government+restructured+hospital+list&amp;rlz=1C1 GCEU\_enSG971SG971&amp;oq=&amp;aqs=chrome.0.69i59i450l8.5179368j0j15&amp;sourceid=chrome&amp;ie= UTF-8 [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

> Hospitalisation means confinement for reason of Illness or Injury to a hospital bed or ward for a continuous period of at least 6 hours for purposes of treatment or surgery and for which the hospital levies a room and board charge. [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md]

Illness or Sickness means any sudden and unexpected pathological deviation from the normal healthy state, marked by interruption, cessation or disorder of body functions, systems or organs as confirmed by a physician [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Injury means bodily injury caused solely and directly by an Accident [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Inpatient means a patient admitted into a hospital for treatment, for which the hospital levies a daily room and board charge [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Insured Person(s) means the person(s) described in the schedule [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

> Intensive Care Unit (ICU) means a section within a hospital which is designated as an intensive care unit by the hospital and which is maintained on a 24-hour basis solely for treatment of patients in critical condition and is equipped to provide special nursing and medical services not available elsewhere in the hospital. [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md]

Period of Insurance means the period of cover shown in the policy schedule for which the appropriate premium has been paid [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Physician or General Practitioner means a qualified medical or dental person with a degree in western medicine who is legally authorised to provide medical, dental or surgical services in the geographical area of his/her practice and practicing within the scope of his/her license and training but excludes the Insured Person or a member of the Insured Person's immediate family [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

> Policy Year means a period of 1 year, commencing from the policy commencement date for the first policy year and from the respective renewal dates for subsequent policy years. [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md]

Pre-existing Illness means disabilities that the Insured and/or Insured Person(s) has reasonable knowledge of. The Insured and/or Insured Person(s) may be considered to have reasonable knowledge of a pre- existing condition where the condition is one for which: [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

- (a) the Insured Person had received or is receiving treatment; or - (b) medical advice, diagnosis, care or treatment has been recommended; or - (c) clear and distinct symptoms are or were evident; or - (d) its existence would have been apparent to a reasonable person in the circumstances [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Prohibited Person means a person or entity who is, or who is related to a person or entity: [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

- (i) Subject to laws, regulations or sanctions administered by any inter-government, government, regulatory or law enforcement authorities of any country, which will prohibit or restrict us from providing insurance or carrying out any transaction under this policy; or - (ii) Who is involved in any terrorist or illegal activities or placed on sanction listing or issued with freezing order [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Reasonable and Customary Charges mean charges and fees for medical care and treatment shall be considered reasonable and customary to the extent that it does not exceed the general level of charges or fees being made by others of similar medical standing in the locality where the charge is incurred when giving like or comparable treatment, services or supplies to individuals of the same gender and of comparable age for a similar illness or injury at Singapore Government Restructured Hospitals [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Specialist means a medical or dental physician with a degree in western medicine registered and licensed as such in the geographical area of his/her practice and who is classified by the appropriate health authorities as a person with superior and special expertise in specified fields of medicine or dentistry and is practicing within the scope of his/her license and training but excludes the Insured Person or a member of the Insured Person's immediate family [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Surgeon means a Specialist who is qualified to perform Surgery [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

Surgery means any invasive surgical intervention [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

You, Your means the Insured named in the schedule as the policyholder [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

We, Our, Us means Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. (Company Reg. No. 201331905K) [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md].

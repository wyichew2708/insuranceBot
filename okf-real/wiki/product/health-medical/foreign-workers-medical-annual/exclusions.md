## 11. Prohibited Person Limitation and Exclusion

We will not provide cover and We shall not be liable to pay any claim or provide any benefit under this policy to the extent that the provision of such cover, payment of such claim or provision of such benefit would expose Us to any sanction, prohibition or restriction under any applicable sanctions law or regulations [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-annual-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md#p9].

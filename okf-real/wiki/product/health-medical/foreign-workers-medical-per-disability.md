## Surgical Benefits

The Surgeon’s fees reimbursable shall be either the actual fees for the Surgery or an amount obtained by multiplying the maximum benefit payable as per the Schedule of Benefits with the percentage number payable according to the group under which the Surgery is classified in the surgical schedule of fees, whichever is the lower [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md#p5].

If two or more surgical procedures are performed during the course of a single operation through the same incision, benefits will be allowed only for surgical procedure having the higher limit [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md#p5].

## 15. Payment of Benefits

Any benefits payable under this Policy shall be paid to You or the Insured Person. Your receipt, or the Insured Person’s receipt, of any benefit payable under this Policy shall in all cases be deemed final and complete discharge of all Our liability [src:raw/wordings/foreign-workers-medical-per-disability-policy-wording-1-july-2025.md#p11].

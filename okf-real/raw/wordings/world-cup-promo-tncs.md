## Campaign Details and Eligibility

3. Participation in the Campaign is open to all individuals aged 70 and below during the Campaign Period. An individual who meets the age criterion and participates in the Campaign shall hereinafter be referred to as a participant (' Participant ').
4. To  participate  in  the  Campaign,  each  Participant  must  fulfil  all  of  the  following requirements:
- a. visit www.etiqa.com.sg/worldcup and complete the quiz comprising:
- i. three (3) multiple-choice football trivia questions; and
- ii. one (1) open-ended question titled:
6. 'Tell us which team you're backing this World Cup and what makes them special to you.'

(collectively referred to as the ' Campaign Entry ');

- b. provides his or her personal details (including name and contact details) and complete all compulsory fields in the Campaign registration form:
- c. provides his/her Marketing Consent (as set out in the Campaign registration form) to Etiqa; and
- d. acts in good faith and submit an original Campaign Entry that does not infringe the rights of any third party.
5. Only Campaign Entries from Participants who have correctly answered all three (3) multiple-choice football trivia questions shall be eligible for prize consideration.

From such eligible entries, the top three (3) Campaign Entries, as determined by Etiqa in  its  sole  and  absolute  discretion  based  on  creativity,  originality,  and  emotional engagement of the open-ended response, shall each receive one (1) PlayStation 5 Slim local set worth approximately Singapore Dollars Seven Hundred Ninety-Nine (S$799) (' Prize ').

6. Eligible winners shall be notified by Etiqa via phone call, email, WhatsApp, or such other mode  as  Etiqa  deems  appropriate  within  thirty  (30)  business  days  following  the Campaign Period, or such later date as determined by Etiqa.
7. The Prize must be collected in accordance with Etiqa's instructions. Failure to collect the  Prize  by  the  stipulated  date  may  result  in  forfeiture  of  the  Prize  at  Etiqa's  sole discretion.

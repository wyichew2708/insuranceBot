These supplementary terms should be read together with the General Provisions for Life Insurance. In the event of a
conflict between the supplementary terms for eEASY savepro and the General Provisions for Life Insurance, the
supplementary terms contained herein shall prevail.

2. eEASY savepro has a 7-year premium payment term. You only have to pay the first 2 years’ premiums in full.
After that, we will give you an Automatic Premium Benefit every year for the remaining 5 years. The Automatic
Premium Benefit can only be used to offset your premiums for the remaining 5 years. It cannot be cashed out.
3. eEASY savepro pays a benefit on the death of the life insured during the policy term. This benefit is the sum
of 105% of the total premiums you paid on this basic policy (excluding any advanced premium paid, interest
and Automatic Premium Benefit) and any attaching bonuses, less any outstanding amount owing to us.
Funeral expense financial aid (FEFA), as prescribed in the General Provisions for Life Insurance is not
applicable for this policy.
4. At maturity date, if the policy is still in force, the life insured survives and we have not paid out any claims, we
will pay you the maturity benefit in one lump sum, less any outstanding amount owing to us.
5. The option to convert eEASY savepro to a paid-up policy as prescribed in the General Provisions for Life
Insurance is not available for this policy.
6. eEASY savepro is a participating policy and will share in the profits of our participating fund in the form of
bonus. Bonus is not guaranteed until we have declared to you in writing.
7. eEASY savepro is a guaranteed issuance policy.
8. Your policy will end when one of these events happens first:
i. death of the life insured;
ii. we paid out 100% of the death benefit of this policy;

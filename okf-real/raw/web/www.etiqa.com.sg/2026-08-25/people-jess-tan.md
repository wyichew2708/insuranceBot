# Jess Tan

## Jess Tan

Jess is responsible for shaping the strategic direction of Partnerships Distribution at Etiqa Singapore, leveraging over 20 years of experience in the financial services industry. In her capacity, Jess nurtures Etiqa’s robust partner relationships within the Bancassurance, Independent Financial Advisory, and Advisory channels.

The Distribution Alliance team is also under Jess’ care and she plays a pivotal role in talent development. Jess holds an MBA in Business Administration from the University of Roehampton, London.

## Frequently Asked Questions

1. When should I file a home insurance claim?

If the damage/theft/incident is significant, you should file a claim immediately.

2. How do I file a claim?

If your home or property has sustained any kind of damage or loss, please take a photo of the damage and contact us immediately.

3. Is the structure of my building covered if damage as a result of fire?

Yes, provided your policy includes your building structure.

4. If my neighbour’s home caught fire and the smoke damage my home, can I claim?

Yes, you can claim for smoke damage resulting from a fire under your policy.

5. Can I claim for damages of my contents caused by bursting of common pipes/water pipe from my neighbor staying in the unit above me?

No, you can only claim for damages to your contents caused by bursting of domestic pipes within your home, provided you have taken up home contents.

6. How will Etiqa assess my claim?

We will review the claim submitted and decide whether it is necessary to appoint a loss adjuster to assist us in the investigation and assessment of the claim.

7. What should I do if a loss adjuster is not appointed?

You should, as far as possible, gather all facts and the circumstances giving rise to the loss. These include details of any third party responsible for the damage to your property and photographs indicating the extent of damage.

8. If I caused damage to third party’s property, should I compensate the third party for the damage?

No, you should not negotiate or make any admission of liability, offer, promise or payment without the written consent from Etiqa. You should submit to us immediately upon receipt of any legal letter (Letter of Demand, Writ of Summons) and all correspondences from third party indicating their intention to claim.

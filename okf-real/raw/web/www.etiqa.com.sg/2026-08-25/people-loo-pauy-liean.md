# Loo Pauy Liean

## Loo Pauy Liean

Ms Loo was appointed as an Independent Non-Executive Director of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. on 16 October 2023. She is the Chairman of the Audit Committee of the Board and also serves as a member of Risk Management Committee of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd.

Ms Loo has about 30 years of working experience in the financial sector, holding various leadership positions with experience in actuarial, product development, internal audit, risk management, compliance and human resource.

Ms Loo was previously Head of Internal Audit of AIA Singapore Pte Ltd, and subsequently Chief Risk Officer of AIA Singapore Pte, Ltd, as well as Compliance Controller of Aviva Limited. Ms Loo was also previously with the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) for about 12 years in various capacities, her last appointment being Director, Insurance Department. During her stint with MAS, she had participated at various committees and workgroups of the International Association of Insurance Supervisors. She holds a Master of Science in Financial Management from University of London and Bachelor of Science in Actuarial Science from London School of Economics and Political Science.

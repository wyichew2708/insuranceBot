# Shahrul Izwan Zaini

## Shahrul Izwan Zaini

Izwan leads the organisation’s human capital strategies, supporting business growth through effective people and talent initiatives. With over 15 years of experience within the Maybank Group, he previously held the role of Human Capital Director at Etiqa General Takaful Berhad (EGTB) and Etiqa Family Takaful Berhad (EFTB)

Izwan holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Quantity Surveying from the International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM) and is a Chartered Member of the Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (CIPD).

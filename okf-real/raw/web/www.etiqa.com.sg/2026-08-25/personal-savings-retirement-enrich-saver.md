# Enrich saver

## Enrich saver

Achieve your goals with a shorter commitment. Make your money work harder with Enrich saver, a capital-guaranteed endowment plan that will see you receive a lump sum payout at the end of 10 years.

## Premium term

## What you can get

Short payment period

Pay for just 5 years and receive a lump sum payout after 10 years 1 .

No health check-up

You can start immediately on your savings journey, with no health checks required for the basic plan.

After 10 years, you will receive a lump sum benefit consisting of a guaranteed and a non-guaranteed amount. This means that you could enjoy returns of up to 2.64% and 0.75% per annum upon maturity, based on an investment rate of return of 4.25% per annum and 3.00% per annum respectively.

Ensure that your loved ones are looked after, as death coverage is provided throughout the 10 years of the policy term.

Your capital is fully guaranteed at the end of 10 policy years.

1 Automatic Premium Benefit (APB) will pay the premiums of the policy from the 6 th to 10 th policy year after the full premium for the first 5 years have been received. Please refer to the policy contract for full details of the terms and conditions.

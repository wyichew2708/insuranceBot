# Essential whole life cover

## Essential whole life cover

Whatever life throws in your way, Etiqa’s comprehensive whole life insurance ensures financial security for you and your loved ones.

## Policy type

Whole life plan

## Policy term

## Premium term

5, 10, 15 or 20 years

## What you can get

Enjoy continuous protection against death, total & permanent disability (until age 70) and terminal illness. You will have the option to multiply your protection up to 400% of the basic sum insured, with a choice of duration till age 65 or  80.

Cash for retirement

When it’s time to enjoy your golden years, boost your retirement income! Choose to receive regular income 1 from your policy for 10 years after you turn 65.

Flexible premium term

Choose to pay your premiums over 5, 10, 15 or 20 years, depending on your financial needs.

Guaranteed future insurability 1

Need more protection as you progress in life? Grow your coverage with additional insurance from Etiqa such as when you graduate from tertiary education, get married or welcome a child – you don’t need to show further proof of good health.

## Enhance your protection with these add-ons

Early CI protection rider

Get advanced payout for death benefit of the basic policy 1 if you are diagnosed with any of the 35 covered early, intermediate or severe stages of critical illnesses.

1 Receive 12 additional monthly payouts if you are diagnosed with any of the 35 covered severe-stage critical illnesses.

1 Receive additional payout if you are diagnosed with any of the 23 special conditions.

CI protection rider

Get advanced payout for death benefit of the basic policy 1 if you are diagnosed with any of the 36 covered severe-stage critical illnesses.

Extra secure waiver

We will waive off the premiums of the basic policy up to age 86 or the end of the premium term of the policy, whichever is earlier, if you are diagnosed with any of the 37 covered severe-stage critical illnesses.

Extra payer waiver

We will waive off the premiums of the basic policy before the Life Insured reaches age 25 or the end of the premium term of the policy, whichever is earlier in the event of the Policyholder’s death, diagnosis of total & permanent disability or any of the 37 covered severe-stage critical illnesses.

1 Please refer to the policy contract for full details of the terms and conditions.

# Essential lifetime secure

## Essential lifetime secure

Prepare for the future and ensure peace of mind with comprehensive riders and multiplier benefits.

## Policy type

Whole life plan

## Policy term

## Premium term

## A Plan That Evolves with You:

Guaranteed Protection for the Future

Enjoy high coverage throughout your life, ensuring your loved ones are financially secure even after retirement. Multiply your protection and get minimum death coverage of up to 5 times the basic sum insured.

Reduce Financial Stress in Challenging Times

Receive a premium relief benefit that reduces financial burden in challenging situations like being admitted to intensive care, retrenchment or immediate family member’s death, total permanent disability or terminal illness.

Protection that Adapts to Your Life

Purchase an additional plan without undergoing more health checks, whether you’re graduating from tertiary education, getting married or having a child.

Flexible Premium Options

Plan your finances with confidence with a payment schedule that suits your budget – whether it’s 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, or 30 years.

## Customise Your Protection with Comprehensive Riders

Early CI benefit rider

Get a payout for diagnosis of any covered critical illness at any stage, plus premium waivers for early/intermediate diagnoses.

Get extra 20% of the rider’s sum insured for major cancer, stroke with permanent deficits, or severe heart attack.

Get extra 12 monthly payouts of 1% of the rider’s sum insured for diagnosis of any of the 63 severe-stage critical illnesses.

Get 20% of the rider’s sum insured for diagnosis of any covered special and juvenile conditions 1 , including ADHD.

CI benefit rider

Receive an advanced payout for up to 100% of the rider’s sum insured if diagnosed with any of the 63 covered severe-stage critical illnesses.

Extra secure waiver

Maintain your coverage without paying premiums if diagnosed with one of the 37 severe-stage critical illnesses.

Extra payer waiver

Stop paying premiums for the basic policy if Policyholder dies, becomes permanently disabled, or is diagnosed with any of the 37 severe-stage critical illnesses before Life Insured turns 25 or the premium term ends.

1 The maximum aggregate amount is S$25,000 per life insured per covered condition. Each covered condition can only be paid once and a maximum of 6 claims can be made under this benefit.

## Here’s How It Works

John, Sophie’s newborn, is insured for S$100,000 under the Essential lifetime secure plan with a S$50,000 Early CI benefit rider and 5x multiplier, covering him until age 81. Sophie pays S$1,646 annually for 25 years.

Emma, a 30-year-old non-smoker, buys the Essential lifetime secure plan with S$150,000 coverage, along with the Early CI and CI benefit riders, each at S$50,000 and a 3x multiplier until age 71. She pays S$5,066.50 annually for 20 years.

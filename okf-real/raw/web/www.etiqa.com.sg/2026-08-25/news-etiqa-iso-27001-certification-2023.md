## Etiqa attains ISO 27001 certification on 24 March 2023

Share this page

Singapore, 19 May 2023 – Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. has attained the internationally recognised ISO/IEC 27001:2013 certification which demonstrates how importantly we view information security. In the face of cybercrime and new digital threats that are constantly emerging, this certification validates Etiqa’s commitment to safeguarding confidentiality, integrity and availability of sensitive information and information systems. Etiqa will continue providing a better customer experience and secured solutions through maintaining our online platforms in compliance with ISO 27001 standards.

About International Organisation for Standardisation

The International Organisation for Standardisation (ISO) is an international standard-setting body composed of representatives from various national standards organisations. ISO/IEC 27001 is the world’s best-known standard for information security management systems (ISMS). This standard provides companies of any size and from all sectors of activity with guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an information security management system. Conformity with ISO/IEC 27001 means that an organisation or business has put in place a system to manage risks related to the security of data owned or handled by the company, and that this system respects all the best practices and principles enshrined in this International Standard.

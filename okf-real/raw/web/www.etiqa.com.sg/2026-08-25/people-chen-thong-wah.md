# Chen Thong Wah

## Chen Thong Wah

Chen Thong Wah leads the Product and ILP Funds Solutions, Product Delivery and Life Pricing at Etiqa Singapore. As Chief Product Officer, Thong Wah brings with him more than 15 years of experience in product development, implementation, pricing and valuation. He spent the early phase of his career as an actuary in Life pricing and valuations and shifted to Product Development since 2017.

Thong Wah currently holds a Bachelor of Business (Actuarial Studies) degree from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore and is a Fellow at the Institute & Faculty of Actuaries, United Kingdom.

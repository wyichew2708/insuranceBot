## Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I am eligible for Etiqa’s CES?

Simply fill in the form with your corporate email address. If you are eligible, we will send you an access link.

Yes, I’m eligible for Etiqa’s CES! How do I make use of the perks?

Use the access link sent to your corporate email address to view the promotions. On checkout, you will be prompted for your corporate email address and promo code to enjoy the discounts.

I’m not eligible for Etiqa’s CES programme. How can I sign up?

Simply inform your HR representative to fill in the form for us to get in touch with them.

Is there a limit on the number of times I can use the promotion codes?

No, there is no limit as long as your company is enrolled in Etiqa’s CES and you are using your corporate email to purchase the policies.

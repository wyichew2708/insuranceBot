# Arvind Shahi

## Arvind Shahi

Arvind comes with more than 30 years of experience in Banking and Insurance sector in the areas of risk management, shareholder reporting and finance.

Prior to joining Etiqa Singapore, he was Chief Risk Officer in Ageas Federal Life Insurance since 2007 and had implemented an enterprise-wide risk management framework, advised boards and regulators, and championed a culture of risk-based decision-making. He has also served briefly as Regional Director, Risk Management in Ageas Asia, Hong Kong.

Arvind is a science graduate and holds a degree in Management Accountancy from the Institute of Cost Accountants of India. He is also a Certified Information System Auditor (CISA) from ISACA, USA.

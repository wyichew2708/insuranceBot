# Enrich flex plus

## Enrich flex plus

Enrich flex plus, an endowment plan designed to help you save for the long term while enjoying the flexibility to support your key milestones in life. You also have the assurance that your policy will continue for your loved ones with a secondary life insured option.

## Product type

## Policy term

Up to age 125

## Premium term

3, 5, 10, 15 or 20 years

## What you can get

Attractive returns for your saving needs

Enjoy guaranteed maturity yield of up to 1.65% p.a. with potential total maturity yield of up to 3.95% p.a. 1

Protection over all walks of life

Receive a lump sum payout upon death or terminal illness during the policy term, and additional payout upon accidental death (until age 80).

Flexibility for key milestones in life

Have the option to withdraw 2 your accumulated cash value from the policy anytime you need it.

No health check necessary

Hassle-free with no health checks required for this guaranteed issuance basic plan.

Policy continuity and ownership options

Option to add another person as the secondary life insured so that your policy will continue for your loved ones after you pass on.

Flexibility with premium deferment

You can request for a premium deferment 2 for up to one year after the end of the 4 th policy year (not applicable for a 3-year premium term) if your policy has sufficient cash value.

Lump sum benefit upon maturity

You or your appointed secondary life insured can expect a lump sum benefit payout on the policy anniversary immediately before you reach 125 years old.

Option to enhance your coverage with these add-on riders:

- Extra payer waiver II

- Extra secure waiver II

Your capital is 100% guaranteed from as early as policy year 5 to 15, depending on your premium term.

## Enhance your protection with these add-ons

Extra secure waiver II

Continue your policy without paying premiums upon the life insured’s diagnosis of any of the 37 covered severe-stage critical illnesses.

Extra payer waiver II

Continue your policy without paying premiums upon death, total and permanent disability or diagnosis of any of the 37 covered severe-stage critical illnesses of the policyholder (before age 86).

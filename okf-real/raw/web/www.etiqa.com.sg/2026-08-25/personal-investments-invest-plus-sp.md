## What you can get

Power-up bonuses to boost your investment

Enjoy a boost to your investment every 3 years with a power-up bonus 1 of up to 1.20% of the average initial account value and the average additional account value of each top-up (if applicable) for the preceding 3 years before each payout date 2 .

No health check-up

You can start immediately on your investment journey, with no health check required for the basic plan.

Access to exclusive reputable funds

Ensure that your money works hard for you by relying on the expertise of professional managers. You can access funds managed by reputable regional and global asset management companies, including institutional funds from as low as S$10,000.

Flexibility to change Life insured 1

Enjoy the flexibility to change Life insured for an unlimited number of times if your policy is issued under a corporation and once, if your policy is issued under an individual.

Switch funds at no charge

Enjoy the flexibility to switch portfolio funds and ILP sub-funds at any time without charges 3 .

Be covered against death at the higher of 101% of net premiums 4 or account value less any outstanding amounts.

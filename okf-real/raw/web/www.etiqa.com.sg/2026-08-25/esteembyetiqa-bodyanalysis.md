# SportsMed

SportsMed focuses on helping individuals move better, recover more effectively, and perform at their best. Their approach combines modern sports recovery techniques with advanced movement and posture analysis to provide personalised insights tailored to each individual’s need.

Through targeted breathing techniques, modernised static analysis using advanced assessment technology, and sports posture correction, SportsMed identifies movement patterns and areas for improvement to support better body alignment, recovery and overall performance.

## What you can enjoy:

Complimentary Comprehensive Assessment (worth S$198) on your first visit.

InBody Composition Analysis Report A state-of-the-art body composition analysis technological device which accurately measures body fat, muscle mass and water content. Foot Pressure Analysis The Foot Pressure Analysis uses pressure plate technology to evaluate weight distribution and gait patterns, identifying imbalances and areas of excessive pressure to support improved posture and movement efficiency.

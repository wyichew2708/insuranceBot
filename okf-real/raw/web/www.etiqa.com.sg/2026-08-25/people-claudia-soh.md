# Claudia Soh

## Claudia Soh

With more than 20 years in the financial services and insurance industry, Claudia’s professional experience spans across finance, strategic planning, risk management, M&A, investor relations and auditing.

Prior to joining the private sector, Claudia spent 5 years at the Monetary Authority of Singapore, focusing on the supervision of financial institutions. Claudia is an accountant by training and holds a Bachelor of Accountancy (1st Class Honours) from the Nanyang Technological University.

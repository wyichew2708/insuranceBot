# Changes to top-up crediting rate

## Changes to top-up crediting rate

CHANGE TO THE TOP-UP CREDITING RATE, EFFECTIVE FROM 1ST JULY 2021 (INCLUSIVE)

Effective from 1 st  July 2021 (inclusive) onwards, the prevailing crediting rate for all top-ups will be revised to 1.20% p.a. for the following products:

- ELASTIQ (for policies issued from 18 July 2019 (inclusive) onwards)
- eSAVE advance
- Enrich advance

Please refer to the  Frequently Asked Questions  for more information.

Individuals may log in to their online self-service portal via Etiqa’s website ( www.etiqa.com.sg/login ) to view their policy details.

For further clarification, customers may contact Etiqa customer care via call or WhatsApp messaging at +65 6887 8777, during Mondays to Fridays, from 8.45am till 5.30pm, or email  customer.service@etiqa.com.sg .

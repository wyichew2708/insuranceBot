## Work Injury Compensation

## Make a Work Injury Compensation Claim

## 1

- iReport to Ministry of Manpower
- Completed claim form (only if iReport is not required)
- Copy of Employee’s work permit/NRIC/appointment letter
- Medical invoices and certificates
- Death Certificate (for fatal injury)
- Injured employee’s earnings for the 12 months immediately prior to the accident
- Contract agreement for yourself and the main contractor (Note: Please confirm whether the main contractor/Principal has obtained separate insurance for all workers on the project. If yes, please provide insurance details and project policy.)
- Inpatient discharge summary/medical reports/radiology reports, if any
- Incident report or witness statements, if any
- Traffic Police Report in the event of traffic accident
- Traffic Investigation Report in the event of traffic accident

## Frequently Asked Questions

1) When and how should an Employer file an injury report?

- For Fatal accidents Notify MOM as soon as possible.
- Submit an iReport within 10 days of notification of accident.
- For non-fatal accidents Submit an iReport within 10 days of notification of accident.
- Employees can claim compensation within one year of accident, or up to the limitation period of the Occupational Disease even if they no longer work for the employer.

2) What should an Employee do when a work-related accident happens?

The Employee should notify their Employer as soon as possible after the accident, so that the Employer can lodge an injury report (iReport) to MOM.

3) What happens after the Employer submits an incident report?

MOM will forward a copy of the incident report to the Employer’s insurer.

4) What kind of compensation are Employee entitled to?

The Employee will be able to claim for the following:

a) Compensation for medical leave wages and light duties

b) Compensation for medical expenses

Employer will pay for the medical expenses related to the work accident up to a maximum limit of S$45,000 or 1 year from the date of the accident, whichever comes first.

c) Compensation for permanent incapacity or death

Etiqa Singapore is helmed by an experienced team of leaders who puts your financial security first.

BOARD OF DIRECTORS

The Board of Directors has the overall responsibility for maintaining the long-term success of the company. This includes ensuring a strong executive leadership, relevance and effectiveness in the governance and achieving the highest standards of business integrity, ethics and professionalism.

Non-Independent Non-Executive Director

Puan Fauziah was appointed as a Non-Independent Non-Executive Director and Chairman of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. on 1 March 2024.

She is a Fellow Member of The Chartered Governance Institute, United Kingdom and Qualified Risk Director, Institute of Enterprise Risk Practitioners, Malaysia.

Puan Fauziah has over 30 years of banking experience having served in senior management positions in J.P. Morgan Chase Bank, Standard Chartered Bank and Australia & New Zealand Banking Group in Malaysia including serving as the Chief Executive Officer of J.P. Morgan Chase Bank Berhad. She served as the Chairman of the board of J.P. Morgan Chase Bank Berhad, post her retirement in January 2015 prior to joining the Malayan Banking Berhad Board in May 2018.

Within the Maybank Group, Puan Fauziah also sits on the board of Maybank Ageas Holdings Berhad, Etiqa International Holdings Sdn Bhd and Maybank Philippines Inc. Her board responsibilities include being Chairman of the Credit Review Committee in Malayan Banking Berhad, Chairman of Maybank Philippines Inc and Chairman of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of Maybank Ageas Holdings Berhad.

Puan Fauziah is also on the board of directors of Agensi Kaunseling & Pengurusan Kredit (AKPK), a body set up by Bank Negara Malaysia in 2006 with the objective of providing financial education and advisory, debt counselling and debt management for households and SMEs.

Non-Independent Non-Executive Director

En. Kamal was appointed as Non-Independent Non-Executive Director of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. on 26 November 2013.

He holds a B. Sc (Hons) Actuarial Science from the University of Kent, England.

En Kamal has more than 30 years of financial services industry experience with large Malaysian corporations and a multinational company. He started his career in 1990 with six years of regulatory and risk management experience with Securities Commission Malaysia and Bank Negara Malaysia. Thereafter he joined several corporations including an investment bank and a multinational insurance company between 1996 and 2012. He was the Chief Executive Officer of Etiqa Insurance Berhad from 2012 to 2014, and has been the Chief Executive Officer of Maybank Ageas Holdings Bhd, the holding company of Etiqa Insurance Pte Ltd since 2013.

Within the Maybank Group, En Kamal also sits on the board of Etiqa General Insurance (Cambodia). He is also currently the Chairman of Etiqa Digital Solutions Sdn Bhd and the President Commissioner of PT Asuransi Etiqa Internasional Indonesia.

Independent Non-Executive Director

Mr Francis was appointed as an Independent Non-Executive Director of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. on 27 December 2022. He is also a member of the Investment Oversight Committee and Audit Committee of the Board of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd.

He has a Master of Business Administration from Schulich School of Business, York University, Canada and recently completed his Master of Technology in Intelligent Systems, Institute of Systems Science from National University of Singapore.

Mr Francis is currently a Director of Weezard Pte Ltd and the Managing Director of Neubio Medial Pte Ltd.

Non-Independent Non-Executive Director

Mr Coremans was appointed as a Non-Independent Non-Executive Director of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. on 28 September 2023. He is the Chairman of the Investment Oversight Committee and also serves as a member of the Risk Management Committee of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd.

Mr Coremans holds a Master of Business Administration Degree in International Business Finance, a Master’s degree in Actuarial Sciences and a Bachelor Degree in Applied Economics, all from Catholic University of Leuven, Belgium.

He has been active in the Insurance industry for 30 years, his entire professional career. He joined ING Insurance Belgium in 1990 where he became Deputy Director overseeing the Save- and Investment product lines both in Insurance and banking. In 1998, he was appointed as Corporate Controller in KBC Insurance Belgium and joined Ageas by the end of 2002.

At Ageas, Mr Coremans held various senior management positions in the Asian entities. He has been Executive Director and CFO of operations in Malaysia until 2007, was the CFO of IDBI Federal Life insurance company (current: Ageas Federal Life Insurance Co Ltd) in India until 2009 and was then appointed Regional CFO/CRO of Ageas Asia overseeing the finance, investment, risk, and actuarial domains for Asia region. He served as Non-Executive Director on the various Boards of operations in Belgium, Portugal, Turkey, China, India, Hong Kong, Malaysia and Thailand.

With effect from 1 July 2014, Mr Coremans joined Ageas Group Executive Committee as Chief Risk Officer and executive director of ageas SA NV, with responsibility for Compliance, Legal, Risk, Human Resources, IT and Office Support. As of June 2019, he took up the role of Chief Development Officer with responsibility for Human Resources, Business Development and Technology development. As of November 2020, he was appointed Managing Director of Asia.

Furthermore, he serves as Non-Executive Director on the boards of Ageas Federal Life Insurance Co Ltd (India), Taiping Life Insurance Company Limited (China), Muang Thai Life Assurances PCL (Thailand) and Muang Thai Holdings Co Ltd (Thailand).

Loo Pauy Liean

Independent Non-Executive Director

Ms Loo was appointed as an Independent Non-Executive Director of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. on 16 October 2023. She is the Chairman of the Audit Committee of the Board and also serves as a member of Risk Management Committee of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd.

Ms Loo has about 30 years of working experience in the financial sector, holding various leadership positions with experience in actuarial, product development, internal audit, risk management, compliance and human resource.

She holds a Master of Science in Financial Management from University of London and Bachelor of Science in Actuarial Science from London School of Economics and Political Science.

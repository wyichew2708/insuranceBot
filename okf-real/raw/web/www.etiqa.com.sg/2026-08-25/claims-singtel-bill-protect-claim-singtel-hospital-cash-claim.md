## Make a Singtel Hospital Cash Claim

## 1

- Hospitalisation insurance form
- Attending Physician’s Medical Report or in-patient Discharge Summary (IPDS)
- Medical Certificate issued by the medical practitioner following discharge after hospitalization
- Copy of your monthly Singtel Bill Additional document if you are hospitalized and retrenched during your hospital confinement:
- Written confirmation from your employer as proof of retrenchment Note: The above documents required is not intended to be exhaustive. We reserve our rights to request for additional documents for claim assessment, if necessary.

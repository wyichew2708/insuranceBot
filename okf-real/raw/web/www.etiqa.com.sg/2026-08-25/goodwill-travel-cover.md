# Jetstar Asia Operations Cessation

Jetstar Asia Operations Cessation – Important Notice for Travel Insurance Policyholders

Following Jetstar Asia’s cessation of operations, Etiqa Insurance Singapore is extending goodwill benefits to eligible customers who purchased our travel insurance plans (Travel Infinite or Tiq Travel) before 8:00 AM on 11 June 2025 and have been affected by the airline’s disruptions.

For flights affected on or before 31 July 2025:

If you are travelling on or before 31 July 2025, your insurance policy remains the same, and you may submit claims for Travel Delay if your original flight schedule is delayed or diverted.

For flights affected after 31 July 2025:

Goodwill benefits apply only to travels affected after 31 July 2025.

You can claim for non-refundable pre-booked accommodation, local transportation (excluding Jetstar flight costs), and travel activities. Kindly refer to the following table for more information.

To submit your claims, kindly download the claim form here and submit via email to travelclaims@etiqa.com.sg by 14 September 2025, 11:59 PM with the relevant supporting documents.

For assistance, please contact our Customer Care team at +65 6887 8777 or customer.service@etiqa.com.sg .

## Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who is eligible for the Jetstar Asia goodwill benefits?

You’re eligible for the goodwill benefits if you meet all of the following criteria:

- Purchased a Tiq Travel or Travel Infinite Insurance policy on or before 8:00 am on 11 June 2025
- Purchased a Jetstar Asia flight booking on or before 8:00 am on 11 June 2025
- Received an email notification from Jetstar that your booking is impacted
- Your travel date must be after 31 July 2025

2. My flight is scheduled on/before 31 July 2025. Am I eligible for goodwill benefits?

While you are not eligible for goodwill benefits as your flight is on/before 31 July 2025, your Tiq Travel Insurance policy coverage still applies. You can submit the relevant claims for your travel plans and other insured events within 30 days of the incident via our online portal here .

3. What documents do I need to submit for goodwill benefit claims?

Upon your verification when you submit a claim, verify your eligibility and identify which benefit you should claim under.

For Air Fare Reimbursement:

- Invoice/receipts of your original Jetstar booking
- Refund statement from Jetstar
- Invoice/receipts of alternative air ticket purchased
- Boarding pass for alternative air ticket
- Additional proof if upgrading flight class (to maintain same travel dates) For Non-Refundable Expenses Reimbursement:

- Invoice/receipts of your original Jetstar booking
- Refund statement from Jetstar
- All prepaid booking receipts/invoices for accommodation, activities, tours before 11 June 2025
- Statement from service providers confirming no refund/partial refund available

4. How do I submit my goodwill benefit claim for my travels after 31 July 2025?

Step 1: Download the manual claim form here .

Step 2: Complete the form with all required information and relevant supporting documents

Step 3: Submit the form and supporting documents via email to travelclaims@etiqa.com.sg by 14 September 2025, 11:59 PM

5. What is not covered under the goodwill benefits?

The following expenses are NOT covered:

- Jetstar flight ticket costs (these should be full refunded from Jetstar)
- Accommodation or other expenses if your travel period remains unchanged
- Claims for flights scheduled on/before 31 July 2025
- Expenses that can be refunded by the original service providers
- Upgrades or changes made for personal preference rather than necessity

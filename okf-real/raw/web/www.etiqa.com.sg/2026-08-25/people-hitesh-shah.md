# Hitesh Shah

## Hitesh Shah

Mr Shah was appointed as an Independent Non-Executive Director of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. on 1 September 2024. He is the Chairman of the Risk Management Committee and also serves as a member of the Audit Committee of the Board of Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd.

Mr Shah brings over 30 years of experience in the financial services industry, with leadership roles at Manulife, Aviva, Prudential, Deloitte, AXA Australia, and National Mutual New Zealand. His expertise spans actuarial science, product development, customer experience, strategy, and marketing.

In recent years, Mr Shah has also served as an advisor to various start-up companies, providing strategic guidance to help them scale and succeed.

Mr Shah currently holds several non-executive board positions, including:

– Vice President, Singapore Actuarial Society – Member Non-Executive Director, Institute and Faculty of Actuaries Board (UK) – Independent Non-Executive Director, SCOR Reinsurance Asia Pacific Pte Ltd

He is a Fellow of Institute of Actuaries (UK) and Singapore Actuarial Society. He is a Senior Accredited Director under Singapore Institute of Directors.

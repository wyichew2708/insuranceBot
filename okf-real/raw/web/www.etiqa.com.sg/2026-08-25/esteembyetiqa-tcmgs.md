# Kin Teck Tong

About Kin Teck Tong

Established in 1967, Kin Teck Tong is a renowned homegrown Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) brand from Singapore. From its origins in Chinese Medicine production, Kin Teck Tong has evolved into a network of modern TCM clinics, with 14 branches islandwide today. As Singapore’s first integrated TCM clinic, Kin Teck Tong offers TCM wellness, internal medicine, acupuncture, sports injury and pain management treatments, all utilising advanced medical devices under one company portfolio.

## Revision to Enrich harvest policies crediting rate

Share this page

Effective from the first business day of the month after your Enrich harvest policy enters its seventh policy year, the current crediting rate for your account value will be revised to 0.30%.

Interest rates have been gradually declining, while global financial markets face persistent investors’ concerns about possible economic recession. These negative sentiments led to higher market volatility and impacted our asset yields. As a result, the crediting rates have been revised downward accordingly to ensure they are at a more sustainable level.

For the avoidance of doubt, if the seventh policy year falls on the first business day of the month, the revised crediting rate will take effect on that day; and if the seventh policy year falls on any other day of the month, the revised crediting rate will take effect on the first business day of the following month.

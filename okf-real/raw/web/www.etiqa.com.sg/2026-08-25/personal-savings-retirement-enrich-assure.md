# Enrich assure

## Enrich assure

Sticking to a regular savings plan can sometimes be challenging. Enrich assure is our capital-guaranteed endowment plan designed to help you achieve your savings goals in 12 years.

## Product type

## Policy term

12, 21 or 26 years

## Premium term

7 years 1

## What you can get

Flexible maturity payments

Enjoy the assurance of your capital being fully guaranteed, and the flexibility of receiving your maturity payment in:

- A lump sum at the end of the 12 th policy year, or
- 10 or 15 yearly payments starting from the end of the 12 th policy year

No health check-up

You can start immediately on your savings journey, with no health checks required for the basic plan.

Short payment period and capital guaranteed

Pay for just 7 years 1 and be assured that your capital is 100% guaranteed at the end of 12 policy years.

Ensure that your loved ones are looked after, as death coverage is provided for the duration of the policy.

## Enhance your protection with this add-on

eXTRA cancer waiver rider

With this rider, you gain peace of mind knowing that if you are diagnosed with a major cancer, we’ll take care of the premiums until the end of the policy term or policy anniversary before age 86, whichever is earlier.

1 Automatic Premium Benefit (APB) will pay the premiums of the policy from the 8th policy year onwards after the full premium for the first 7 years has been received.

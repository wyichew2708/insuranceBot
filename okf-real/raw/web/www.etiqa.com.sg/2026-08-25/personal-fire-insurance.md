# HDB Fire Insurance

## HDB Fire Insurance​

Appointed Insurer for the HDB Fire Insurance Scheme

## Why do you need HDB Fire Insurance?

## Mandatory insurance

Homeowners with HDB loans commencing on or after 1 September 1994 are required to buy and renew HDB Fire Insurance every 5 years, for the entire loan period.

## Covers damages to building structure

Covers damage caused by fire to the building, structures, fixtures, and fittings provided by HDB and its approved developers.

## Extended benefits

Includes extension to cover fires in adjoining properties and water damage from burst pipes.

## Optional enhanced home content insurance

Boost coverage for your home contents and renovation by adding on Tiq Home Insurance.

## Premiums & Coverage

## What does HDB Fire Insurance cover?

It is compulsory for flat owners with HDB housing loan to buy HDB Fire Insurance. Please note that it only covers the internal building structures, fixtures and fittings provided by HDB and its approved developers, but excludes household contents, renovation and personal belongings. You may enhance your coverage with Tiq Home Insurance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What does HDB Fire Insurance from Etiqa cover?

The HDB Fire Insurance Scheme was introduced to help relieve flat owners of the financial burden of repair work in the unfortunate event of a fire. The HDB Fire Insurance covers the cost of reinstating damaged internal structures, fixtures, as well as areas built and provided by HDB caused by fire and extraneous perils. It does not include home contents such as furniture, renovations and personal belongings.

Etiqa Insurance Pte. Ltd. is the appointed insurer from 16 August 2024 for the HDB Fire Insurance Scheme. HDB Fire Insurance is valid for a 5-year period, and renewal is done once every 5 years.

What’s the difference between HDB Fire Insurance and Tiq Home Insurance?

HDB Fire insurance covers fire damage to building structures, fixtures, and fittings provided by the HDB or its approved developers.

Tiq Home insurance offers a wider coverage as follows:

- loss or damage to home contents and renovations including the cost of removing debris
- emergency cash allowance which can be used for alternative accommodation expenses and pay for basic necessities such as food, clothes, transport
- accidental breakage of mirrors and fixed glass
- any legal liability for the loss or damage you accidentally cause
- home assistance services such as electrical, plumbing, locksmithing, and pest control (for 3 or 5 year plans only) To find out more about HDB Fire insurance and Tiq Home insurance, you can refer to:

What is the HDB scheme account number / HDB Reference Number?

Your HDB Scheme Account number is the 11-digit HDB reference number / scheme account number found on the top right hand corner of your HDB Letter.

You’ll need your HDB Reference Number on-hand if you’d like to buy or renew your HDB Fire insurance. Here’s where to find it:

Please enter your 11-digit HDB reference number / scheme account number found on the top right corner of the HDB Appointment Letter for Taking Possession of the Flat.

Please enter your 11-digit HDB reference number found on the top left corner of the appointment letter for resale completion.

Renewal of Fire Insurance

Please enter your 11-digit HDB reference number found on the top left corner of the HDB renewal notice / reminder letter(s) for HDB Fire Insurance.

Why do you need to collect my NRIC and date of birth (DOB)?

NRIC and Date of Birth are required to verify the identity of each individual claimant.

How do I renew my Fire Insurance?

To renew your HDB Fire insurance, please follow these steps:

#1: If your HDB Fire insurance policy is due for renewal and you have received a HDB renewal notice/reminder letter from HDB

- Step 1: You can proceed to renew your HDB Fire insurance here , via AXS kiosks or AXS Mobile App by providing details such as address or HDB Reference No. and your personal information.
- Step 2: Afterwards, you will be provided an option to add on Tiq Home insurance to enjoy the full coverage as shown below before proceeding to make payment for your HDB Fire insurance

#2: If your HDB Fire insurance policy has expired

- Check if you have fully paid your housing loan with HDB. If you have not fully paid your housing loan with HDB, you are required to purchase a new HDB Fire insurance policy with us here .
- If you have fully paid your house loan, it is optional for you to purchase a new HDB Fire insurance policy with us here .

I have my existing home insurance, do I still need to buy HDB Fire Insurance from Etiqa?

Yes, as long as you have an outstanding HDB housing loan, this is mandatory and required by Housing & Development Board (HDB).

Do I have to inform HDB after buying the HDB Fire Insurance from Etiqa?

No. You do not have to inform HDB. HDB will be informed upon successful payment and purchase of the HDB Fire Insurance. You will also receive the Certificate of Insurance which will be sent to your email entered during the application.

How do I submit a claim?

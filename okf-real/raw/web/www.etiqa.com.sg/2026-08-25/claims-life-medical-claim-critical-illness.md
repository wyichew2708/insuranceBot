## Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I file for a claim?

Click here to make a claim.

2. How would the death claim proceeds be paid?

Death claim proceeds will be paid to nominee if there is a nomination or the assignee if the policy is assigned.

3. What if I have not make any nomination?

The claim proceeds will be paid to a Proper Claimant under Section 61 of the Insurance Act for an amount up to $150,000 without the production of the Grant of Probate or Grant of Letters of Administration (at the sole discrection of the insurer) and any remaining claim proceeds will be paid upon production of the Grant of Probate or Grant of Letters of Administration of the deceased estate.

Proper Claimant refers to executor, widow, widower, parent, child, siblings, nephew or niece of the deceased.

4. Does the claimant need to apply for the Grant of Probate or the Grant of Letters of Adminstration?

- If there is a nominee or assignee in the policy, the production of the Grant of Probate or the Grant of Letters of Administration is not required for claim settlement.
- If there is no nomination in the policy, please refer to FAQ 3.

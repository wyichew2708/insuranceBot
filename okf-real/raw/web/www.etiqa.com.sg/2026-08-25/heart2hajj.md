## Make your dream of Hajj a reality

In this heartfelt session, well-loved influencer Sujimy Mohamad will share how disciplined savings can help every Muslim prepare for one of life’s most meaningful journeys, the Hajj. For many, the waiting period can stretch from 10 to 20 years, and when the call finally comes, being financially unprepared could mean missing out on this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity.

Sujimy will reveal his personal approach to purposeful financial planning, showing how commitment and discipline can make the dream of Hajj a reality, while also enabling us to reach other important milestones along the way. Joined by inspiring voices Sainava Bee Bee and Norsuria Jani, this warm session offers practical insights, heartfelt stories, and motivation to take charge of your savings today, so that when the time comes, you and your loved ones are ready to embrace this sacred journey with peace of mind.

## About our speakers

Sainava Bee Bee

Head of Takaful Distribution at Etiqa Insurance Singapore

With over 25 years in the insurance and financial services sector, Bee Bee is a seasoned wealth management professional who is passionate about growth and improvement across all functions. Also serving as Lead for MENDAKI’s Banking and Finance Network, she champions the return of Takaful through ethical, inclusive, and values-based Islamic finance solutions.

Managing Director of ScreenBox and veteran content creator

Known for his storytelling and content creation, Sujimy has spent 38 years in the media industry and built a following of over 840,000. He shares stories, family values, and life inspiration, and now champions future planning, drawing from his own experience of preparing for major life milestones such as supporting his children’s education. He believes every important milestone should be guided by knowledge and thoughtful preparation.

Ustazah Norsuria Jani

ARS-certified Takaful Distribution Manager at Etiqa Insurance Singapore

Norsuria is a legal and Shariah expert in Islamic finance with extensive experience across insurance, fund management and regulatory finance. At Etiqa Insurance, she leads Shariah governance and Takaful product development.

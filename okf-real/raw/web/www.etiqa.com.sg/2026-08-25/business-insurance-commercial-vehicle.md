## Frequently Asked Questions

Have more questions? Read our FAQs.

What vehicle types can be insured under Commercial Vehicle Insurance?

Commercial Vehicle Insurance is offered for vehicles used for commercial purposes and registered either under an individual’s name or a company.

Coverage is available for vehicle types such as lorries, station wagons, pick-ups, vans and refrigerated vehicles. Vehicles used for hire, such as Grab ride-hailing services, are not covered under this policy.

Who can buy Etiqa's Commercial Vehicle insurance?

To be eligible to purchase Commercial Vehicle Insurance,

- For company-owned vehicles: The vehicle is registered under a company that you own, with a valid Company or Business Registration Number.
- For individually-owned vehicles: You are the main driver and the vehicle is registered under your name. The policyholder and any named drivers are Singapore residents with a valid NRIC/FIN.
- The vehicle is registered in Singapore.
- Any vehicle modifications must comply with LTA regulations.
- You and any named drivers have not had a driving licence suspended or cancelled within the past 3 years.
- You and any named drivers have not made more than 2 motor insurance claims, or accumulated more than $30,000 in total claims, with any insurer within the past 3 years.
- You have not been refused motor insurance coverage, declined renewal, or had a motor insurance policy cancelled or terminated by an insurer.

Where can I check my demerit points?

To check your demerit points, you may login to your Singpass account via the Singpass mobile app. Navigate to My Profile > Vehicle & Driving Licence , where your demerit points will be displayed under Qualified Driving Licence .

Alternatively, you may check your demerit points via the Singapore Police Force E-Services portal:

- Visit the Singapore Police Force E-Services portal at https://www.police.gov.sg/e-services
- Select Individual > Check Driver Improvement Points
- Log in using your Singpass.
- Your current demerit points will be displayed upon successful verification.

Plans and Coverage

What is the difference between the three different types of plan?

The three plan types provide different coverage levels:

- Comprehensive covers your legal liability to third party’s bodily injury and property damage, as well as loss or damage to your own vehicle due to accidental causes, fire, or theft.

- Third Party Fire and Theft covers your legal liability to third party’s bodily injury and property damage, as well as loss or damage to your own vehicle due to fire or theft only. It does not cover loss or damage to your own vehicle due to accidental causes.

- Third Party covers your legal liability to third party’s bodily injury and property damage only. It does not cover loss or damage to your own vehicle. This is the minimum mandatory coverage for all vehicles in Singapore.

What does "Excess" mean?

Excess is the amount you need to pay towards a claim before your insurance coverage applies. The excess amount is stated in your Policy Schedule and applies to each claimable incident.

For example, if replacing your windscreen costs $1,000 and your windscreen excess is $100, you will pay the first $100, and your insurance will cover the remaining $900 (subject to policy terms and conditions).

Is there an additional excess for young, elderly and inexperienced drivers?

Yes, there will be an additional excess of $4,000 imposed on top of the policy excess for drivers

- aged under 27, or above 75,
- and/or who has held a full driving licence for less than 2 years.

The excess is only applicable to Loss or Damage claims to your own vehicle.

Am I still covered when I drive the vehicle out of Singapore?

Yes, you are covered in West Malaysia and in Thailand(within 80 kilometres of the border between Thailand and West Malaysia).

An additional excess of $2,000 will be imposed on top of the policy excess if the vehicle is stolen whilst driven outside of Singapore.

Can my accident repairs be done at a workshop of my choice?

Yes, you may get your vehicle repaired at your preferred workshop at no additional charge or premium cover.

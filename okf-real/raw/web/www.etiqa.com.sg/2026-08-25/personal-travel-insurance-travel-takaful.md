## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is Takaful?

Takaful refers to the spirit of co-operation and joint responsibility
amongst policyholders according to Shariah principles.
What is Shariah?
It refers to a set of principles under Islamic law. Takaful product(s)
and its services comply with the Shariah principles. We manage and/or
administer Takaful product(s) that are free from usury( riba )
and non-permissible activities such as alcohol, pork, entertainment
and tobacco industries.

Is Travel Takaful Shariah-Certified?

Yes. It is Shariah-certified by an independent Shariah advisory body – Financial Shariah Advisory and Consultancy of Singapore Islamic Scholars & Religious Teachers Association (Pergas). Travel Takaful is Shariah-compliant under the concept of Wakalah (agency), Tabarru’ (donation) and Takaful (mutual guarantee).

Are the activities and processes relating to Travel Takaful Shariah-compliant?

It is an end-to-end Shariah-compliant plan where policyholder contributes monies into a segregated Takaful fund until the policy expires or upon payment of claims. The monies will be allocated and restricted to Shariah-compliant investments only. In addition, the Takaful principles are embedded in the key product document.

This plan is also subjected to review and approval by our Shariah advisory body who ensures that the product continuously complies with the relevant Shariah principles.

Can a Non-Muslim customer sign up for Travel Takaful?

Travel Takaful is open for all. One does not have to be a Muslim to sign up for a Takaful plan and its coverage. Every customer will be able to enjoy all benefits and coverage under Travel Takaful.

In addition, this plan also underscores the elements of ethical and values-based principles.

What are the underlying Shariah principles?

- Wakalah (agency): Policyholders agree to appoint Etiqa to administer the premiums, including the investment of the General Takaful fund and the disbursement of benefits to the entitled policyholders.
- Tabarru’ (donation): Policyholders agree that the remaining portion of the premiums will be allocated as donation for the purposes of mutual support and cooperation. Note: To meet the Shariah requirements, policyholders’ acknowledgement on the following needs to be made upfront:

- On the appointment of Etiqa as an administrator under Wakalah principle;
- On the concept of donation under Tabarru’ principle; and
- On the waiver of excess of Tabarru’ (i.e. underwriting profits) if any and give to Etiqa where it deems fit in administering and/or investing the overall General Takaful fund. For transparency purposes, this utilisation will be subjected to Shariah advisory body review and approval.

Is there a pledge for charity for Takaful product(s)?

In the spirit of Takaful, Etiqa donates 0.1% of the premiums collected from Travel Takaful to Community Chest – at no additional cost to policyholders. This contribution is made by us without affecting policyholders’ premiums or plan benefits.

What is the difference between Travel Takaful and Travel Infinite?

Both travel insurance provides comparable coverage and benefits. The key difference is that Travel Takaful is Shariah-Certified and structured based on Takaful principles, while Travel Infinite follows a conventional insurance structure. The distinction is structural rather than benefit-based.

The policyholder is the person who buys and manages the policy. You may purchase Travel Takaful as a policyholder if:

- you are at least 18 years old on the effective date of the policy, and
- a Singapore Resident (Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident), or a foreigner holding a valid Work Pass, Student Pass, Dependant’s Pass or Long-term Visit Pass.

The insured person is the traveller who is covered and can claim benefits under the policy. The insured person must meet the following criteria:

- a Singapore Resident, or

Both of you decided to travel together from Singapore to Bali for a vacation. You may purchase the Group Plan as the policyholder and include your friend (a transit traveller) as an insured person in your policy. Optional Add-on: Pre-existing Medical Conditions

Does Travel Takaful cover claims related to pre-existing medical conditions?

Travel Takaful base plan does not cover claims related to pre-existing medical conditions. To be covered, you will need to purchase the Pre-existing Medical Conditions add-on, which is available at an additional premium. This add-on is only applicable to Single Trip policies of up to 30 days.

What is covered under the Pre-existing Medical Conditions add-on?

Coverage before the start date of your trip due to pre-existing medical conditions : If you have to cancel your entire trip within 30 days before the commencement date stated in your policy due to a pre-existing medical condition, Travel Takaful covers the transport expenses, accommodation costs and other unused prepaid expenses (such as excursions and tours) that cannot be recovered from the service provider, subject to a 50% co-payment.

If you have to postpone your entire trip due to a pre-existing medical condition occurring within 30 days from the start of the trip, Travel Takaful covers the administrative costs incurred to postpone the trip, subject to a 50% co-payment.

Coverage during your trip due to pre-existing medical conditions : If during your trip overseas, you suffer an acute onset of a pre-existing medical condition, Travel Takaful covers you for:

- overseas medical expenses, emergency medical evacuation & repatriation back to Singapore; and
- irrecoverable unused prepaid expenses because of you cutting short of your journey, subject to a 50% co-payment; or
- additional transportation expenses and accommodations costs incurred because of your trip disruption, subject to a 50% co-payment. Please refer to our Policy Wording for full terms, conditions and exclusions.

How do you define pre-existing medical conditions?

Pre-existing medical conditions generally include any illness, disease, injury, or medical condition that you had before the start of your trip; it means any medical condition for which

- You received medical treatment, diagnosis, consultation or prescribed drugs or which symptoms or manifestations have existed whether treatment was actually received within a twelve (12) months period before your travel start date;
- Medical advice or treatment was recommended by a medical practitioner within a twelve (12) months period before your travel start date; or
- You should reasonably be aware of within a twelve (12) months period before your travel start date.

Does Travel Takaful cover all of my pre-existing medical conditions?

Claims arising from pre-existing medical conditions will not be covered for

- any pre-existing medical condition that worsens within the thirty (30) days before the start of your trip for which doctor has advised to see a medical specialist, undergo investigative test, surgery, or change in treatment, prescribed medication or dosage. This does not include changes to prescribed medication or dosage for lowering blood cholesterol;
- you are diagnosed with terminal illness with life expectancy of less than 12 months; HIV related illness, sexually transmitted diseases and mental disorders regardless whether diagnosis was received before or after this insurance was purchased;
- Outpatient medical treatment;
- Medical expenses incurred in Singapore. Please refer to our Policy Wording on full terms, conditions and exclusions.

Depending on how you and rest of the insured persons submit the claim.

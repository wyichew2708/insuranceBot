# How Do I Buy The Right Critical Illness Insurance?

## How Do I Buy The Right Critical Illness Insurance?

- Date: July 1, 2025

You’ve decided that now’s the time to get critical illness insurance, but you’re confused and unsure about how you can compare critical illness coverage now. Here is a critical illness insurance guide to help you understand how you can make a decision on choosing a critical illness plan that meets your needs.

- Benefits of purchasing your critical illness insurance online

## What are important factors to compare in critical illness insurance?

Coverage and Conditions
Start by reviewing the list of critical illnesses covered by the plan. Overwhelmed by the number of conditions covered? Look out for illnesses that you’re most concerned about. You can consider if there are any conditions like stroke, cancer or heart attack that are prevalent in your family’s medical history.

Benefits and Payouts
Many online insurers now offer you the flexibility to decide on how much payout you’d like to receive in the event you’re diagnosed with a covered critical illness. Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness, for example, allows you to get an online quote quickly for coverage between $30,000 and $300,000.

While you may already have great hospital coverage, you could consider how this sum of money will be able to cover other costs. Your financial needs may be different depending on whether you’re a sole breadwinner of your family with kids or if you’re one half of a DINK couple (dual income no kids). Would this additional payout be able to cover your daily living expenses and caregiving expenses for your family?

Read about the hidden costs of critical illness and personal stories on what surviving and recovering from a critical illness can mean for your family.

Review the policy terms, including the coverage period and renewal options. Does the policy have a limited coverage period or renewable terms? It’s better to be aware about the limitations of each critical illness insurance policy.

Does the premium fit your budget while offering you the critical illness coverage you need? Compare the premium cost across different insurance providers. Ensure that the premium amount fits within your budget and remains manageable over time. You can refer to premium tables that show you the cost of your policy over your desired policy term. For Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness, you can download the premium table after getting your quote.

When getting a quote and applying for a critical illness plan from an insurer, you may need to get a medical check-up done, disclose your medical history and conditions or make a declaration about your health. It’s best to be honest! When you make a claim, the insurer may investigate your medical records and they can deny your claim or even cancel your policy if they discover any discrepancies.

When you get a quote for Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness, you don’t have to worry about going for a medical check-up if you pass the medical questionnaire. You’ll only have to pass these three medical questions and you’ll qualify to purchase Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness without any hassle. The 3 qualifying questions are:

- Have you ever had, or been told that you have, or are you currently undergoing medical testing or investigation for diabetes, high blood pressure, cardiovascular disease, cerebral vascular disease, disorders of the nervous system, urogenital disorder, rheumatoid arthritis, AIDS or infection with HIV, cancer, carcinoma in situ, pre-malignant changes, tumour, lump or polyp? (For tumour, lump or polyp which have been removed without recurrence and confirmed as benign, please select ‘No’)

- In the past 2 years, have you had abnormal results or results outside the normal range in a pap smear, mammogram, breast ultrasound, prostate examination or tumour marker blood test?

- In the past 3 months, have you experienced unexplained weight loss of 5kg or more, blood in urine, persistent coughing, bleeding from the bowels or in stools, diarrhea or constipation for 30 days or more?

What are the limitations and exclusions of your shortlisted critical illness insurance policy? Some policies might not cover certain pre-existing conditions or high risk activities. If you’re currently on medication for specific illnesses, you may want to check on whether you they are considered a pre-existing condition that is excluded from being covered.

Some policies offer optional riders, such as early critical illness coverage or disability coverage. This will increase the premium of your critical illness insurance. You may want to consider if these riders and the additional coverage is in line with your needs. Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness offers a Heart and Neurological Disorder Rider 1 that you can opt for, which covers additional early and intermediate stage heart and neurological conditions, to expand your critical illness coverage beyond stroke, heart attack and all stages of cancer.

Critical illness insurance policies may have a waiting period after purchase before you can claim for specific covered conditions. Make sure you’ve checked on the waiting period for the critical illnesses you are concerned about.

## Is there any benefit to purchasing my critical illness coverage online?

You may be wondering what are some benefits of purchasing your critical illness coverage online and if you’d be better off doing it online yourself.

No Medical Checks Needed
You may need to get a medical check done before you can get a quote from an agent for critical illness. Your family history may also be considered.

Purchasing online may allow you to research, compare, and buy policies all from the comfort of your own home, without the need to schedule meetings and follow-up meetings with an agent.

Buying a critical illness insurance online may eliminate agent commissions and this might result in a lower premium. You should still do your research and request for quotes to compare if this is true for your case.

When you purchase your critical illness insurance online, you may find that you’re able to do so faster than through an agent since you won’t need to wait for an agent to process your paperwork or schedule appointments.

Worried about hard-selling or bias that an agent might exert to sell you a specific policy? Purchasing your policy online may mean avoiding that.

## Don’t purchase your critical illness insurance impulsively

While there are many benefits to purchasing your critical illness insurance online, remember that purchasing any insurance is a long-term commitment. You shouldn’t rush into buying your critical illness plan and may want to take the time to research, ask more questions, understand the promotions available, and choose a plan that provides the right balance of coverage and affordability for your situation and needs.

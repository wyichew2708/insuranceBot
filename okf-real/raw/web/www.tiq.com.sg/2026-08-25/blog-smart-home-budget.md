# How to Create a Smart Home on a Budget

## How to Create a Smart Home on a Budget

- Date: June 2, 2025

As Singapore embraces digital transformation, our #SmartNation vision is becoming a reality with smart towns and homes. The good news? Building a smart home doesn’t have to break the bank. With affordable tech solutions like CCTV cameras and Google Home, you can enhance security, convenience, and efficiency without overspending. Read on to discover how you can create a smarter, safer home for just a few hundred dollars!

## Digital Door Lock

No more fiddling around with keys and you don’t have to worry about forgetting where you placed them! Possibly one of the best investments for your home, basic digital locks cost a couple of hundred dollars and come with various modes of access such as passcodes, fingerprints or RFID cards.

A digital door lock will also give your home an added layer of security as most of them come with fire alarms and anti-theft alarms that will sound if someone tries to tamper with it.

## CCTV Cameras

Home security is essential, and a smart CCTV camera is one of the best ways to protect your home. Budget-friendly options now offer features like motion detection, night vision, and cloud storage at competitive prices. A few smart variations include:

- Wi-Fi CCTV Cameras – Allow remote access via a mobile app

- Motion-Activated CCTV – Records only when movement is detected to save storage

- Cloud-Based CCTV Systems – Store footage securely online for easy retrieval

## Smart Speaker

A great introduction to smart home, smart speakers do more than just put on your Spotify playlist or tell you the weather forecast. They can also control other smart home devices such as your lights or coffee machine so that one trigger word from you shall set your usual routine into motion. How convenient!

The more popular smart speakers in the market are Amazon Echo, Apple HomePod and Google Nest (previously called Google Home). You can buy a smart speaker for less than S$100!

## IoT Devices

The Internet of Things (IoT) is making HDB homes smarter by automating everyday tasks effortlessly. These gadgets not only make life more convenient but also help you save energy. Here are some budget-friendly IoT upgrades to consider:

- Smart Plugs – Convert standard appliances into smart devices

- Smart Light Bulbs – Control brightness and colour via mobile apps

- Smart Sensors – Detect motion, temperature changes, or leaks to prevent damage

## Smart Kitchen Appliances

While a fancy fridge with a screen and built-in cameras or a smart full surface induction hob with flexible cooking zones can transform your kitchen into an atas -looking showroom, there are other more affordable smart home appliances that you can adapt to make your life easier and even help to elevate you to domestic goddess status.

Smart kitchen appliances such as a smart microwave can reduce cooking time by half and they are also energy efficient. Some smart microwaves even feature an anti-bacterial interior, which makes cleaning it so easy!

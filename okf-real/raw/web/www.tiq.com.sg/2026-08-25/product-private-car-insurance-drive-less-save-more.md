# Drive Less Save More

## Drive Less? Save More.

Enjoy up to 30% annual cashback based on your mileage. The less you drive, the more you save—while helping to reduce carbon emissions.

Some scenarios where the Drive Less Save More (DLSM) add-on will benefit you:

- Leaving the car parked in a carpark while you take your family on a well-deserved break overseas.
- Own a car, but you work in the Central Business District. You prefer commuting via public transport to avoid the hassles of ERP/parking costs.
- Underutilised car that is used only for short errands like picking up your children or grocery runs.
- Just not driving much due to the rising petrol costs. 😛 The DLSM  add-on is complimentary and automatically included when you sign up for your Private Car Insurance as a new customer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

I've just signed up for Private Car Insurance with the DLSM add-on. What should I do now?

To get started, you’ll need to activate your DLSM add-on via the Etiqa+SG mobile app. Activation will be available from three (3) days before your policy start date, and a TiqConnect account is required for activation.

For a step-by-step guide on activating your DLSM add-on and setting up a TiqConnect account, please refer to the full FAQ here .

How do I claim my cash rebates?

After activating your DLSM add-on via the Etiqa+ SG mobile app, you’ll need to upload a photo showing your car’s odometer reading and vehicle number plate.

Thereafter, you’ll need to submit updated odometer photos periodically through the app, with at least 10 days between each submission. These submissions are required for us to calculate and process any cash rebate you may be eligible for during the coverage period.

Do I still enjoy the No Claim Discount (NCD) with the Drive Less Save More (DLSM) add- on?

Yes, you’re still entitled to the NCD for your Car Insurance. The DLSM cash rebate is calculated as a percentage of your premium, nett of the NCD (if any).

I've cancelled my policy. Am I still entitled for the cash rebates under Drive Less Save More (DLSM)?

No, you’ll only be entitled for the cash rebate for the days preceding the termination of your policy.

I am facing issues with my image uploads or cashback. What should I do?

Kindly contact our Customer Care team for assistance.

WhatsApp: +65 6887 8777 Operating hours: Mon–Fri, 8.45am–5.30pm (Closed on weekends and Public Holidays)

Please note: You’ll not be eligible for a cash rebate if:

- Your policy is cancelled before the end of the policy period (any eligible rebate earned prior to cancellation will still be paid)
- There is unauthorised tampering, modification, replacement, or malfunction of your odometer, or issues with uploaded images
- Your mileage does not qualify under the Rebate Table for high-mileage drivers

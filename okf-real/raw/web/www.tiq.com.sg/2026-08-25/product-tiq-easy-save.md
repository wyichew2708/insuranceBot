*Free Partial Withdrawal Benefit During the first 6 years from Policy issue, you may perform partial withdrawal(s) without any charge under the following circumstances: If You or Your spouse is certified by a Doctor to: be physically or mentally incapacitated from ever continuing in any employment; have a severely impaired life expectancy; lack capacity within the meaning of Section 4 of the Mental Capacity Act (MCA) and the lack of capacity is likely to be permanent; or be diagnosed with Terminal illness. You can exercise this benefit subject to the following conditions: Any of the above-specified events has to occur after the Policy issue date or latest Reinstatement date; The maximum partial withdrawal amount is the lower of S$50,000 or 50% of the total Regular premiums paid (not including Premiums paid in advance); and this benefit can only be exercised once throughout the policy term.

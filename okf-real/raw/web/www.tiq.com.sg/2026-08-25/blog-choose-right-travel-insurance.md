# How to Choose a Suitable Travel Insurance

## How to Choose a Suitable Travel Insurance

- Date: June 17, 2025

Digital disruptions and changing travel trends have made it easier for you and me to make our own travel plans, but it’s likely that we are missing some essential considerations! If you have been taking chances and skipping travel protection to save a few bucks, this article will share with you the importance of getting the right travel insurance and guide you to get the one that suits you!

## First off, here’s what you need to know about travel insurance:

Travel insurance helps protect against risks like trip cancellations, medical emergencies, or lost belongings. It also provides global 24-hour assistance, which is essential if you’re stranded in a foreign country.

This guide to travel insurance covers the common types of travel insurance available in Singapore, so you can choose the right plan based on your travel habits and needs.

## Common types of travel insurance

Single Trip Policy  – A protection plan meant for traveller(s) on an overseas trip that does not exceed over 182 consecutive days.

Annual Multi-Trip Policy  – A protection plan meant for a traveller(s) on an unlimited number of overseas trips across a 1-year period, whereby each trip does not exceed 90 consecutive days.

Pre-existing Medical Cover  – An enhanced plan that covers pre-existing medical conditions for travellers during their overseas trip, which is not covered under common travel insurance plans.

Family Plan  – A protection plan meant for family members that are travelling together for the entire overseas trip. The definition for ‘family’ differs across different insurers, so you should check prior to your purchase. Is it cheaper to get a family plan? Yes, such plans are generally great for savings and booking convenience.

## Choosing the right travel insurance for you

## 1. Identify your travel profile

Are you a frequent traveller or planning high-risk activities like skydiving or bungee jumping overseas? Your destination and travel style determine the coverage you need. This helps narrow down the right travel insurance plan.

For example, solo female travellers may prioritise affordability and protection for safety and belongings. Skiers should look for coverage that includes sports equipment. Frequent travellers can save with annual travel insurance plans. Families and groups enjoy discounts and convenience when buying travel insurance together.

2. Know your priorities

Ask yourself: “Why am I buying travel insurance?” Identify what you want to protect—personal wellbeing or financial loss.

Different travellers have different needs. Leisure travellers may choose basic plans for peace of mind. Families with young children may require added protection like child education cover and comprehensive medical benefits.

3. Do your homework

As a free and independent traveller, it’s your responsibility to ensure you have the right travel insurance coverage. Know your options before choosing a plan. The cheapest travel insurance may not offer the best protection for your needs. Seek advice from travel agents or experienced travellers. Their insights can complement your research on aggregator sites, forums, or insurer websites. This helps you make an informed choice based on real experiences and reliable sources.

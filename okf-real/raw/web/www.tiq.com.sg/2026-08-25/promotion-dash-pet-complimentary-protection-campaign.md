# Dash PET Complimentary Protection Campaign

## Dash PET Complimentary Protection Campaign

For selected Dash PET users, get complimentary Death & Total and Permanent Disability^ add-on protection for the first year of your Dash PET policy. You may login to your  Singtel Dash app to check your eligibility.

Boost your returns: Earn 1.5%* p.a. on your first S$10,000 Dash PET savings when you opt in and activate your free protection.

*Additional 0.2% p.a. to the existing 1.3% p.a. returns for your first S$10,000 Dash PET savings during the first policy year while the complimentary protection is active. ^Complimentary Accidental Death coverage will be offered for the same sum assured and duration if you are not eligible for this add-on protection. Sum assured will be based on your age and occupation.

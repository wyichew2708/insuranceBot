## Frequently Asked Questions

What is Travel Pass?

Travel Pass is where travel meets perks, the first-of-its-kind travel insurance rewards experience that turns every trip into exclusive perks and member benefits.

How do I join Travel Pass / become a member?

You’re automatically enrolled into Travel Pass when you purchase your first Tiq Travel insurance policy and meet the minimum premium paid of S$100. After your policy is issued, you’ll be able to access your membership details, track your tier status and start earning toward higher tiers.

How do I progress to the next tier?

- Bronze Tier Qualification To qualify as a Bronze Member, you must accumulate a minimum premium spend of S$100 on Tiq Travel Insurance within a twelve (12) month period.
- Silver Tier Qualification To qualify as a Silver Member, you must accumulate a minimum premium spend of S$200 on Tiq Travel Insurance within a twelve (12) month period.
- Gold Tier Qualification To qualify as a Gold Member, you must accumulate a minimum premium spend of S$500 on Tiq Travel Insurance within a twelve (12) month period.
- Platinum Tier Qualification To qualify as a Platinum Member, you must accumulate a minimum premium spend of S$1,000 on Tiq Travel Insurance within a twelve (12) month period.

How do I maintain or renew my tier?

Your tier is valid for 12 months from the day you qualify for it. If you move up to a new tier during that time, a new tier validity period will begin, which means that your new tier will be valid for the next 12 months from the day you qualify for it. When your tier validity period ends, we’ll review your total accumulated premium spend from the past 12 months to determine your tier for the next cycle.

Can I progress directly to Platinum Tier if I am currently a Bronze Tier member?

Yes. If your total premium spend meets the Platinum requirement of at least S$1,000 (as stated in the T&Cs), you can progress directly from Bronze to Platinum.

When does tier status take effect?

A tier upgrade happens immediately when the accumulated premium reaches the next tier qualification. Your new tier validity period will be valid for 12 months from the upgrade date. In the event if you don’t meet the required accumulated spend for the tier, your membership tier will remain unchanged until that tier’s expiry date.

When does my tier get downgraded?

Your tier is reviewed at the end of your 12-month tier validity period. If your total accumulated premium over the last 12 months does not meet the required spend for your current tier, your tier will be adjusted accordingly based on the spend you have accumulated. For a clearer view of how tier changes work, you may refer to the illustration below.

What happens when my 12-month tier period ends?

Your tier will be reviewed at the end of each 12-month period from the start date of your tier. If your total accumulated premium in the immediately preceding 12 months is less than S$100, your Travel Pass membership will expire, and you’ll no longer have access to tier perks and benefits.

I’ve already purchased a Tiq Travel policy but my membership tier accumulated spend progress is not updated yet. Why?

Your accumulated premium will be updated in your Travel Pass provided that the relevant policy has not been cancelled or the premium paid refunded to you. The policy’s premium is credited only to the policyholder who owns the Travel Pass, even if the Tiq Travel Insurance policy is purchased on behalf of another person.

Premium amounts will still count toward accumulated premium for Tier qualification even if a claim has been made on the policy:

- Single Trip Policies: accumulated premium will be updated three (3) days after the policy end date.
- Annual Multi-Trip Policies: accumulated premium will be updated thirty (30) days after the policy start date.

What if I cancel my policy or trip?

If you cancel a policy/trip, the spend for the policy will not be included in the accumulated premium for Travel Pass membership in accordance with the Travel Pass terms and conditions. Any perks not used will still be valid for the duration of your current tier’s validity period.

How do I check my tier status, points, and perks?

Log into your Etiqa+ SG app and visit the Travel Pass section. You’ll be able to see your current tier, your points balance, how much more accumulated is needed to upgrade/maintain your tier as well as a list of your unlocked perks.

What perks do I receive at each tier?

Are the perks instant or do I need to activate them?

You are able to get access to the perks automatically once you reach the respective tier. Any required activation steps will be clearly shown in your Travel Pass on the Etiqa+ SG app.

When can I use my perks?

Your Travel Pass perks are valid for up to twelve (12) months, for as long as you remain in your current tier. If your tier expires or is upgraded, access to the perks from your previous tier will end and will no longer be available for use.

What are Etiqa Rewards Points and how do I earn them?

You earn Etiqa Rewards Points whenever you purchase a Tiq Travel insurance policy. For full details on earning, redemption, and applicable terms and conditions, please refer to https://www.tiq.com.sg/rewards/ .

Can I use my tier perks and cancel my policy after?

No. Tier perks are only available to active members who maintain an eligible policy and meet the required Accumulated premium. If you cancel your policy, the cancelled policy’s premium will not be added to your total Accumulated premium. In the event if you qualify for a lower tier, any unused perks will be forfeited and the value of any used perks will be clawed back from your refund amount.

Can Travel Pass membership be revoked?

Yes. Travel Pass membership is subject to the Terms and Conditions. In the event of any breach, including fraud or misuse, Etiqa reserves the right to revoke membership or adjust a Member’s tier status. For full details, please refer to the Travel Pass Terms and Conditions.

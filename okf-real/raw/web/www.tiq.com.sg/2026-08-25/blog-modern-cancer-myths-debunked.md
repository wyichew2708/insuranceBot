# 7 Modern Cancer Myths

## 7 Modern Cancer Myths – Debunked!

- Date: April 6, 2021

Cancer is such a common yet misunderstood disease. The question of curing cancer has been on the minds of doctors and scientists alike for decades. With it comes many cancer misconceptions. Here, we debunked 7 modern cancer myths for you.

## Myth #1: Cancer is a man-made disease designed to profit the pharmaceutical industry

Debunked!  In recorded history, cancer was first described in the Edwin Smith Papyrus (3,000 BC) from Egypt. The document described the diagnosis of cancer as well as treatment method by  cauterisation;  a medical technique of burning an injured part of the body in order to close off wounds or destroy undesired growth.

Cancer was also described as a growth that is malignant, venous and crab-like by the Greek physician Hippocrates (460-370 BC). Unlike what cancer myths suggested, archaeologists even found cancerous growth in the bones of a 240 million-year-old dinosaur fossil that they dug up in Germany. Talk about ancient!

While it seems like cancer is on the rise all around the world today, the biggest risk factor for cancer is actually age. Our lifestyle, diet and other factors such as air pollution have a huge impact on our risk of cancer but that does not mean that it is entirely a modern, man-made disease.

We now simply live long enough to develop cancer, since most of us in developed countries can combat infectious diseases and other causes of death such as malnutrition. As we age, the DNA damage in our cells build, and such damage can lead to the development of cancer.

## Myth #2: Acidic food like meat, dairy and grains cause cancer

Debunked!  Blood is naturally slightly alkaline (pH 7.35-7.45). This acidity is maintained by the kidneys and nothing you eat can change the pH level of the blood. In fact, a change in blood pH level is a medical emergency called  acidosis  which causes confusion, drowsiness and shortness of breath.

#TiqOurWord  Fruits and vegetables like lemons, grapes, mushrooms, blueberries and pomegranates are acidic, too. But they contain high antioxidants to fend off cancer and are generally good for health.

## Myth #3: Superfoods prevent cancer

Debunked! There is no such thing as ‘superfood’. It is a marketing term used to sell products and have no scientific basis . No need to waste a lot of money on expensive ‘superfoods’ with funny and difficult-to-pronounce names such as acai berries, chia seeds, quinoa, kohlrabi, arugula and so on.

A healthy lifestyle with a diet high in fruits and vegetables, regular exercise, adequate sleep, will help to strengthen your immunity system and is the best way to reduce your risk of cancer! Of course, alcohol consumption should be moderate and no smoking.

## Myth #4: Microwaving food in plastic containers releases harmful cancer-causing substances

Debunked! Plastic containers and wraps labelled as safe for use in the microwave don’t pose a threat to your health. There are reports showing that microwaved plastic releases cancer-causing chemicals called dioxins into food.

But wait, plastics on its own doesn’t contain dioxins. Dioxins are only created when rubbish, plastics, metals, wood and other materials are burned. You won’t be exposing yourself to these harmful dioxins as long as you don’t burn your food in the microwave!

#TiqOurWord Only microwave plastic containers that are labelled microwave-safe or if you are in doubt, try to avoid it by transferring your food into a glass or ceramic bowl (those which are safe to use in the microwave, of course!) before heating it in the microwave oven.

## Myth #5: Chemotherapy is painful and detrimental to the body

Debunked!  First of all, we need to be honest with you: No medical treatment, surgery or medication are easy or comfortable. But over the past decade, the survival rate of cancer patients who seek treatment in Singapore has gone up from 13% to 48% for men, and from 28% to 57% for women.

## Myth #6: Smartphones give off radiation that can cause cancer

Debunked!  Radiation does cause damages to our DNA, which might lead to cancerous growth in the body. However, our electronic gadgets give off around 450 to 3,800 megahertz of radiation.

This is too minute to cause cancer. But prolonged exposure from X-rays (30 petahertz to 30 exahertz) and gamma rays (more than 10 19  hertz), such as those from nuclear power plants, CT scans, and radon from farmhouses, can indeed cause cancer due to their high amount of radiation but not as the cancer myths suggested.

## Myth #7: There is no cure for cancer

TRUE!  There is no specific cure for cancer. But cancer can be treated. We repeat – cancer can be treated! Cancer is an abnormal growth of the cells due to the malfunctioning of DNA. That does not equate a death sentence.

In theory, if we remove those malfunctioning cells, the body will revert back to normal. This can be done through surgery, chemotherapy, hormone therapy and many other methods. That is why early stage detection can save almost 100% of all patients.

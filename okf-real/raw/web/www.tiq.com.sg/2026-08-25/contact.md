Your Opinion Matters to Us

What you think really matters to us. If you have a feedback or complaint, please get in touch with us via email at customer.service@etiqa.com.sg or call us at +65 6887 8777 from 8.45am to 5.30pm, Monday to Friday (excluding Public Holiday).

Upon receiving your feedback or complaint

- Our Customer Care Officer will acknowledge within 2 business days.
- Your feedback or complaint will be addressed as soon as possible within Etiqa and where necessary, with the relevant business partners.
- If you have made a complaint to us, we will assign a complaint officer to handle your complaint and we will contact you within 7 business days from the date of your complaint if we require additional information.
- We will update you within 15 business days of our last communication to you if your complaint takes longer to resolve.
- We are committed to resolve your complaint within 30 business days after the date of receipt of your complaint.
- If the outcome of your complaint is not handled to your satisfaction, you can write to our Chief Executive at CXFeedback.MT@etiqa.com.sg . We will respond to your appeal within 14 business days.

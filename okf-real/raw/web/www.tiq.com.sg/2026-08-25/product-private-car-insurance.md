## Frequently Asked Questions

Can I claim for damages caused by flooding or bad weather?

Yes, our Comprehensive Private Car Insurance plan covers damage caused by falling tree(s) or inflicted by weather conditions such as floods. Please refer to Policy Wording for more details.

How does Etiqa 30 minutes claims approval work?

Our fast claims approval is only applicable to own-damage motor claims under the amount of $5,000 at Etiqa’s authorised workshops. Upon receiving the necessary claims and estimated damage documents, repair claims of up to $5,000 can be approved in just 30 minutes. Exclusions apply for claims which are subjected to further investigation or a breach of policy conditions.

What is covered under 24/7 roadside assistance benefit?

You will be covered for towing in West Malaysia, up to a maximum limit of S$500 per policy year within the Geographical Limit, provided you have opted for Etiqa Package add-on with a comprehensive plan.

You may still engage this towing service even without Etiqa Package add-on. Towing service will be subject to cost borne by you.

For assistance, simply contact the following hotline:

- Hotline Number for Singapore: +65 6311 4128
- Hotline Number for Malaysia: +603 22968600

How do I make a claim?

If you have TiqConnect (online self-service) account, please log into  TiqConnect  to submit in your claims. Otherwise, you may  register  for an account (applicable only to policy owners). Access can be created immediately. You may contact Etiqa Customer Care via WhatsApp messaging or phone call at +65 6887 8777 or email us at  customer.service@etiqa.com.sg  for assistance.

Does Etiqa Private Car Insurance covers Electric Vehicles (EVs)?

Yes, our Private Car Insurance comprehensive plan offers coverage for your electric car battery and other electric components if they are accidentally damaged, or damaged by fire, flood or theft.

You may visit your preferred EV specialist workshops for repairs by opting for the ‘My Workshop’ add-on.

You may also enjoy towing assistance to the nearest charging station or workshop in the event of a flat battery or battery failure with the ‘Etiqa Package’ add-on.

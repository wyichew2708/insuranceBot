## Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to purchase?

You can buy Tiq Personal Accident if you are a Singaporean citizen, Permanent Resident of Singapore or foreigner with a valid Work Pass, Student Pass, Dependant’s Pass or Long Term Visit Pass, aged between 17 to 70 years old.

The policy shall be renewed up to the maximum age of seventy-five (75) years subject to our satisfaction on Your state of health.

What is covered under Tiq Personal Accident?

Tiq Personal Accident provides a wide range of coverage such as:

- Unexpected falls or slips
- Food poisoning
- Dengue Fever
- Hand, Foot and Mouth Disease (HFMD)
- Physiotherapy expenses
- Hospital Cash benefit due to COVID-19
- Home Recovery Cash benefit upon COVID-19 diagnosis Please refer to our Policy Wording for the comprehensive coverage list.

How do I file for a claim?

If you have a TiqConnect account with Etiqa, please log onto TiqConnect to submit your claims online. Otherwise, you may register for an account on our corporate website . Access can be created immediately.

You may also contact our Customer Care hotline at +65 6887 8777 or email them at customer.service@etiqa.com.sg for further assistance.

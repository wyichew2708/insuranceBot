## Examples Of Fire-Safe Habits At Home

Need inspiration? Try these:

Turning off appliances completely before bed

Using one power plug per socket (no daisy chains!)

Keeping stovetops grease-free and clutter-free

## Why It Matters

Fire hazards can appear when you least expect them:

Overloaded sockets / electrical faults

Unattended cooking / kitchen fires (the top cause of home fires in Singapore)

Overheating of batteries in modified Personal Mobility Devices (PMDs)

A simple fire extinguisher can stop a spark from becoming a disaster, protecting the home you’ve built.

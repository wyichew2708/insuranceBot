# Etiqa clinches new awards for customer centricity and choice home contents insurer

## Etiqa clinches new awards for customer centricity and choice home contents insurer

- Date: March 25, 2020

Singapore, 26 Feb 2020 – Etiqa Insurance is honoured to be recognised as the “Best Customer-Centric Insurance Brand” by Global Brands Magazine for the year 2019. Running for the 7 th  year by the leading brand magazine, the awards was established to honour excellence in performance across industries. Etiqa emerged winner from a competitive group of entrants, all of whom demonstrated unique and exceptional service delivery towards evolving insurance sectors, and was awarded this honour for its exceptional commitment to Innovation, Quality, Branding Activities, Customer Service & Performance and providing a Robust Insurance System in Singapore.

To bolster the local insurer’s glowing repertoire, Etiqa has emerged as the Gold winner in the highly competitive category of Home and Contents Insurance as judged by the Singaporean public in the inaugural Reader’s Digest Quality Service Awards 2020. Conducted with the objective to gain insights into companies that provide the highest quality of service, Etiqa was voted for its high levels of quality service recognised by Singaporeans nationwide, and efforts to satisfy consumer demand for high service standards as a leading digital insurer in the market.

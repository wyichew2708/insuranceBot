## Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for this plan?

As long as you are aged between 17 and 70, a resident of Singapore with an NRIC or FIN, or a foreigner holding a valid work pass or long-term visit pass, and have passed 3 simple health questions, you’re eligible to purchase this plan. Unlike traditional critical illness insurance plans, you don’t need to go for any medical checks. Here are the 3 health questions:

1. Have you ever had, or been told that you have, or are you currently undergoing medical testing or investigation for diabetes, high blood pressure, cardiovascular disease, cerebral vascular disease, disorders of the nervous system, urogenital disorder, rheumatoid arthritis, AIDS or infection with HIV, cancer, carcinoma in situ, pre-malignant changes, tumour, lump or polyp? (For tumour, lump or polyp which have been removed without recurrence and confirmed as benign, please select ‘No’)

2. In the past 2 years, have you had abnormal results or results outside the normal range in a pap smear, mammogram, breast ultrasound, prostate examination or tumour marker blood test?

3. In the past 3 months, have you experienced unexplained weight loss of 5kg or more, blood in urine, persistent coughing, bleeding from the bowels or in stools, diarrhea or constipation for 30 days or more?

If you have a pre-existing condition mentioned in the above 3 health questions, you will not be able to purchase Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness.

Is covering just cancer, stroke and heart attack enough?

There are many ways you might benefit from getting a critical illness plan like Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness that covers only cancer, stroke and heart attack. Here are a few reasons:

- You want to focus on major health threats and most commonly claimed conditions.
- You have a family history of specific conditions like stroke, cancer and heart attack.
- You already have existing coverage, but want more financial support for most claimed critical illnesses.
- Your financial commitments have increased and you’d like to safeguard your family’s financial security in event of a critical illness with additional coverage.

Still unsure? Learn more here .

Can I buy Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness if I already have existing critical illness insurance coverage with another insurer?

Yes, you can purchase Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness to increase your coverage for stroke, cancer and heart attack even if you have existing coverage with another insurer.

Learn more about using Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness to enhance your existing critical illness insurance here .

Can I purchase both Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness and Cancer Insurance?

You can purchase both Tiq 3 Plus Critical Illness and Cancer Insurance. Claims can be made on both policies if the condition is a covered condition in both insurance plans.

Should you purchase multiple policies and riders with CI Benefits from Etiqa Insurance, do note that there is a maximum claim amount of $2,000,000 per Life insured for early, intermediate or severe stages of critical illness apply. This is also subject to a cap of S$350,000 per Life.

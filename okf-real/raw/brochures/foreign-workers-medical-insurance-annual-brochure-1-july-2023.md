Pre-contract disclosure for medical insurance plans for Work Permit and S Pass Holders

This product provides coverage for the following features that comply with the Ministry of Manpower’s (MOM)
enhanced Medical Insurance requirement1:

Annual claim limit of at least $60,000, inclusive of a first-dollar cover of $15,000

For portion of the bill above $15,000, the employer must co-pay up to 25% (to the hospital)

Exclusions are in line with MOM’s list of allowable exclusion2

Age-differentiated premiums are in 2 age bands: (1) <50 years old and (2) >50 years old

Insurers will reimburse our portion of the hospital bill to hospitals directly upon admissibility
of the medical claim

1 Refer to Annex of the press release for the list of allowable exclusions.

2 Scan QR code for MOM’s press release on the enhanced medical insurance.

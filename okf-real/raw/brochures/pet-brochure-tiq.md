Protection for what is in your pet’s DNA with coverage for Congenital and Hereditary conditions.
Some health conditions are part of your pet’s genetics and can show up when you least expect it. That’s why
our Pet Insurance include coverage for congenital and hereditary conditions, so you can focus on getting your
pet the care they need, instead of thinking about the cost. The plans covers the following conditions:
• Hip and Elbow Dysplasia
• Luxating patella
• Glaucoma
• Cherry Eye
• Intervertebral disc disease (IVDD)
• Conditions which require femoral head and neck excision

-  Kickstart your journey with a start-up bonus of up to 60%² in your first year of investment.

-  Top up your premiums  anytime  to grow  your investment further.

## How it works:

Adam, age 42, non-smoker is looking for an investment-linked plan that will grow his wealth and leave a legacy to his family. He signs up for Invest smart flex II with a payment term of 20 years at S$6,000 in annual premium. Adam decides to invest his premiums in dividend-paying funds, and reinvest his dividends 6 . He is protected with a basic policy sum assured equal to the higher of 101% of total premiums 5  paid or regular premium account value.

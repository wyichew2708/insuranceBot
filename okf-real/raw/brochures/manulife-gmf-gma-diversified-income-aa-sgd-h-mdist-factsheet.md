Investment Objective
Global Multi-Asset Diversified Income Fund aims to achieve income generation by investing primarily in a diversified portfolio of equity,
equity-related, fixed income and fixed income-related securities of companies and/or governments (which include agencies and supra-
nationals in respect of fixed income and fixed income-related securities) globally (including emerging markets from time to time).

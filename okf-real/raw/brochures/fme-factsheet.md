## Global Equity (AGE)

USD Fund Factsheet | As of 30 June 2026

## Investment objective

The  Fund  aims  to  maximize  compounded  returns  by  allocating capital  to  equity  opportunities  globally.  AGE  combines  tactical allocation across regions and sectors with thematic exposures to structural trend and select market-neutral strategies to generate alpha, while actively reducing concentration risk.

## Investment approach

Tactical: Allocate to areas that are more attractive while scaling back  where  risk  are  rising  to  manage  volatility  and  improve returns

Diversified:  Participates  in  global  growth  across  developed  and emerging markets, reducing concentration risk in any single area

Thematic:  Invest  in  structural  trends  that  cuts  across  countries, sectors and business -like AI, Electrification, Cybersecurity

Allocation range: Equity 100 | Bond 0 | Alternative 0

## Manager comments

- Market Review: Markets moved from shock to recovery in the second quarter. The US -Iran conflict initially disrupted oil flows and revived  inflation  concerns,  putting  pressure  on  both  equities  and  bonds.  As  tensions  eased  and  oil  prices  retreated,  confidence returned. Resilient corporate earnings and continued investment in Artificial Intelligence (AI) gave the recovery a firmer foundation.
- Outlook: The overall macro backdrop remains resilient as the tailwind from the AI investment has offset the energy supply shock from the closure  of  SoH.  That  said,  sticky  inflation  and  geopolitical  tensions  remain  important  sources  of  uncertainty.  After  the strong Q2 rebound, it would not be unusual for markets to undergo a period of consolidation, as investors reassess earnings, policy expectations  and  global  growth.  Diversification,  selectivity  and  disciplined  risk  management  will  remain  important  as  markets navigate the next phase of the recovery.
- Portfolio performance and activities: AGE delivered a strong quarterly return of 15.23%, although June was more challenging, with the  portfolio  posting  a  modest  decline  of  -0.82%  amid  heightened  market  volatility.  After  the  strong  rally  in  semiconductor  and memory chip names, positioning in parts of the technology sector appeared increasingly crowded, prompting us to take profit and rotate toward a broader equity recovery theme. This involved trimming exposure to more tech-heavy areas, including U.S. and Emerging Market equities, and reallocating into  European equities.  We  believe  Europe  offers  more  attractive  relative  value and should benefit from easing geopolitical tensions, lower oil prices, and a broader improvement in cyclical sentiment.

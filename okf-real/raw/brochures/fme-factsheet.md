Manager comments
• Market Review: Markets moved from shock to recovery in the second quarter. The US–Iran conflict initially disrupted oil flows and
revived inflation concerns, putting pressure on both equities and bonds. As tensions eased and oil prices retreated, confidence
returned. Resilient corporate earnings and continued investment in Artificial Intelligence (AI) gave the recovery a firmer foundation.
• Portfolio performance and activities: AGE delivered a strong quarterly return of 15.23%, although June was more challenging, with
the portfolio posting a modest decline of -0.82% amid heightened market volatility. After the strong rally in semiconductor and
memory chip names, positioning in parts of the technology sector appeared increasingly crowded, prompting us to take profit and
rotate toward a broader equity recovery theme. This involved trimming exposure to more tech-heavy areas, including U.S. and
Emerging Market equities, and reallocating into European equities. We believe Europe offers more attractive relative value and
should benefit from easing geopolitical tensions, lower oil prices, and a broader improvement in cyclical sentiment.
• Outlook: The overall macro backdrop remains resilient as the tailwind from the AI investment has offset the energy supply shock
from the closure of SoH. That said, sticky inflation and geopolitical tensions remain important sources of uncertainty. After the
strong Q2 rebound, it would not be unusual for markets to undergo a period of consolidation, as investors reassess earnings, policy
expectations and global growth. Diversification, selectivity and disciplined risk management will remain important as markets
navigate the next phase of the recovery.

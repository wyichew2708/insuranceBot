## How it works:

Sarah, age 30, non-smoker is looking for a Shariah-compliant investment-linked plan that will grow her wealth and provide insurance coverage. She signs up for Invest vista with a payment term of 10 Years - Flexi 5 at S$800 in monthly premiums. Sarah decides to invest her premiums in dividendpaying funds, and reinvest her dividends 8 . She is protected with a basic policy sum assured equal to the higher of 101% of total premiums 6  paid or regular premium account value.

## Sarah signs up for Invest vista.

Based on her annual premium of S$9,600 (S$800 monthly premium x 12), she receives a start-up bonus of: 25% x S$9,600 = S$2,400

Age 35 to 39: Sarah receives a special bonus of 3% x S$9,600 = S$288 every year

Sarah pays her final year's premium of S$9,600.

Sarah retires and requests for dividend payouts to fund her retirement.

Illustrated account value before payout option change is S$538,023 9 . Sarah receives annual dividend 8  ranging between S$34,290 to S$35,117 9 between age 63 to age 85 (23 years).

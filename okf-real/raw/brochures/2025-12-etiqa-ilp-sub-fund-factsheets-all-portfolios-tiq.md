## Tiq Invest Conservative Packaged Fund

## Fund Profile

## Investment Objective :

The investment objective of the packaged fund  is  to  maximize total return, consistent with prudent  asset  allocation  at  a conservative  level  of  risk.  The multi-asset packaged fund combines different types of asset classes, such as equities and  fixed  income,  to  create  a more nimble and broadly diversified allocation. The packaged fund invests approximately  20%  of  its  total assets in equities.

# Travel Insurance — Product Summary (version 2026.1)

## delay
Travel delay benefit applies once the departure is delayed beyond the stated
threshold, and pays per completed block thereafter, subject to the benefit cap.
See benefit-tables/travel.csv for threshold_hours, payout_per_block and cap.

## medical
Overseas medical expenses are reimbursed up to the limit for the plan tier held.
Emergency dental treatment arising from an accident is included.

## baggage
Loss of or damage to baggage and personal effects is covered up to the tier
limit, with a per-item sub-limit.

## cancellation
Trip cancellation for a covered reason is reimbursed up to the tier limit.

## exclusions
Pre-existing medical conditions are excluded unless the pre-existing add-on was
purchased at inception. War, civil commotion and unlawful acts are excluded.

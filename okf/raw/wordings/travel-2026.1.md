# Travel Insurance — Policy Wording 2026.1

## s4.2 Travel delay
The Company shall pay the benefit stated in the Schedule where departure of the
scheduled public conveyance is delayed beyond the threshold stated therein.

## s4.7 Baggage
Cover applies to accidental loss of or damage to baggage. A police or carrier
report must be obtained within 24 hours of discovering the loss.

## s6.1 Exclusions
No benefit is payable in respect of pre-existing medical conditions unless the
optional pre-existing condition extension was effected at policy inception.

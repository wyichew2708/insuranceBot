## Pre-existing medical conditions

No benefit is payable for pre-existing medical conditions unless the optional
extension was effected at policy inception [src:raw/wordings/travel-2026.1.md#s6.1].

## General exclusions

War, civil commotion and unlawful acts are excluded [src:raw/product-summaries/travel-2026.1.md#exclusions].

Travel undertaken against a government advisory is excluded [src:raw/product-summaries/travel-2026.1.md#exclusions].

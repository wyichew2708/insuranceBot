## What it means

A pre-existing condition is a medical condition for which signs, symptoms or
treatment existed before the policy started [src:raw/wordings/travel-2026.1.md#s6.1].

## Why it matters

Claims arising from a pre-existing condition are excluded unless the optional
extension was effected at inception [src:raw/wordings/travel-2026.1.md#s6.1].

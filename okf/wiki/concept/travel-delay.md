## What it means

Travel delay is a delay to the departure of the scheduled public conveyance,
measured from the scheduled departure time [src:raw/wordings/travel-2026.1.md#s4.2].

## How the benefit works

The benefit begins only after the threshold stated in the schedule, and accrues
per completed block thereafter up to a cap [src:raw/product-summaries/travel-2026.1.md#delay].

## Before you start

Have the policy number, travel documents and all receipts or carrier reports
ready before starting the claim [src:raw/wordings/travel-2026.1.md#s4.7].

## Steps

Submit the claim through the customer portal or the mobile app, attaching the
supporting documents for the benefit claimed [src:raw/product-summaries/travel-2026.1.md#delay].

For baggage claims a police or carrier report must be obtained promptly after
the loss is discovered [src:raw/wordings/travel-2026.1.md#s4.7].

## After submission

An acknowledgement with a claim reference is issued, and assessment begins once
the documents are complete [src:raw/product-summaries/travel-2026.1.md#delay].
